### Into the Spire 
We enter and see a very creepy room. Weird shapes reaching top to bottom, other details, spiky throne in the room 
Then we see eyes open all over the room 
![[The Wasteland Spire's Throne.png]]
As we approach the throne, we hear a skittering above us on the ceiling. This massive form like a centipede crawls its way down from the top, crawls over the throne, and sits on it 
It looks at us curiously, and speaks into our minds in every single language at the same time, until it settles towards common 
![[Xethra on her Throne.png]]
"You have finally made it. I am grateful to see... "
It shifts forms to be less unsettling 
![[Xethra.png]]
She shrinks down a bit so she's not as gargantuan 
She stands and walks towards us, pausing a few feet from us 
She helps [[Athlas]] up, looks curious 
Same black ichor from before in her veins, but not the same. This ichor almost sparkles and is almost radiant 
She's had a few children 
She calls herself [[Xethra]], a name our minds can comprehend 
She agrees to a mother label 
She senses what was done to [[Noctis]] 
She feels sorry for what we've been through. She senses our pain 
The artifact would resolve a problem she had... it's from one of her children 
[[Athlas]] says Batholith requires it. Must be his master 
This place was created with malicious intent 
It's a home she didn't choose but she has grown comfortable with it
She's been alone for some time 
Over the past few hundred years they have began coming here, they did not stay sane and became these monsters 
The monsters do not dare cross her 
## Storytime with [[Xethra]] 
There was nothing and no time, then there was her 
She created time, then shaped the world and her children 
Her first child was [[Xurxus]], he was so headstrong, so determined to create and do more. Despite his forthcomings, she gave him many creative powers 
Batholith stood for justice and what was right. She adores him dearly. [[unrath]], her third, he had a fighting spirit. He got into many shenanigans, trying so hard to please 
[[Phoerah]] is her eldest daughter. [[Larion]] was a feisty one. her most intelligent daughter, and had the most creative ideas 
Her twins: [[Ethos]] and [[Enos]]. Ethos sent the artifact to her, they got up to so much trickery behind the scenes 
[[Dalaketh]]. He had such rage inside of him. Always directed it towards what needed to be destroyed 
Her 2 youngest: [[Nexalus]] and [[Zeldorh]]. She loves her children dearly, hoping they could build something beautiful 
They grew dissatisfied. They wanted dominion. Some stood against her, others made to obey. They buried her underneath the world they created. They made a cage out of their mother 
There is sadness and sorrow in her heart. She's been here for centuries, waiting for cracks to form. And it has begun 
She is [[Revelar]]'s grandmother in many ways 
Hopefully the artifact could help her break out. There was so much potential. As she says this, the skies get darker. Their minds were so little. There could've been so much, so many beasts and creatures 
But this is the now and this is what we shall work with 
The skies brighten 
She is stuck down here. She has no ways to get home 
If she could traverse to our world, what could she do? 
- She touches [[Athlas]]'s forehead and the skies get dark. "She did *that?!* I need to right some wrongs!"

There are many injustices to be rightened. The burden should not fall on us 
All of us were made from the gods in one way or another 
She doesn't see much of the world above. She can send creatures out, but that's... a bad idea 
She can see through our eyes potentially, if we allow it 
She hasn't seen the stars in so long. 
[[Xurxus]] may have feared it could let her escape 
What is [[Enos]]'s purpose? Her fear was for it to get into the wrong hands 
Our goal going forward is... she desires her freedom. She would like to see through some of our eyes 
She desires to be free. She doesn't understand how to break her chains 
[[Revelar]] and [[Kiara Bashara]] offer their eyes. [[Athlas]] offers his eyes to right their wrong 
She will not recreate our world, she's not destructive like [[Xurxus]], she will fulfill the potential it was supposed to have. She will help set us free and fulfill our potential 
This isn't simply a prison or a cage. She has not spoken to her children in a long time. She is unable to, and they cannot speak with her 
She doesn't fully understand what is happening with [[Noctis]]'s situation. 
She can make a deal with us later if one of us still has eyes 
She acknowledges [[Fern]] 
She has no plan for [[Xurxus]] until she sees what they've done to the world 
[[Fern]] gives [[Xethra]] a flower. Awwww
[[Larion]] is a very creative god 
We will see if we can converse with her after we get home 
[[Athlas]] asks how old he actually is. he doesn't know...
