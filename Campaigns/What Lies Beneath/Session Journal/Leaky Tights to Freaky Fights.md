## Log

### The Leaky Tights

[[Kiara Bashara]] and [[Revelar]] arrived at the Leaky Tights in [[Stal'hgite]], following [[Xurxus]]'s instruction to seek the Keeper of Knowledge. At the tavern they encountered several travelers who would soon join their expedition. [[Nox]], a quiet water genasi with a history at sea, listened to their conversation. A hooded, gray-tailed stranger worked bone into a blade and followed the group toward the library. Revelar scrutinized the tavernkeeper's price for a barrel of ale, finding the fair price to be two gold pieces rather than five.

### The Keeper of Knowledge

The library stood apart from the town, shabby outside but unexpectedly spacious within. Revelar kicked the door open. The group met [[Darian Pageweather]], a gnome known as the Keeper of Knowledge. His private chamber was lavish and saturated with magic, and Darian appeared bruised and walked with a limp. The source of his injuries was not established.

Darian explained that he had possessed and studied the artifact before it vanished. It had reportedly appeared to [[Xurxus]], in a forest, and to a young halfling at different times. Its unexplained movements suggested it might possess desires or a will of its own, although that possibility was not proven. Darian reported that a way to track it had been found; whether he worked alone or with others remains to be clarified with the DM.

The hooded stranger introduced himself as [[Athlas]], a gray-scaled lizardfolk with a druidic appearance. He said his order had tasked him with finding the artifact. Revelar threatened Nox with his crossbow before the group de-escalated the encounter. [[Noctis]], a partially mechanical, warforged-like traveler capable of telepathic speech, also joined the gathering. Darian directed the party to a cave near the orc lands, unusually close to the region's outskirts, and showed its location on a map.

### Into the Goblin Cave

The party traveled through the forests near the [[Iron Hills]]. Nox recognized the region from an earlier voyage, and Athlas had been there before. A warning sign outside the cave declared that no goblins were present. Kiara urged diplomacy and a cautious approach. A goblin pointed the travelers toward another entrance, through which they brought their barrel of ale.

Inside, goblins and a worg initially accepted the purported delivery. The ale proved poisoned: two goblins lost consciousness, and a fight followed. Kiara attempted to resolve the encounter without further bloodshed, but the effort failed. After the immediate danger passed, Revelar killed the unconscious goblins. The act sharply contrasted with Kiara's commitment to her [[Oath of Redemption]].

Exploring farther, the group reached barracks and solved a bookshelf puzzle to uncover a hidden passage. Revelar used his disguised appearance to impersonate a goblin called Joe. Kiara again advocated negotiation before violence.

### The Artifact and the Aberration

Beyond the passage, a more intelligent goblin displayed the sought-after artifact: a small gold circlet with runic markings suspended from a necklace.

![[Artifact.png]]

When negotiation failed, the party entered combat. Revelar used magical [[Darkness]], and [[Hunger of Hadar]] was also cast. The darkness concealed disturbing sounds, including cracking bones and tearing flesh. When it cleared, the goblins were no longer visible. Above the group, clinging to the high ceiling, was a grotesque, pale creature with numerous faces and eyes. The artifact hung from one of its arms.

![[Artifact-Spawned Abberation.png|376]]

The creature fled and vanished ahead of the pursuing adventurers. The party suddenly experienced a disorienting change in gravity and surroundings. They found themselves suspended over a lifeless, pale wasteland of jagged rocks, beneath a faded purple sky. Distant thunder sounded around a towering spire wrapped in a storm. They fell to the ground without injury. The party appeared to be somewhere beyond their familiar world.

> [!note] Table clarification
> Wiley stepped away from the session because he was unwell. Athlas's absence from the later encounter is **not** evidence that the creature transformed, consumed, or abducted him.
