## Log

### ✧ The World Before Our Story

The world is a vast and varied land, dominated by a great continent and surrounded by scattered islands. Its peoples live beneath the influence of gods who are not merely distant objects of worship, but living powers who walk the world and, in some cases, govern its kingdoms.

The boundaries between the planes are unlike those of other worlds. Rather than existing entirely apart from the material world, realms associated with the planes occupy physical regions of the land itself.

Among the known gods is [[Xurxus]], God of Balance, whose authority extends across the prosperous [[Kingdom of Xanthar]]. His realm is governed through the [[Church of Xurxus]], rather than a traditional monarchy. Within these lands are the [[Fey Forests]], home to communities of elves and other peoples, and the settlement of [[Riverrun]].

Elsewhere, the northern hills provide much of the continent's food, while the northern mountains are renowned for their dwarven craftsmanship. To the south lie forests inhabited by orcs, goblins, and ogres, and farther still, lands occupied by tieflings and other hellfolk. The eastern islands are home to birdfolk under the protection of [[Unrath]], God of the Heavens, while pirates inhabit islands to the west.

Other divine powers include [[Phoerah]], Goddess of the Abyss, and [[Nexalus]], God of Fruitful Lands.

Though the world enjoys a measure of peace, old conflicts and longstanding prejudices remain.

### ✧ Echoes of History

The world's history has been shaped by struggles involving both mortals and gods.

Long ago, dragons attempted to establish dominion over the world. Their ambitions were ultimately defeated by Xurxus, leaving behind ruins and a great crater in the southwest. The full circumstances of that conflict remain uncertain.

Another great conflict involved an alleged attempt to bring forth Hell. Accounts speak of a tremendous red beacon rising from the mountains and of the violence that followed. Tieflings and other hellfolk were subsequently driven into the southern reaches of the world.

The precise causes of these events, and the truth behind the surviving accounts, remain subjects of uncertainty.

### ✧ Kiara Bashara

[[Kiara Bashara]] is a 197-year-old eladrin from Riverrun, raised within the religious and cultural influence of Xurxus.

Her life was profoundly shaped by the loss of her older brother, [[Shayann]].

When Kiara was young, a priest associated with the Church of Xurxus threatened her. Shayann intervened and killed the priest to protect his sister. Despite his intentions, the killing brought severe condemnation. Shayann fled and was eventually banished, and even the mention of his name became forbidden.

Kiara never accepted the judgment against her brother. She believes he acted to protect her and that his punishment was unjust. Although she has no proof of his present whereabouts, she continues to believe he is alive.

Determined to one day restore his name, Kiara pursued the path of a paladin. She initially embraced the [[Oath of Redemption]] in the hope of gaining sufficient standing to challenge the injustice against Shayann, but over time its teachings became a genuine part of her beliefs.

She strives to resolve conflict through patience, compassion, and diplomacy, believing that even those who have done wrong may deserve the opportunity to change.

Her devotion to Xurxus remains sincere, though her brother's fate presents difficult unanswered questions about the church she serves.

### ✧ Revelar

[[Revelar]] is a drow who also serves Xurxus, though his methods differ sharply from Kiara's.

A mercenary and capable killer, Revelar has described himself as a _viper_ serving the church's council. He approaches danger pragmatically and is considerably more willing to employ lethal force against perceived enemies.

Where Kiara seeks opportunities for mercy and redemption, Revelar is prepared to act decisively against threats.

Despite their contrasting philosophies, both have been selected to undertake the same important assignment.

### ✧ A Summons from Xurxus

Before the beginning of the adventure, Xurxus personally summoned Kiara and Revelar.

A powerful artifact had been stolen, and Xurxus warned that its misuse could bring dire consequences. He entrusted the two with recovering it before it could be used for destructive purposes.

The artifact's identity, appearance, abilities, and current whereabouts were not revealed. The identity of the thief also remains unknown, although goblins have been mentioned as possible suspects.

The companions were directed toward [[Stal'hgite]], a town situated within the [[Iron Hills]] at the boundary between the mountains and southern forests.

There, they were instructed to visit a local tavern and seek information about someone known as the **Keeper of Knowledge**.

For Kiara, the mission represents more than an obligation to her god. It may also be an opportunity to earn Xurxus's trust and eventually gain the authority or influence necessary to pursue justice for Shayann.

### ✧ Where Our Story Begins

Kiara and Revelar have arrived in Stal'hgite, carrying a divine assignment and little information about the object they must recover.

They do not yet know who possesses the artifact, what dangers await them, or whether the Keeper of Knowledge will provide the answers they seek.

Two servants of the same god, divided by their approaches to justice and mercy, must now work together.

**Their search begins at a tavern in Stal'hgite.**
