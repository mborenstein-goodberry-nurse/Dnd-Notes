## Log

### Stranded in the Wasteland
The party found itself in an exposed, barren landscape after the pursuit through the goblin caves. [[Athlas]] could not find familiar stars or locate the tunnel they had entered. [[Kiara Bashara]] apologized to [[Revelar]] for their earlier conflict, urging unity in their dangerous situation. [[Fern]], a small fairy ranger with ivy-like hair, approached without initially being noticed. She and Revelar appeared to recognize one another, though the nature of their acquaintance remained unclear. Fern's arrival did not explain how the party reached this place.

Kiara cast [[Locate Object]] on the stolen artifact and briefly sensed it moving at extraordinary speed down the valley toward the distant spire. The group set off in that direction. The spell subsequently ended; the party retained the observed direction, not a continuous fix on the artifact.

### The Black Ichor
Descending along a ridge, the party saw slow-moving rivers and pools of black ichor. [[Nox]] threw a rock into one of the flows; the substance crept over it and dragged it under. Revelar tried to collect a sample but slipped into the ichor near the bank. It clung to him and caused necrotic harm. Kiara helped pull him upright, allowing him to escape with assistance. The substance appeared reluctant to release him.

The party sensed that something was nearby. Another traveler had been following them: [[Evard Underburrow]], a pale halfling wearing dark blue and black robes, carrying a gravekeeper's shovel. He introduced himself as a gravekeeper. Evard said he had felt compelled to travel, entered a clean cave, and fallen through a bright opening into this strange realm. He knew nothing about the artifact, but after consulting his faith he also felt drawn toward the spire. He joined the group.

### The Unchanging Sky and the Spire
As the party continued traveling, no sun or ordinary day-night progression became visible. The clouds and storm remained strangely constant. Athlas examined the heavens; through gaps in the clouds he saw not an ordinary starry sky but the suggestion of something vast beyond them. Its nature was not established. Even after considerable travel the spire remained very far away.

The group made several [[Survival]] checks during the journey.

### Noctis and Evard Hear Voices
[[Noctis]] spoke more freely than before and explained to Kiara that the programming imposed on his mechanical body seemed to have weakened. He reported having been confined for more than a century, originally being a tiefling, and being punished by an institution he called the **Ecclesiarchy** because of his closeness to [[Enos]], goddess of trickery. He said killing him had been considered inadvisable, so his body became his prison. Noctis communicated privately with Kiara by telepathy.

Noctis then heard and appeared to converse with a familiar voice he identified as Enos. This exchange took place before the group lost consciousness; the voice's actual source was not established.

Evard also heard a voice in his head. A successful Religion check identified it to him as his patron, whom he had never heard directly before. The voice said it had been reaching out for many years, indicated that Evard had not been intended to fall through the opening, and beckoned him toward the spire. Evard knelt in devotion before resuming travel.

### The Blackouts
The travelers began lapsing into a peculiar state one after another. Their eyes appeared glazed, but their bodies continued walking. Kiara was affected first. Fern, Evard, Revelar, Noctis, and Athlas followed; Nox remained aware longest and attempted to run ahead before succumbing as well. Those still conscious could not elicit normal responses from the affected companions. All experienced distinct visions.

### Kiara — The Clearing and Shayann
[[Kiara Bashara|Kiara]] suddenly found herself alone in an autumn forest. She heard her missing brother [[Shayann]] calling her into a game of hide-and-seek and pursued his voice. She repeatedly returned to a clearing that felt vaguely familiar. After several unsuccessful attempts to recognize the place, she finally understood it as the site of the incident in her past that led to Shayann's banishment.

Near a stump she encountered a grievously injured deer whose body was pierced by black ichor. The creature begged for release. Kiara tried [[Paladin#Lay on Hands|Lay on Hands]], but healing had no apparent effect. A successful Medicine check indicated that the ichor was penetrating its flesh and that it could not be saved. Kiara carefully considered her [[Oath of Redemption#Tenets of Redemption|Oath of Redemption]] and, believing a swift death was the merciful course, beheaded it with her sword. When she looked again, the body appeared to be Shayann's. Her vision ended. **The apparent death is part of the vision, not evidence of Shayann's actual fate.**

### Fern — A Recurring Nightmare
[[Fern]] found herself as a child in her family home built beneath a hill, surrounded by fire. She heard her brother calling for help. As she struggled through the burning building, her wings were singed and she lost the ability to fly. The escape route appeared to lengthen and distort while she crawled toward it. She eventually got outside and saw what appeared to be the charred bodies of both parents.

Her brother stood nearby, seemingly pleased by the destruction. When Fern pursued and confronted him, he expressed resentment over the family's devotion to [[Nexalus]], saying their parents had cared more for the god's wishes than his own. He departed, telling Fern that she would understand one day. **The DM explicitly clarified that Fern was reliving a recurring nightmare, not a literal memory.**

### Evard — The Field of the Dead
[[Evard Underburrow|Evard]] awoke pinned to a storm-soaked battlefield by a spear through his chest. Three indistinct figures—one taller and dark, one shorter, and one radiating shifting colors—mocked his repeated defiance. Around them lay multitudes of dead from many peoples. The figures discussed disposing of the bodies, mentioned having thrown many into the ocean on an earlier occasion, and suggested giving the corpses to an unidentified "her."

As Evard struggled, a figure tore the spear from him and the landscape tilted into a funnel. Evard and the dead slid toward a volcanic caldera. He recognized the mountain as one that had drawn his attention since childhood, visible from the hills near his graveyard. A voice he recognized as his patron urged him not to surrender to death and to oppose the injustice done to those who had died before their time.

Evard used magic and climbed through the piled corpses. **As he climbed, he performed the funeral rites of [[Ceremony]] for the bodies he could reach**, dedicating the act to death and the dignity of the dead. He fashioned part of an ascent with [[Mold Earth]], reached the rim while badly injured, saw a collapsing landscape, and fell unconscious. The vision does not establish that the battlefield or these past deaths truly existed.

### Revelar — The Spider
[[Revelar]] found himself walking through familiar dark woods near his home while thinking about an important summons to a palace. Distracted, he slipped into a cavern with no obvious way back. He heard a spider skittering and, before seeing it, addressed the darkness as if expecting a familiar presence. A large venomous spider emerged behind him.

Revelar attacked, first with a thrown dagger and then with a poisoned rapier. He defeated the spider after it failed to ensnare or strike him. During the finishing attack he declared that he had become the monster, mutilated it, deliberately prolonged its suffering with a little healing potion, and told it, **"You suffer as I suffer."** As he left through a passage, voices pleaded for help and accused him of trapping them. He refused, replying that they would learn as he had learned. He emerged into darkness, bloodied, and the vision ended. The spider's identity, the speakers' identities, and any historical connection remain unknown.

### Noctis — Enos and Zayne in the Void
[[Noctis]] awoke in a seemingly limitless dark void. He tried to contact [[Enos]], who appeared bewildered and afraid. An apparent Enos called forth [[Zayne]], described in the scene as a devil whom Noctis had seen before. Neither could explain the location. Noctis investigated whether his mechanical restrictions were responsible and determined that some other force appeared to be involved. The figures spoke of a place outside ordinary time, with past, present, and future entangled.

Noctis recalled fragmentary images of his imprisonment and a face he could not identify. The apparent Enos and Zayne then began bleeding black ichor from their eyes, laughed, and accused him of having been alone since entering the domain. They dissolved, and the voices fell silent. **Their conduct and claims occurred within the unreliable vision and cannot establish the real beings' actions or condition.**

### Athlas — The Amulet and the Escape
[[Athlas]] experienced himself as a small child trying to reach a magical amulet in a church-like space associated with his childhood home. His mother affectionately called him "Ash," gave him the amulet on a necklace, and said it could protect him and help him contact her. Athlas noticed that she seemed frightened beneath her reassurance.

His mother used powerful locking magic and opened a concealed passage within the home. She instructed Athlas to flee through the familiar tunnels, explaining that she and his father would remain to fight and would try to find him. Athlas was reluctant to leave. After the passage was sealed, he heard fighting, threats, and his mother's scream. He eventually fled and emerged to see his city burning. **The scene's historical accuracy, the amulet's existence outside the vision, and his parents' actual fates remain unconfirmed.**

### Nox — The Ship and the Sea Monster
[[Nox]] found herself on her ship amid a storm, initially at ease in the familiar setting. She heard a scream she recognized as her  **Captain's**, turned, and discovered a huge sea creature attacking the ship and crew. Its tentacles gripped the vessel and held the captain aloft; its body featured multiple mouths and many eyes, and it produced black ichor. [[Detect Magic]] found no unusual magical signature on the creature within the vision.

Nox attacked the tentacle holding the captain with [[Ice Knife]], freeing him. She determined that the remaining tentacles were the immediate threat to the ship, destroyed them through successive attacks, and finished the creature with her firearm. As it died, it sank and dissolved into mist. The ichor recalled what the party encountered in the Wasteland, but this likeness does not establish that the monster was real or shared an origin with the [[The Mind Gourger|Mind Gourger]].

### The Mind Gourger Appears
The party returned to awareness, positioned together before a dark cave in the same strange landscape. A monstrous entity emerged from black ichor: a towering amalgamation with multiple heads, teeth, claws, tentacles, horns, eyes, and other distorted features. It spoke directly into their minds in a chorus of familiar voices, including those of loved ones and divine figures. It claimed that their gods had abandoned them and declared that their fear would fuel its desire to live.

The session ended with the creature confronting the party; its exact relationship to the visions, black ichor, and stolen artifact remains unknown.

- Did [[The Mind Gourger]] cause the visions, and why were they individually tailored? See [[The Visions of the Wasteland]].
- Is the Mind Gourger's claim about sustaining itself on fear literal, metaphorical, or deceptive?
- What is the black ichor, and why did closely related imagery appear in Kiara's, Noctis's, and Nox's visions?
- Why did Noctis's mechanical restrictions appear weakened before the blackouts? Can he still contact Enos? See [[The Mystery of Noctis's Imprisonment]].
- Why was Evard's patron drawing him toward the spire, and does his childhood connection to a mountain have any bearing on the vision?
- Does the artifact remain in the direction of the spire, and where is the creature that carried it? See [[The Mystery of the Stolen Artifact]].
- What genuine personal history, if any, did the visions reveal about Fern's family, Athlas's parents, or Revelar's past?
