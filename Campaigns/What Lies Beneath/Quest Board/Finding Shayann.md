## Overview

For more than a century, [[Kiara Bashara]] has lived with the loss of her older brother, [[Shayann]].

When Kiara was young, Shayann killed a priest of [[Xurxus]] while protecting her from the priest's predatory intentions. Although Kiara believes her brother acted justly, he was condemned, forced to flee, and ultimately banished. Even speaking his name became forbidden.

Kiara has never accepted that judgment. She believes Shayann is still alive somewhere in the world, and she intends to find him.

Her devotion to Xurxus and her decision to become a Paladin of Redemption were initially motivated, in part, by her desire to gain the standing necessary to challenge her brother's condemnation. Over time, she came to genuinely embrace her oath.

Now that Kiara has been personally entrusted with a mission by Xurxus, she hopes that faithful service may eventually give her the opportunity to seek justice for Shayann.

**Her goal is not to obtain forgiveness for her brother. It is to establish that he never deserved condemnation in the first place.**

## Objectives

- [ ] Discover where Shayann went after his banishment.
- [ ] Find evidence that Shayann is still alive.
- [ ] Locate Shayann or someone who has encountered him.
- [ ] Reunite with Shayann.
- [ ] Learn what happened to him during his exile.
- [ ] Uncover who was responsible for his condemnation.
- [ ] Establish the truth of the circumstances surrounding the priest's death.
- [ ] Seek the restoration of Shayann's name and standing.

## Progression

### Before Session 1

- Shayann killed a priest who threatened Kiara.
- Shayann fled and was subsequently banished.
- Kiara was forbidden from speaking her brother's name.
- Kiara believes Shayann remains alive, although she has no confirmation of his whereabouts.
- Kiara became a Paladin of Redemption, initially hoping to acquire the authority needed to seek justice.
- Xurxus has personally selected Kiara for an important mission, potentially bringing her closer to an opportunity to petition him.

## Leads & Discoveries

*No confirmed leads to Shayann's present whereabouts.*

Potential avenues for investigation include the circumstances of his original banishment, the people who authorized it, and records or witnesses associated with the [[Church of Xurxus]].

These are possibilities for investigation, not established leads.

## Related Mystery

[[The Truth Behind Shayann's Banishment]]

The mystery tracks the evidence, competing accounts, and unanswered questions surrounding Shayann's exile. This quest tracks Kiara's personal objective of finding him and securing justice.

## Completion

This quest should remain active until Kiara has found Shayann and meaningfully resolved the injustice surrounding his banishment.

Finding him may be a major milestone without necessarily completing the entire quest.

## Additional Details

### Kiara's Motivation

Kiara's search is driven by love for her brother, her belief in his innocence, and her conviction that justice must be tempered by compassion.

Her Oath of Redemption may be tested if the people responsible for Shayann's suffering remain powerful or unrepentant.

### Unanswered Questions

- Where is Shayann now?
- Is he still alive?
- Does he know Kiara is searching for him?
- Who formally ordered his banishment?
- Did Xurxus know about the incident?
- What would it take to restore Shayann's name?
- How will Shayann respond if Kiara finds him?
