# The Five Creators — Retrospective Working Notes

> [!warning] ✧ Knowledge boundary
> This is **player and fellow-player retrospective lore**, **not** knowledge established for the traveling Sands party by the end of Session 21. DM **Wiley** has not yet confirmed the full account. Do not copy the pantheon hierarchy into the session journals or generic rules guide as established in-character fact.

## The five

The five highest creator gods are **Guile, Dread, Hostility, Vigor, and Purity**. A fellow player corrected an earlier good-versus-evil framing: these beings represent the concepts evoked by their names rather than fixed moral alignments. **Only Purity is female**; the others are male, by the fellow player's account. Dread, for example, concerns fear affecting benevolent and malevolent people alike.

**Guile is not the leader.** He is characterized as especially reasonable and perhaps the oldest, the latter still requiring confirmation from Wiley. Guile has **two Chosen**, reflecting the view that decisions should not be made with one mind alone. Each of the other four has one Chosen. Each god also has an associated arch-being; **Guile has two**.

| **Purity** | **Ghidorah**, dragon living in the **Islands of Moorne** (player account; geography not yet verified) | **Unmet / unknown**; earlier note says Aureal, but identity and category not reconciled |

The uploaded **Arch Demon Notes** also mention Bastion as an Arch Elemental and refer to an Arch Angel without securely matching all names, types and gods. Record those references as **unreconciled**, not proof that Solas = Bastion or that Aureal has a specified classification.

## What the party actually encountered through Session 21

- **Session 19:** The party met Narci, a blind monk/shaman who said she served Vigor and could consult him. There is **no direct Vigor appearance** in this session. Narci's status as Vigor's Chosen was clarified **retrospectively** by the player.
- **Session 20:** In the Caldera, the group found **Havoc's Sword / Hero's Bane**. Its divine significance as a purported arch-being's vessel was **not established in-character**.
- **Session 21:** Weary reclaimed the blade and seemingly bargained with its voice. Its soul-consuming nature and Havoc's wider position remain matters of historical uncertainty and retrospective lore.

## Earlier-campaign testimony and things to ask Wiley

The prior Astodan-series campaign reportedly involved meetings with Guile, Dread, Iferit, Withengar, and perhaps Purity, with Jodie becoming Dread's Chosen. Iferit and Withengar were reportedly freed; the exact method and dates are not recovered. The circumstances of Havoc's imprisonment and relation to Hostility also need authoritative confirmation.

**Questions for Wiley:** Is Guile oldest? Can the gods' conceptual domains be defined precisely? What are the names and types of Vigor's and Purity's arch-beings? What are the correct spellings and classification of Intrix and Ghidorah? Are Bastion and Solas separate? How do Chosen compare to arch-beings, and does the title 'arch-being' formally apply in this setting?
