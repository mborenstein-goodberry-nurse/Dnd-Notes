# The Five Creators - Retrospective Working Notes

> [!warning] ✧ Knowledge boundary
> This page records **retrospective player lore and a November 18, 2026 interview with DM Wiley**, not blanket knowledge held by Sands of Desolation characters through Session 21. See individual linked notes for what the traveling party actually encountered.

## Five Creators

The five primordial gods are [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Guile|Guile]], [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Dread|Dread]], [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Hostility|Hostility]], [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Vigor|Vigor]], and [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Purity|Purity]]. **No established leader or age ranking** exists. The player specifically clarified that Guile is **not the eldest**. He may be the most willing to listen to the others before deciding, but that is the player's tentative understanding, not a ranking.

Wiley confirmed in the November 18 recording that the gods **embody the concepts or emotions named for them**, not simple moral alignments (03:42-04:45). He explicitly connects Guile to cunning/slyness and Hostility to violence, war and murder, without identifying Hostility as morally evil. Other players recalled Dread as the embodiment of fear. **Only Purity is female**, per fellow-player correction; the others are male. Domain details not explicitly described by Wiley remain provisional.

**Earlier than the Five?** There is **no recorded history** of what came before them (02:55-03:40); this does not prove literal nothingness. The recording's clipped opening mentions a nameless creator, possibly an out-of-universe reference to Wiley; **do not treat it as evidence of a sixth god**.

## Chosen and arch-beings

**Wiley confirmed (05:51-06:45):** The Five created arch-beings around **20-50 years after the Five's emergence** (approximate estimate). The arch-beings are the **eldest of their respective entity types**. Chosen came later, from sentient beings. The exact method of selecting or empowering a Chosen was not fully explained.

## Lesser gods and mortal worship

**DM-confirmed (34:11-35:44):** The Five created the later gods as **buffers for mortal devotion**. They did not want worship directed at themselves because the additional power from devotion could fuel rivalry and conflict among them. The precise chronological dates and subsequent relationships remain unresolved.

## Earlier campaigns and present party

The player recalls encountering **Guile, Dread, Iferit and Withengar** in a previous Astodan campaign, and perhaps briefly Purity. **Iferit and Withengar were freed**, by player recollection; no date, method or resulting status is established here. Jodie became Dread's Chosen at the conclusion of the earlier campaign. Intrix and Bastion are reported as encountered in other adventures, but their precise placement in the recovered Sands chronology is not established.

- **Session 19:** Sands party met [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Narci|Narci]], who said she served Vigor. Her Chosen identity comes from later player recollections; **Vigor himself did not appear**.
- **Session 20:** The party found [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Other/Havoc's Sword|Havoc's Sword (Hero's Bane)]] in the Caldera; the arch-being association was not established then.
- **Session 21:** Weary recovered the sword and an unspecified bargain was reported; do not treat the world hierarchy or the bargain's exact terms as known in-character.
