## ✧ Abilities & Reactions

### Class & Subclass Features

| Feature | When to use it | At-the-table reminder |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Wild Magic Surge (level 1) | Once per turn when casting a spell of level 1+ | Roll the current **Surge Die**. A 1 triggers a Wild Magic Surge; each Sorcery Point spent on Metamagic also increases the trigger range by 1. |
| Surge Die (level 1) | Track after surges and long rests | Begins as **d12**, then can erode through **d10 -> d8 -> d6 -> d4** as surges occur. A long rest restores one step. Confirm the current die with the player sheet. |
| Tides of Chaos (level 1) | Advantage on an attack roll, check, or save | Regains use after a long rest **or immediately after making a Surge Check**. |
| Bend Magic (level 6) | Bonus action; usable once per long rest | For up to 1 minute, check when magic is used within 120 feet; surges from this feature are **Good** only. Ends after triggering a surge. |
| Controlled Chaos (level 14) | When surging or casting | On the outcome d20, only a **1** is Bad (2-15 Neutral, 16-20 Good). Spend 1 Sorcery Point when casting to skip a Surge Check. |
| Sorcery Surges (level 18) | When a spell triggers a surge | Can apply up to 3 additional Sorcery Points for free to the spell or the surge, where allowed by the GM. |
