# Sands of Desolation — Before Session 1

> [!info] Historical source and scope
> Reconstructed from the player's retrospective account provided **2026-11-05**, plus the surviving original Google Docs information sheets, [[Old Original Notes - Diane and Weary Quest - Google Docs.pdf]] (especially pp. 5–10, 38–43, 46–47). The document was maintained during Sessions 1–27, so later-session additions are **not** assumed to be Session 0 knowledge. This note is for **pre-Session-1 context only**. Do not use it to backfill the outcome of mysteries revealed later in Sands of Desolation.

## Before the first campaign

### Diane Aceso Quest

[[Diane]] was raised in [[Sun Home]], a sub-kingdom of [[Sirvanna]], by her adoptive father [[Gavin Aceso]]. Her old character notes name [[Meridian]], in Sirvanna, as her hometown (the location of the dragon attack is not established). She grew up with the Sun Religion's devotion to Apollo and pursued a calling as a Life Domain cleric, later taking two levels in Circle of Stars druid. A dragon attacked her village and badly burned her, leaving serious trauma.

### Weary Quest

[[Weary]], a tiefling Wild Magic sorcerer, grew up in a prominent metalworking family in [[Tangambalanga]], [[Griffin Empire]]. His father [[Ernon Quest]] tried to control his dangerous surges through a blood vial and other methods, eventually forbidding him from casting. His mother is listed as [[Divari Quest]] in the historic notes. Conflict with his younger brother [[Warrick]] escalated into a fight. After Warrick pushed their father aside, Weary accidentally released a devastating magical surge that scarred Warrick, broke a horn, and warped the home. Weary fled in guilt.

### Their meeting

Diane and Weary met at a tavern and began traveling together. She was comparatively sheltered and fearful of the wider world; he loved travel but avoided responsibility. Weary increasingly used his magic despite the surges, fearing the consequences of suppressing them for too long.

## The first Astodan-series campaign

The party was sent into [[Resurgence|the Resurgence]], another sub-kingdom of Sirvanna, to stop recurring demon-summoning rituals. Neighboring domains, including Sun Home, had intervened there before. The original pre-session worldbuilding notes explain its continued tolerance through its production of valuable trade resources, though the kingdom's true political motives are not independently verified.

During the expedition, Weary acquired [[Havoc's Sword|a powerful magical sword]] containing [[Havoc]], a malicious entity that whispered to him, encouraged violence, consumed his soul, and prevented him from telling Diane what was happening. He could not get rid of the sword.

At the ritual chamber, the antagonist was revealed to be Weary's younger brother Warrick. [[Wonder]], a blue tiefling traveling with the group and secretly Warrick's wife and accomplice, seized Havoc's sword from Weary. Removing it left a red scar stretching from his right hand to his right eye, marking damage to his soul.

Warrick attempted to summon a demon army. The ritual instead caused enormous **necrotic spires** to appear around the world. Their purpose and consequences were not understood at that point; horrific creatures, notably Nightwalkers, appeared to be associated with the growing danger.

## The intervening year

Diane and Weary remained together and married as a commitment to protect each other. They stayed in [[Sun Home]]. The original character information sheets document their traveling caravan, [[Aceso Aid]], and their two horses, **Dawn** and **Dusk**. The player tentatively recalls receiving the cart after the wedding, but its exact arrival and the giver are unknown. Weary continued correspondence with [[Ernon Quest]] and [[Divari Quest]], but their letters unexpectedly stopped.

They traveled together to his family home in [[Tangambalanga]] and found **the family missing**. After searching without success, they were summoned to [[Isabel's Castle]]. The family's disappearance was still unresolved at the start of Sands of Desolation.

## Opening situation — Session 1

**Starting-character snapshot:** The original level plans list Diane as **Life cleric 7 / Stars druid 2** and Weary as **Wild Magic sorcerer 9**. These are starting levels only; do not overwrite present-day character-sheet values. The character-description pages also establish their appearances, personalities, hobbies, and equipment; see [[Diane]], [[Weary]], and [[Aceso Aid]].


The couple's arrival at [[Isabel's Castle]] sets up [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Session Journal/It Begins...|Session 1]], where [[Queen Isabel]] tasks the party with investigating two places that might offer aid against the spreading corruption: [[Towers of Icarus]] and [[Luciferian Caldera]]. This session connection follows the existing journal; no additional Session 1 events are inferred here.
