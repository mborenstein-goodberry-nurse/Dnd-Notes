## Log

### The mound of flesh

The conflict in the [[Worlds/Astodan/Places/Luciferian Caldera|Luciferian Caldera]] resumed where the previous session ended: the company was still fighting the living heap of mangled bodies that held the sword [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Weary|Weary]] recognized. In the confusion, [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Grayson|Grayson]] tore up or damaged the **fake deck** purchased from the goblin Zoff. The mound attacked him while [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Arthur Morgan|Arthur]] charged into the mass and struck it with his fists. [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Thornwick|Thornwick]] joined the fight.

The party's attraction to the weapon had not entirely vanished. Zach's notes indicate that [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Diane|Diane]] was no longer held and still wanted the sword. [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Jonks|Jonks]] attempted to pull the blade from the mound, but it did not immediately come free. Meanwhile, **Linus Lightfoot** pulled the person Zach called **"Haley"** out of a hole with an exceptionally successful effort. Moments later, she struck Linus hard enough to nearly kill him. In earlier sessions, "Haley" was an inconsistent table name for [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Amorpha|Amorpha]], and the player agrees this sounds like Amorpha; the identification is **likely but not certain for this particular scene**.

Weary pressed toward the sword, using [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Haste|Haste]] and [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Mind Sliver|Mind Sliver]] and apparently teleporting toward the weapon during the struggle. Someone attempted [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Command|Command]], but Zach's shorthand does not securely establish the caster, target, or outcome. Grayson got atop the mound; the notes describe him becoming "big" before going down, without enough detail to establish an exact magical effect.

The flesh creature attacked Weary, Arthur and Grayson, **swallowing both Weary and Grayson**. Grayson fell but retaliated using [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Hellish Rebuke|Hellish Rebuke]]. Arthur ultimately struck the mound and **killed it**. In the fighting Arthur also brought Weary down, while Thornwick tried unsuccessfully to restrain or stop Arthur. [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Diane|Diane]] subsequently healed Weary and Grayson. The notes include a joke that left Jonks with two points of inspiration, though the exact in-game context is not recoverable.

### Jonks frees the sword

With the mound defeated, Jonks used a magical or conjured hand to **pull [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Other/Havoc's Sword|Havoc's Sword]] free**. Zach records that **his hand became corrupted** during the attempt. Arthur leaped for the sword but failed to seize it. Linus also tended to his own wounds.

The person written as "Haley" approached Jonks from behind, concealed herself from him, and **stabbed him to break the hand's hold**. Zach writes that this worked. Whether the stab severed a magical link, interrupted the sword's influence, or reversed the corruption permanently is not clear from the shorthand alone. The player does not recall any lasting consequences to Jonks's hand.

### Weary's bargain

Weary then **took the sword**. The notes describe a surge of demonic energy, as well as a sensation of **the missing part of his soul**. A voice from the blade echoed through Weary's own voice. Zach writes that **"a deal was made" with the sword**. The weapon was described as **sentient** and full of demonic energy.

This is a major change from the previous session, when the sword was still embedded in the mound: **the party has now recovered it, and Weary has personally handled it**. The notes do **not** establish that Weary's soul fragment was permanently restored, reveal the exact bargain, or identify the voice with certainty. **Hero's Bane is the sword's original name**, as confirmed by the player during review.

> [!note]- OOC recollection — the proposed bargain (not a confirmed transcript)
> The player tentatively remembers that Weary would **free Havoc** in order to regain his missing soul and avoid being **consumed by the sword**. The player clarifies that **Havoc wanted to consume souls**. Consumption was also intrinsic to the sword's nature and Havoc's survival — likened to breathing — rather than something he could simply stop doing. This does **not** mean Havoc was unwilling to consume souls. This is retrospective interpretation, **not verified wording of the Session 21 agreement**, and does not establish that any soul restoration or release occurred here.

### Coins, a possible third persona, and rest

After the immediate danger, Grayson tried something involving **bones**, with no obvious success recorded. Zach also reports that Grayson **embedded another coin within his skeleton**; a later shorthand line mentions a **hand**. The coin's source, appearance, location, and function remain unclear. There is no basis yet for identifying it with the coin the Fox earlier gave Jonks.

Zach's notes additionally say that **Arthur has a "3rd personality."** That is an important lead given what was already known about Arthur and [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Other/Damien Castellanos|Damien]]. The player does not remember a specific reveal and thinks **Soren** would be the explanation if a third presence was indeed seen; no independent confirmation of that Session 21 appearance is preserved. The party eventually **took a long rest**, closing the documented session.

## ✧ Questions & Mysteries

- Did Weary's **tentatively recalled promise to free Havoc** accurately describe the bargain with [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Other/Havoc's Sword|Hero's Bane]], and was any of his missing soul actually restored?
- Who or what spoke through Weary when he took **Hero's Bane**, and how did the sword's soul consumption affect Havoc and his bargain?
- What exactly happened to **Jonks's corrupted hand**? Was the corruption reversed, or only his hold on the sword broken?
- Did **Soren** manifest during this encounter, or was Zach's "3rd personality" note an interpretation of Arthur's behavior?
- What is the **coin Grayson embedded into his skeletal body**? Is it related to other mysterious coins, or unrelated?
- Was the person Zach called "Haley" definitely [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Amorpha|Amorpha]] (as the player considers likely), and what led to her attack on Linus and Jonks?
