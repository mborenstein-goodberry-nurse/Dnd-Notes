## Log

### ✧ The Altar's Aftermath

The company remained in the ruined altar chambers of a cavern in the [[Worlds/Astodan/Places/Grey Mountains|Grey Mountains]], where [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Baba Eris Whitewood|Baba Eris Whitewood]], a [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Bestiary/Mouth of Grolantor|Mouth of Grolantor]], and numerous duergar had fallen during the previous session. Several companions searched the surrounding chambers while the rest regrouped, battered and exhausted. They had advanced to **level 12** but had not yet taken a long rest.

[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Thornwick|Thornwick]] searched the hag's remains and recovered his family's **signet ring**. Touching it brought a flood of memories: his son, the kingdom he had lost, and events the hag's magic had obscured. He dropped to his knees under the weight of the recollection and suffered a level of exhaustion. [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Diane|Diane]] returned to him a picture frame found in a nearby chamber, depicting a woman and a child; the company supported him as he struggled to process what he remembered. His bone mask **remained fused to his face**, and neither his son nor the full scope of the curse was recovered.

The company examined a sinister coin recovered amid the aftermath. It carried a fox-like or skeletal-hand insignia and was suffused with necrotic magic. Handling it caused [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Diane|Diane]] pain and a dark ichor-like effect before the substance withdrew into the coin; [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Thornwick|Thornwick]] and [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Grayson|Grayson]] appeared unaffected. The group speculated about the hag, a lich, and other possible connections, but **the recording does not confirm that this coin is the same object as Falatir's concealed coin or the Fox's earlier token**.

[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Diane|Diane]] cast [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Aura of Vitality|Aura of Vitality]] to restore the injured party. After discussing the recovered mining equipment and fuel, [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Thornwick|Thornwick]] gathered everyone onto dwarven stone and used his newly obtained axe to **transport the group to the city**. Their carriage and animals had been left near the cavern entrance; the subsequent collection arrangements are not clearly captured.

### ✧ A Pickpocket in Anvil's Flame

In [[Worlds/Astodan/Places/Anvil's Flame|Anvil's Flame]], [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Isabelle|Isabelle]] was overwhelmed by how much technology, construction, and industry had advanced beyond the world she remembered. She relied on [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Diane|Diane]]'s help navigating the city and expressed unease with its mechanical wonders.

A **hobgoblin named [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Amorpha|Amorpha]]** stumbled into the party after being thrown out of a nearby shop. The newcomer promptly relieved several companions of their coin purses. [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Diane|Diane]] and [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Arthur Morgan|Arthur]] detected the theft; Arthur introduced himself while recovering his own belongings, and the group negotiated the rest back amid a mixture of warnings and jokes. Amorpha remained with the party and accompanied them to the Mechanical Wonders. [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Krieg|Krieg]], now appearing as a small imp companion, also remained with the company.

### ✧ The Reward and Gideon's Hemisphere

At the [[Worlds/Astodan/Organizations/Mechanical Wonders|Mechanical Wonders]], [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Drax|Drax]] and [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Thrax|Thrax]] learned that the raiders and hag had been defeated and the stolen equipment freed. The guild produced **seven bags containing 500 gp each** as payment. The party brought out the remains of the hag, to the discomfort of their hosts. In a particularly irreverent display, someone used [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Mage Hand|Mage Hand]] to lift her severed head, [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Prestidigitation|Prestidigitation]] to clear away blood, and [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Minor Illusion|Minor Illusion]] to make the head appear to speak. [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Thornwick|Thornwick]] ended the display by splitting it with his axe. The company then requested further examination of the hemisphere once lodged in [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Gideon|Gideon]]. The members also discussed pieces of psychic-attuned metal recovered from the duergar machinery, potential armor modifications, and other crafting work.

Drax's findings transformed the company's understanding of the hemisphere. It was **one half of a much larger rejuvenation engine** dating at least to the age of the **Equalizer War**. The portion recovered from Gideon was the **destructive or matter-consuming half**. A missing, complementary portion would restore and reuse what it consumed, potentially enabling the machine to rejuvenate lands on a vast scale — perhaps even the Wastes.

Alone, however, the hemisphere was dangerous. It could drain life from whatever it attached to, cause necrotic corruption, and continue consuming matter despite being separated from its proper mechanism. The protective sheet Drax had fitted earlier was only a temporary restraint and would eventually degrade. The full machine, location of the other half, and safe method of containment were **unknown**. The group considered whether Gideon might want the object returned or destroyed, but these were hypotheses, not established intentions.

Drax agreed to handle the discovery discreetly, although the possibility of informing the queen was raised. The company understood that possession of this object could put them at risk.

[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Thornwick|Thornwick]] asked for his axe to hover instead of requiring him to carry its great weight. The brothers completed an enchantment that allowed it to float and respond to him. One founder referred to its age and rarity, with a name sounding like **“Axe of the Dwarven Scorch”** in the transcript; the exact name requires verification. They warned that some guilds or factions might object to its possession.

### ✧ Research and Commissions

[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Isabelle|Isabelle]] and [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Diane|Diane]] visited a large library in Anvil's Flame. Isabelle found [[Worlds/Astodan/Places/Sirvanna|Sirvanna]] among the historical kingdoms and began reading about its distant past, comparing it with Diane's knowledge of [[Worlds/Astodan/Places/Sun Home|Sun Home]]. She also asked about mechanical animals and their place alongside living creatures. Using [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Speak with Animals|Speak with Animals]] to consult her animal companions, she found them receptive to the idea of a mechanical companion.

[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Diane|Diane]] sought books on the present political landscape, magical disguises, and the capabilities of hags. The library visit did **not** settle whether Isabelle had once been a ruler of Sirvanna or establish an exact time elapsed since her first life.

Later, [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Isabelle|Isabelle]] returned to the guild and offered **500 gp** toward the construction of a **steel defender**, hoping a mechanical companion might help her understand the strange technology around her. The proposed companion was commissioned; its completion and exact form are not established here.

[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Diane|Diane]] visited a mobile alchemist's shop operated by **Jeremy** to identify potions found during the cavern expedition. The conversation describes a potion of [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Clairvoyance|Clairvoyance]], a **Potion of Watchful Rest**, and a potion made up of seven separate colorful layers (the final name is indistinct, resembling “Polychromy”). The shopkeeper accidentally sprayed her with a cherry-scented **beholder-derived perfume**, producing disorientation and hallucinations. Diane used [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Sending|Sending]] to contact [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Weary|Weary]], warning that she had been drugged and asking him to come to her. He helped recover her; an application of *Lay on Hands* eased the immediate episode, but she was warned of effects that could recur later that night.

### ✧ Dreams in the Night

The company rested at the guild, where several members experienced striking dreams and visions.

**[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Thornwick|Thornwick]].** He saw his missing son slipping from reach as the slain hag appeared to taunt him. Her appearance changed into an amorphous, dark, ichor-covered figure that claimed to have played with Thornwick and his companions for a long time, promised to keep his son hidden, and suggested Thornwick would not recognize the child even if they met. Thornwick tried and failed to strike the apparition before waking in distress. He sought comfort from [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Arthur Morgan|Arthur]], who made room for him to sleep nearby. The inherited journal associates the figure with [[Worlds/Astodan/People/The Fox|the Fox]], but the figure did **not** identify itself by name in the surviving recording.

**[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Amorpha|Amorpha]].** In a dream of pickpocketing through city streets, a noble-looking man caught Amorpha's arm and warned that unnamed pursuers knew what she had done. The man declared himself **“the king of the desert”**, spoke of a continuing cycle, and hinted he might recruit her to a guild. His form shifted to a rough, pirate-like figure marked across one reddish eye and wearing a hat with a red feather. His actual identity and allegiance remained unknown.

**[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Arthur Morgan|Arthur]].** Arthur initially felt at ease, then sensed that **chains had broken** and that something within him was awakening. He saw [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Thornwick|Thornwick]] dead and shattered, and [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Diane|Diane]] and [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Weary|Weary]] reaching toward each other without touching. Upon waking, Arthur noticed **red marks forming across his hands or arms** and concealed them beneath his sleeves. The cause was not explained.

**Grayson.** [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Grayson|Grayson]] dreamed of his patron, [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Xerac|Xerac]], who interrupted a card game and discussed the newly acquired hemisphere. The patron cautioned him against carelessly exploiting its powerful necrotic magic and suggested learning more about it. The exchange did not establish precisely what Xerac knew or intended.

**[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Isabelle|Isabelle]].** She dreamed of an earlier life, including a dance or formal gathering, before an arrow pierced her chest. She died in another person's arms as her spirit seemed to fracture. The attacker was indistinct, though **dark hair** stood out in her recollection. She remembered being loved by the figure holding her, but could not clearly recover a face or name. The vision offered no reliable identity for her killer.

**[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Weary|Weary]].** He rested relatively peacefully but noticed [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Diane|Diane]]'s disturbance and tried to comfort her.

**[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Diane|Diane]].** Diane relived the devastation of [[Worlds/Astodan/Places/Sun Home|Sun Home]] after a dragon attack when she was a child. In the vision, she searched the damaged town for medicinal herbs and feared she saw a dragon overhead. She then seemed to wake in the guild beside [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Weary|Weary]], only to face the familiar **rose-masked woman**, who spoke about Diane's past, her name, and unfinished business in the Gilded Lands. Weary appeared unconscious with a dart in his neck; Diane could neither wake him nor successfully cast [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Thaumaturgy|Thaumaturgy]] to amplify her call for help, and the woman's knife struck her.

[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Diane|Diane]] then **woke for real**. Weary was unharmed and responsive, and the immediate confrontation had been part of the dream. The earlier beholder-perfume exposure may have contributed to its intensity, though the real source of the specific apparition was not proven. She hurried to ask about the hemisphere and the masked woman. A founder assured her that the hemisphere was in safekeeping and identified the person described as **[[Worlds/Astodan/People/Pleasure|Pleasure]]**, a powerful figure among the leaders of the [[Worlds/Astodan/Places/Gilded Lands|Gilded Lands]]. This was the first clear name provided for the woman whom Diane had called **[[Worlds/Astodan/People/Pleasure|Rose]]**.

- Who is the self-described **king of the desert**, and what is pursuing Amorpha?
- What caused Arthur's broken-chains vision and the red marks on his arms?
- What does Grayson's patron know about the rejuvenation engine and the hemisphere's potential?
- Where is the missing life-giving half of the rejuvenation engine, and what should be done with the destructive half in the meantime?
- What is the rejuvenation engine's relationship to the **Equalizer War** beyond dating to the same era?
- Who killed Isabelle in her vision, and what does that memory reveal about her past?
- How does [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Pleasure|Pleasure]] know Diane's history, and why does she continue to threaten her?
