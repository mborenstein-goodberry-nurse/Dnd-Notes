## Log

### ✧ A Quiet Morning — Briefly

The company wakes in [[Worlds/Astodan/Places/New Phyrexia|New Phyrexia]] after the long night of Wild Magic. The temporary effects that had left everyone transformed or behaving strangely are gone; [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Weary|Weary]] is no longer under the surges that carried into the private meeting with the Pirate King. The group gathers for breakfast and discusses whether to take the dangerous path across the Wastes or travel by sea. They ultimately prepare the caravan for the overland route, with [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Noisy Owlbear|Noisy Owlbear]] serving as a guide.

[[Worlds/Astodan/People/Graves Hellborne|Graves Hellborne]] arrives in a visibly tense state, apologizes for interrupting, and asks the party to clear the meeting room. A high-priority guest is coming: a woman with whom he has made several arrangements in the past. Graves will not explain the substance of those dealings. As the companions leave, they pass a woman in **pink armor** walking up to meet him. The DM identifies her as [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Pleasure|Pleasure]], the leader of the **Guild of Legends**; Diane recognizes her and tries to keep from attracting attention.

During the passing encounter, **Pleasure personally slips an object into Diane's pocket**, as the player later confirmed. Outside, Diane withdraws a **note** that addresses her as a girl of **Sun Home** (player-confirmed reading of the muddled transcription) and promises to see her again soon. Diane crumples the message, visibly distressed, and lets it fall. Owlbear picks it up, but to him the page is **blank**. **Pleasure delivered the note**, but whether she wrote or enchanted it—and why only Diane could read it—remain unknown.

Some companions discuss a visit to a potion shop while others briefly separate toward the castle or caravan. Their intentions in those side conversations are not fully clear from the transcription.

### ✧ The Potion Seller and Jonks

[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Thornwick|Thornwick]] and Owlbear enter a potion shop. Inside is a **red kobold**, only about two feet tall, and a **yuan-ti merchant**. Owlbear seeks a potion to enhance speed, but the shopkeeper presses the group for payment and offers a yellowish vial. The bargaining becomes absurd when the contents are described as **urine**, prompting mistrust of the merchant and considerable argument. The proprietor ultimately throws down a bottle that magically sends the visitors out of the shop and locks them out; nobody confirms that they obtained a usable potion.

The kobold gives his name as **[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Jonks|Jonks]]** (spelled aloud at the table as J-O-N-K-S). He is a **Necromancy Wizard** (confirmed by the player), and says he is **running from a very wealthy, dangerous necromancer**, hoping for safety in numbers. Thornwick and Owlbear invite him to travel with them. Jonks agrees, introducing himself to the others when the small group rejoins the caravan. He gives no reliable name for the person pursuing him during this recording. His first appearance here is the reason for the `new` marker.

At the cart, Owlbear lifts the vehicle to help the companions fit the treads acquired during their earlier stay in New Phyrexia. The company also introduces Jonks to **Sprout**, Arthur's peculiar little carrot-shaped companion. With the cart repaired and the group assembled, they set out from the Pirate Kingdom, Dawn and Dusk pulling their shared [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Other/Aceso Aid|Aceso Aid]] caravan.

### ✧ A Guide, a Wrong Turn, and a Gathering Storm

The company soon discovers that Owlbear's confidence in his familiarity with [[Worlds/Astodan/Places/The Wastes|the Wastes]] is not matched by his navigation. Instead of hugging the shoreline, the caravan veers inland and seems to circle back on itself, passing views of an enormous **Necrotic Spire**. The original written journal and Zach’s notes additionally report **Nightwalkers visible at a distance while leaving New Phyrexia** and necrotic energy emanating from the Spire; those details are **written-source testimony**, not independently captured in A04, and do not identify the later storm pursuer as a Nightwalker. Diane and Thornwick argue that the cart is headed the wrong way and take charge of the reins. Their checks suggest that the party has lost a significant amount of ground and may be nearer the route toward the Gilded Lands than intended.

A storm rises over the sands. It is far too violent and moves too directly toward the caravan to appear natural. Flashes of lightning illuminate dense rain and blowing sand, while the ground itself begins to tremble. Diane asks Apollo for guidance using [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Divination|Divination]]: would it be safer to reach the shore, or to remain and prepare? The answer is terse—**the outcome will be the same**—and does little to resolve the argument. The party first digs a sand barrier and considers shielding the horses behind the cart, but the storm closes faster than expected.

The companions decide to flee back toward **New Phyrexia**, hoping to reach shelter before they are overtaken. As they rush the horses along, lightning strikes the ground and **undead emerge from some of the impact sites**. More alarming still, something huge is moving beneath the sand in pursuit. A **skeletal head** emerges behind the wagon, threatening the wheels, seemingly connected to the unnatural storm itself.

### ✧ The Pursuit and the Broken Record

The party scrambles to respond as the creature closes in. At one point the DM narrates **Noisy Owlbear** rushing into the storm, using [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Gust of Wind|Gust of Wind]] to force the foe back, but being crushed or swallowed in the attack. The ensuing dialogue speaks of his sacrifice and death. **Owlbear dies in the confrontation**, as the player has confirmed. Neither the original journal nor Zach's dated page mentions the death; the recording preserves this important moment that the written accounts missed.

The pursuing creature unleashes **necrotic breath** across the cart. The DM calls for Dexterity saves and describes **88 necrotic damage** on a failed save; Weary succeeds and takes **44**, while others suffer the full attack. The party debates urging Dawn and Dusk onward or trying to heal and fight. Diane invokes her druidic **Starry Form (Archer)** and fires a luminous arrow toward the pursuer. **The recording ends in the middle of the combat.** Nothing about the outcome of this particular exchange can be recovered from that source alone.

### ✧ What the Written Notes Preserve

Two contemporaneous note sets—this journal's original log and Zach's handwritten page—continue beyond the recording's abrupt end. They say that **Thornwick wakes the threatening creature**, after which **Weary involuntarily deafens the entire party in a Wild Magic surge** and Thornwick catches or delays the pursuer with an **[[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Treasure/Immovable Rod|Immovable Rod]]**, giving the caravan time to escape. The precise order of those final actions relative to the recording is **not established**. The written accounts agree that the company eventually reaches **[[Worlds/Astodan/Places/Foo Fighters|Foo Fighter territory]]**. The storm runs into the border and **does not cross it**. At or near the border, **Diane experiences a direct warning not to trespass**, recalled by the player as **similar to receiving a Sending**. No physical note or visible messenger is documented. The speaker, exact words, and whether this was literally the *Sending* spell are not known; the apparent territorial connection is an inference from timing.

The same handwritten and journal accounts preserve a **separate Grayson development**: [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Grayson|Grayson]] wins a dice contest against **Graves Hellborne**, secures his freedom, and afterward raises a drink with the company. The notes also say that **Creed is dead**. A neighboring line reads *"Ate Ben's wedding ring"*; the player clarifies it almost certainly refers to **Grayson consuming Isabelle’s Ring of Mind Shielding**, consistent with his established habit of consuming magic items. **Grayson is back with the party by around this point**, according to player recollection, though A04 does not capture the dice game or reunion. The **time, place, deal terms, and sequence** are not forced into an unsupported chronology.
