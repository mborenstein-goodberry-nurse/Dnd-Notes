## Log

### ✧ A Hemisphere and a Falling Tower

The surviving account begins immediately after the fight with [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Gideon|Gideon the Judge]]. The **original historical journal** says [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Thornwick|Thornwick]] used inspiration to tear the **hemisphere** from Gideon's chest. The player has also confirmed that Thornwick succeeded on a **Strength check** to accomplish this in the final stretch of the preceding Session 5 fight. The recording **B09 begins after that moment**: Gideon is gone, the relic is in the party's possession, and the nearby woodland is beginning to regain life. The recovery seems to have relieved some of the land's corruption, although the DM specifically described rejuvenation as a **slow process**, not an instant cure for the Wastes.

The central structure began crumbling. To help conceal the recovered object, [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Weary|Weary]] used *[[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Nystul's Magic Aura|Nystul's Magic Aura]]* on the hemisphere to make it register as nonmagical to certain means of detection. A surviving **[[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Bestiary/Radiant Idol|Radiant Idol]]**, previously imprisoned in another chamber, began drawing material from the surrounding towers toward the center. The idol did not attack as the party fled; instead, she attempted to preserve or reconstruct the central tower. The connecting bridges pulled apart and began changing shape.

At one widening gap, Weary teleported Diane across with *[[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Vortex Warp|Vortex Warp]]*, while using his [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Treasure/Astral Shard|Astral Shard]] to move himself. Farther along, the group was still hundreds of feet above the ground when the bridge began separating from the spire. **Weary and Diane leapt and transformed into giant owls** through a twinned *[[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Polymorph|Polymorph]]* spell, intending to catch companions as they fell. Several catches failed before the remaining party members were brought to safety; Arthur's hair was briefly caught by an attempted rescue, providing the group with an especially memorable indignity. By the conclusion of the sequence, **everyone reached the ground alive**, close to the [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Other/Aceso Aid|Aceso Aid]] caravan. The three towers were no longer standing separately: their substance had joined into **one large Tower of Icarus**, complete with new ground-level doors.

### ✧ The Road Toward the Forge

With the hemisphere in hand, the party discussed the broader mission from [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Queen Isabel|Queen Isabel]]. The other named destination was the [[Worlds/Astodan/Places/Luciferian Caldera|Luciferian Caldera]], but Diane proposed traveling first toward the [[Worlds/Astodan/Places/Dwarven Forge|Dwarven Forge]], where [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Drax|Drax]] had said he could be found. The group accepted the western route as a potential refuge and staging point. Diane told her companions about the powerful rose-masked woman who had confronted her in captivity, emphasizing that the woman's identity and political role remained unverified. The party worried that restoring or visibly altering the towers could attract her notice.

They traveled beyond the Gilded Lands' border and found a large cave suitable for shelter. Weary cast *[[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Mordenkainen's Private Sanctum|Mordenkainen's Private Sanctum]]* to protect the camp. The spell triggered a **neutral wild-magic surge** whose immediate effect was not visible; its significance became apparent later in the night. The party arranged watches while others rested.

### ✧ Falatir and the Fox

During the first watch, a talking **[[Worlds/Astodan/People/The Fox|Fox]]** approached [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Falatir|Falatir]] from outside the protected camp and set down an iron token he recognized. The Fox spoke to him as a familiar ally or sponsor, referred to **two shards already obtained**, and implied that more were needed for Falatir's search. The Fox warned that difficult choices lay ahead and that he could not supply every answer or be present for every danger. Falatir remained determined to find **her** again. The Fox returned toward the Gilded Lands. This private exchange corroborates the Fox's involvement with Falatir without establishing his deeper identity or what all the shards do.

During a later watch, [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Thornwick|Thornwick]] noticed an imprint of a similar token near the cave entrance. He recognized it as familiar from his past but could not place it. The impression brought him a sense of **sorrow**. Thornwick also spoke privately with [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Arthur Morgan|Arthur]], who admitted that the appearance he presented to others was a **disguise**, adopted because of the hostility he had faced from people who feared or mistreated those who were different. The conversation did not disclose Arthur's concealed appearance, and this confidence was not addressed to the whole party.

### ✧ Diane's Memory and Weary's Visitor

On a later watch, **Diane** experienced an overwhelming vision of the burning of her childhood village. She saw the destruction of her home and the death of her mother, and recalled her father carrying her away from the carnage. The event was presented through a deeply frightening sensory vision; Diane trembled and collapsed before returning to her senses. **Weary** stayed with her and offered comfort. The vision is part of Diane's own past, not proof that a new attack had occurred outside the cave that night.

Soon afterward, **Weary** experienced a separate impossible scene: the wagon and surrounding world gave way to a future-seeming domestic setting where he and an older Diane had apparently lived together for decades. In this scene, his skin was its original purple rather than its familiar altered coloration. The apparent Diane spoke in an unfamiliar way; Weary questioned the illusion. The visitor dropped the disguise and introduced herself as **[[Worlds/Astodan/People/Lunatir|Lunitari]]**, a goddess associated with **magic, illusions, knowledge, and trickery**. She said she had heard Weary's magical call and intended the scene as a playful introduction, but Weary objected strongly to her borrowing his wife's face. The scene ended, leaving him awake near the wagon and Diane still asleep.

Out-of-character discussion clarified that Weary's **neutral wild-magic surge during the Private Sanctum** had resulted in the influence of a **neutral patron**. The deity's larger intentions were not disclosed. Weary did **not** tell the whole party about the encounter at this checkpoint. On waking, Diane prayed to **Apollo** and felt reassured after the nightmare. The company then continued traveling, passing into the settled, craft-oriented lands of the Dwarven Forge, where the session ended.

**Possessions, developments and conditions:** A heavy, mysterious hemisphere taken from Gideon is held by the party and hidden behind a magic-aura spell; the Tower site now consists of one stable spire. The party survives its escape, levels are discussed at the table but no permanent numerical level is written into a character sheet without confirmation. The Fox's token is **seen**; whether anyone picked it up is not established by the transcript. Diane's nightmare and Weary's meeting with Lunitari are private experiences, not newly disclosed communal knowledge.
