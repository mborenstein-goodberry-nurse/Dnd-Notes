## Log

### ✧ Separated Below the Estate

After the capture at [[Ingrail's Estate]], the prisoners were brought down a long spiral staircase to cells below the building. [[Diane]] was separated from [[Weary]] and held in a sparse room containing little more than a sink and bucket. She had been restrained but still possessed her [[Amulet of the Devout|+2 Amulet of the Devout]]. Weary was likewise confined and wore a suppressing collar; the original notes say he still had his [[Astral Shard]].

### ✧ The Guards' Visits

A large, rough-spoken guard whom the notes identify as **Joey** visited Diane. He berated her for being married to a tiefling and warned that she and her companions would be forced to fight for their lives within several days. He was accompanied by a subordinate called **Billy**. Diane noticed that Billy's eyes and clothing looked familiar, resembling [[Arthur Morgan|Arthur]]. Billy gave her a guarded warning, but the journal does not establish that the guard was truly Arthur or explain any possible disguise.

Joey and Billy also visited Weary. Their exchange with him was more openly hostile. After Joey moved on, Billy lingered and secretly slipped Weary a **lockpick**. His clothing again seemed to match Arthur's. Whether Billy was a friendly infiltrator, someone in disguise, or merely appeared familiar remained unsettled.

**Items and conditions:** Weary received a lockpick while restrained; Diane retained her [[Amulet of the Devout|amulet]]; the notes indicate Weary retained an [[Astral Shard]]. The prisoners remained captives at the close of the interlude.

## ✧ Questions & Mysteries

- [[Who Is Billy|Is Billy actually Arthur in disguise, and what is he trying to accomplish?]]
- Can the prisoners use the lockpick to escape before the announced arena fight?
- Where have the other companions been taken?
