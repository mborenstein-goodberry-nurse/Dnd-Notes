## Log

### ✧ A Session Diane Missed

The company had agreed to aid the [[Worlds/Astodan/Organizations/Mechanical Wonders|Mechanical Wonders]] in the [[Worlds/Astodan/Places/Dwarven Forge|Dwarven Forge]] after learning that [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Races/Duergar|duergar]] raids had disrupted their mines and stolen essential equipment. In exchange, the guild's founders had offered assistance investigating [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Mysteries/The Hemisphere of Gideon|Gideon's hemisphere]].

**This session took place, but Diane was not present.** The original Session 8 journal was titled *Missed Session* and contains no eyewitness narrative. Its party roster names Arthur, Falatir, and Thornwick. No matched Session 8 recording has been identified.

### ✧ What the Following Journal Establishes

The opening of [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Session Journal/Falatir's Sacrifice|Session 9]] looks back on the group's expedition. It reports that **mining equipment belonging to the Mechanical Wonders had been recovered**. The surviving summary does not say precisely where it was found, how it was recovered, what opposition the party overcame, or whether the equipment had already been delivered to the guild. It should be treated as **recovered guild property**, not newly acquired party treasure.

That same opening refers to the group **realizing that a coin was nearby**. The notes say that companions other than [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Falatir|Falatir]] produced their coins and that suspicion fell on him. They do not identify the coin's origin, owner, or properties. Crucially, the notes do not clearly separate the final moments of Session 8 from events resuming at the start of Session 9. The coin presentation and the subsequent confrontation therefore **cannot be securely assigned to Session 8**.

The recap also mentions someone stumbling and a larger-than-expected encounter with duergar. By Session 9, the party was facing a force that the journal loosely describes as **about fifty duergar**. This is a retrospective estimate, not a verified combatant count. **The defeat of the duergar is recorded in Session 9**, not here.

No other conversations, combat actions, spells, purchases, or individual accomplishments can be reconstructed reliably from the available evidence.

- **What was the coin sensed near the group?** Who held it, and why did suspicion fall on Falatir? Do not assume it was [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Mysteries/The Fox's Token|the Fox's token]] or the hag-associated object mentioned in Session 7.
- **What caused the duergar confrontation?** The next journal mentions a stumble and a large force, but not a reliable sequence of events or an independently confirmed count.
