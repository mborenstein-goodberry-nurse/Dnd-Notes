## Log

### Hero's Bane and the vanishing of Grayson

The party remained near the [[Worlds/Astodan/Places/Luciferian Caldera|Luciferian Caldera]] after their encounter with [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Cinderfang|Cinderfang]]. Attention turned back to [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Other/Havoc's Sword|Hero's Bane]], the sentient sword [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Weary|Weary]] had reclaimed from the mound of flesh. During an exchange witnessed by the companions, [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Grayson|Grayson]] picked up the blade. After a brief conversation, **Grayson vanished into black smoke**.

A humanoid figure appeared before the party, clad in full armor that was pristine on one side and grotesquely demonic on the other. One arm had become a large claw. The figure identified himself as **[[Worlds/Astodan/People/Havoc|Havoc]], Archdemon of Hostility**—the entity [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Weary|Weary]] had spoken with privately during the previous session. Havoc spoke of observing the coming confrontation with the **[[Worlds/Astodan/People/The Sovereign|Sovereign]]** and indicated that surviving it might earn the company some form of reward or blessing. He then departed.

**[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Weary|Weary]] recovered the missing part of his soul.** The original notes state plainly that he was free and had his soul back; the following session's recording corroborates that his soul fragment was returned. Neither surviving source preserves the complete terms or mechanics of the exchange with [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Grayson|Grayson]]. **Grayson's disappearance is certain; his permanent death or location is not.** By the following session companions would speak of him as dead, reflecting their understanding rather than a verified final fate.

### Separate paths toward the obelisk

Following the exchange, the company divided. [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Diane|Diane]], [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Weary|Weary]], and [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Jonks|Jonks]] remained behind as other companions moved to investigate a prominent obelisk near the Caldera. The obelisk was seen **sending lightning downward**. From a distance the waiting group witnessed the great structure **crack and collapse** and heard sounds suggesting a confrontation nearby.

[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Thornwick|Thornwick]] was seen enormously enlarged and flying around the Caldera. The surviving recap does not explain what magic or events caused this transformation, nor does it document the complete attack against the obelisk. The investigating companions did succeed in bringing the structure down.

### A figure of white light

During the investigation, the companions found an **unidentified, feminine-shaped being formed of bright white light** associated with the obelisk. The figure had no discernible facial features. Her voice reminded some of a deceased acquaintance, possibly **[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Noisy Owlbear|Owlbear]]**, but this was an uncertain resemblance—not an identification. The luminous form produced a **painful radiant effect on contact**, yet the group brought her away from the destroyed structure.

The party had not yet established who or what she was, what had happened to her, or why she had been held in the obelisk. The deeper examination of her magic, memory, and spiritual nature is captured in the **March 28 recording** and is reserved for the following session.

### A response from the Caldera

The destruction of the obelisk drew the attention of **demons and other fiendish forces**. Some reportedly spotted the investigating party and began approaching or charging. The company knew that an army or substantial gathering of fiends was nearby, although the precise numbers and kinds of creatures were not established in the contemporaneous Session 23 record.

[[Worlds/Astodan/People/Cinderfang|Cinderfang]], freed in the preceding session, had been seen moving **into the Caldera**. He had not been seen returning. His stated desire to attack the [[Worlds/Astodan/People/The Sovereign|Sovereign]] remained relevant, but the group had no confirmation of what he was doing inside.

The surviving retrospective recap reaches the end of these events with the companions **separated and heading toward one another**. Their actual reunion and the extended conversation with the luminous figure begin in the March 28 recording, rather than being conclusively documented as completed during Session 23.

- What exactly did [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Grayson|Grayson]] exchange, and where did he go when he vanished? The party regarded him as dead, but the evidence does not establish his ultimate fate.
- What does [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Havoc|Havoc]] intend now that he can appear before the party? What did he mean by possibly rewarding survivors of the [[Worlds/Astodan/People/The Sovereign|Sovereign]]'s battle?
- Who is the **luminous woman**, and why was she associated with the obelisk? Why did her voice sound familiar?
- What was the obelisk doing before it was destroyed, and what will its destruction change?
- How many fiends have gathered around the Caldera, and will they pursue the party?
- Where did **[[Worlds/Astodan/People/Cinderfang|Cinderfang]]** go after entering the Caldera?
