## Log

### ✧ The Queen's Assignment

[[Diane]] and [[Weary]] arrived at [[Isabel's Castle]] following their unsuccessful search for Weary's missing family. The assembled adventurers met with [[Queen Isabel]] of the [[Griffin Empire]]. The queen described the advance of [[The Wastes|the Wastes]] and a spreading undead corruption. To aid efforts to reclaim the affected lands, she charged the party with investigating two sites thought to contain powerful magic or artifacts and recovering anything that might help:

- The [[Towers of Icarus]], located in the [[Gilded Lands]] beyond the Whirlpool. [[Arthur Morgan|Arthur]] recalled from his reading that the towers were said to have been built by celestials, stretched toward the heavens, and were dangerous and poorly understood.
- The [[Luciferian Caldera]] in the territory of the Foo Fighters, described as a pit opening toward one of the layers of Hell.

The initial party included Diane, Weary, Arthur, [[Thornwick]], and [[Falatir]]. Falatir was searching for a pale, white-haired humanoid woman important to him. The nature of her disappearance or death was not established by the Session 1 notes. A shadowy figure seemed familiar to Falatir; its identity was not confirmed.

### ✧ Across the Border

The party traveled through the Griffin Empire and passed [[Sparrow]], near the frontier. [[Arthur Morgan|Arthur]] said Sparrow was his hometown and that he lived there with an elderly couple he considered family. The precise nature of their relationship was not recorded. The party saw a settlement or buildings left in ruins following fighting. They continued toward the Towers of Icarus and crossed into the Gilded Lands.

There, a small bandit group calling itself the **Brutus Squad**, led by someone called Brutus, confronted the travelers. The attackers had a chained [[Basilisk|basilisk]]. During the fight the party killed some bandits, others fled, and they killed the basilisk. The group found or received a letter associated with the attack and signed **Pleasure**, a name tied to one of the Gilded Lands' rulers. The party did not yet know why this particular group had been sent after them or how the bandits acquired the basilisk.

### ✧ A Night in the Ruins

After the encounter, the party rested among abandoned, damaged buildings near the border. [[Weary]] used *[[Mordenkainen's Private Sanctum]]* while they sheltered. When the next session began, the group had completed a long rest and was preparing to continue farther into the Gilded Lands.

**Encounters and consequences:** Brutus Squad defeated or dispersed; [[Basilisk|basilisk]] killed; at least one attacker escaped. No verified new treasure or permanent condition was recorded for this session.
