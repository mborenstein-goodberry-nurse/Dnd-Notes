## Log

### Preparations at the last stable tunnel

The company gathered outside the mountain tunnel that [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Warrick|Warrick]] and [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Narci|Narci]] had identified as a possible approach to the [[Worlds/Astodan/Places/Luciferian Caldera|Luciferian Caldera]]. The party had heard that others had gone through the tunnel and never returned. It was described as the last stable passage of its kind. A new traveler joined them: [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Linus Lightfoot|Linus Lightfoot]], an aasimar bard who had accompanied Warrick. Linus had been causing trouble among the orcs of [[Worlds/Astodan/Places/Foo Fighters|Foo Fighter territory]], and was sent along with the expedition instead of remaining there. The group made its introductions before entering the mountain.

Before setting off, [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Grayson|Grayson]] investigated the deck purchased from the goblin merchant [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Zoff|Zoff]]. When he finally drew a card, the supposed [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Treasure/Deck of Many Things|Deck of Many Things]] proved to be an ordinary, crudely illustrated imitation. It carried **no detectable magic**, and the draw triggered none of the feared effects. Grayson was furious at the deception. The purchase of the deck and magical axe for **75 gp together** had occurred during the previous session; no second purchase took place here.

The cart was too large to enter the narrow tunnel. Warrick agreed to **stay behind with the caravan**, while [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Weary|Weary]] undertook the burden of the recovered **hemisphere** rather than leave it with the cart. The group arranged a marching order, keeping its less protected companions near the middle. [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Jonks|Jonks]] sent his undead scout, known as **JJ / Jonks Junior**, ahead.

### The dark passage and the fiendish chamber

The tunnel was cramped and **pitch-black**, with little room to maneuver. The scout nearly walked onto a concealed **pit trap**, but Arthur spotted it and called a halt. The party examined and disarmed or safely bypassed the danger before continuing. Light and darkvision helped them navigate, though stealth was uneven.

Farther inside, the tunnel opened into a much larger room. Chains covered the surrounding stone. Two crouching shapes watched from one side while the sound of dragging metal approached. When the travelers illuminated the room, they recognized a blood-red, bandage-wrapped **chain devil** wielding lengths of chain, accompanied by **two tanarukks**, or demon-corrupted orcs. The creatures prepared to attack, and the party entered combat.

The group survived the confrontation. The account preserved in the accompanying handwritten notes credits **Arthur with bringing down the chain devil and the other enemies**. Those notes also describe **a tunnel collapse at a gate associated with the Infernal Plane**, after which the travelers emerged into a volcanic area of the Caldera. The precise sequence and nature of the apparent planar boundary were not established by the surviving dialogue or understood by the party at that time.

### A climb toward the volcanic heart

After the fight, Linus offered healing to an injured companion. The group struggled up broad, steep steps built on an immense scale. Weary, carrying the heavy hemisphere, lost his footing on the ascent; **Thornwick helped him get back up**. Above them was an opening large enough for a giant, leading toward the interior of a volcano. The rock was hot but not immediately burning them.

Several party members described an increasingly strong **sense that they ought to continue toward the volcanic heart**. Their magic suggested wisps of **enchantment** in the air, although the feeling to those affected seemed natural, like certainty rather than an obvious command. Not everyone experienced it the same way. The party discussed the risk but pressed on.

Stonework and imagery along the route changed into increasingly infernal scenes. The carvings depicted the **Blood War**, the more recent raising of the [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Mysteries/The Necrotic Spires|necrotic spires]], and a colossal figure occupying an infernal throne. The group did **not identify** the enthroned being. Diane examined the hemisphere and found it **apparently dormant**, as it had been during the journey, with no new reaction to the carvings. Its covering continued to show deterioration.

### The sword that Weary feared

The descending passage opened into a great volcanic crucible. At its center lay an enormous **mound of mangled humanoid and fiendish bodies**, with an elegant-looking **longsword** embedded in the mass. When [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Weary|Weary]] studied it, he recognized the weapon connected to the scar and lost piece of his soul: **[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Other/Havoc's Sword|Havoc's Sword]]**. He immediately urged everyone to stay back.

The warning came too late to prevent an argument. A number of companions felt an extraordinary **need to possess the sword**, especially [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Diane|Diane]] and the body shared by [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Arthur Morgan|Arthur]] and [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Other/Damien Castellanos|Damien]]. [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Grayson|Grayson]] was also drawn toward the weapon. Weary recognized the danger, saying that the pull echoed what he already knew of the sword, and pleaded with the others to resist. Some were less aware of any pull, and Jonks became disoriented about why the group was there at all. The sword was still some distance across the chamber.

### Fear, restraint, and the dash toward the mound

Diane began walking toward the blade, and Weary cast **[[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Hold Person|Hold Person]]** to stop her. As the argument escalated, Arthur used a frightening divine display, referred to at the table as **Dreadful Aspect**, which left several companions fearful of him. His **eyes appeared to glow red**. That observation did not, by itself, establish whether Arthur, Damien, or another influence controlled the shared body.

[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Thornwick|Thornwick]] enlarged himself and moved to physically block Arthur's advance, calling for the group to stop. [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Jonks|Jonks]] opened a [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Rope Trick|Rope Trick]] refuge, and [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Amorpha|Amorpha]] joined him to get clear of the conflict. Linus attempted to intervene with a verbal insult through [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Vicious Mockery|Vicious Mockery]], without visibly stopping the situation.

Trying to keep the sword out of reach, Weary attempted **[[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Vortex Warp|Vortex Warp]]** on Arthur, followed by a quickened **[[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Confusion|Confusion]]**. The first attempt did not achieve the intended separation; the subsequent effect is not clearly resolved in the preserved exchange. Meanwhile, Grayson used **[[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Thunder Step|Thunder Step]]** to surge toward the mound, and the burst of thunder injured nearby party members.

As Grayson climbed the heap of bodies, **hands from within the mound seized him**. He took slashing damage and was grappled before he could reach the sword. Still in the creature's grasp, he consumed a concoction called **Potion Mix #5**, obtaining a temporary alteration to his attributes whose exact bookkeeping and duration were debated at the table. The confrontation, and the contest over the sword, remained unresolved as the session's surviving account ended.

> [!info]- Later DM clarification — Potion Mix #5 and Caldera (OOC)
> Wiley described Mix #5 as “some beholder piss,” but **could not provide its actual effect** and thought the party had **not used it yet**. This conflicts with the session's surviving written note that Grayson consumed it; preserve both sources without deciding the usage. Wiley also described weakly anchored land in this region allowing **a layer of Hell to temporarily merge with the Material Plane**. Whether such merges occur naturally elsewhere was beyond the party's knowledge.

## ✧ Questions & Mysteries

- Why did the apparently stable tunnel contain a fiendish garrison, and what was the **infernal-looking gateway** encountered beyond it? What accounted for the infernal influence in the [[Worlds/Astodan/Places/Luciferian Caldera|Caldera]]?
- Who was the **enthroned infernal figure** shown in the volcanic carvings, and why were the [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Mysteries/The Necrotic Spires|necrotic spires]] depicted alongside the Blood War?
- Why had [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Other/Havoc's Sword|Havoc's Sword]] appeared in a mound of corpses, and **who or what** was drawing people toward it?
- What is causing [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Arthur Morgan|Arthur]]'s red-eyed behavior, and which of the consciousnesses sharing his body is acting at this moment?
- Can [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Grayson|Grayson]] escape the **animating corpse mound**, and can the party keep the sword from claiming another victim?
- What precisely does **Potion Mix #5** do, and how long do its altered attributes last?
