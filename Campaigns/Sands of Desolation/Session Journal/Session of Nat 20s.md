## Log

### Into the ravens' path

Free of the Wastes after their harrowing escape, the company continued into [[Worlds/Astodan/Places/Foo Fighters|Foo Fighter territory]]. A storm-like mass of vultures and ravens traveled in the same direction the party needed to go. Their path brought them to a goblin-and-orc settlement that had suffered a dreadful attack. Its outer buildings were burned, although parts of the settlement appeared less damaged within. Bodies lay throughout the town, and blood filled the fountain. The barn yielded two corpses but no obvious additional clue.

According to the surviving accounts, only a **child** and a **goblin chief** survived. **Kenneth Stormjaw** was identified as chieftain of the **Outskirts clan**. The party also learned that the broader Foo Fighter territory was home to approximately **20–30 clans**.

### Traces of an unknown killer

The party considered whether the destruction was the work of one assailant or several. A vision or comparable revelation showed an apparently human figure covered in blood, with a scarred face and red eyes; the same figure was described as having red hair and favoring his fists in combat. Jonks suspected the attacker might be the master he had fled, but no identification was confirmed.

The notes briefly record **Arthur counterspelling another character's spell**, without explaining the spell or reason. Diane shared information concerning fire, the details of which are lost. The **Iron Shadows** came up as a group behaving like secret police, though they were **not formally identified as secret police**; their organization and involvement in the killings were not established. One note raises the possibility they had killed a potion maker, but leaves it as a question.

### The Outskirts clan

The party's dealings with the chieftain included an unlikely run of natural 20s. Zach's notes portray the chieftain as treating someone with extravagant, almost religious regard and joking that a cult was beginning. The moment was mostly a joke, not an established founding of an organization. The recipient of this attention might have been Jonks or Grayson, but remains unknown.

The company also found time for drinks and games. Jonks drank; Arthur and Grayson reportedly received potions, and others played cards. The recorded potion types and how they were obtained are unknown. Jonks was also given a corpse with the intention of reanimating it as an undead servant. The notes do not establish whether he completed the reanimation during this session.

### Moving north

The travelers departed northward as a group of orcs also moved north from the ruined village. At some stage **Silence** was cast upon the party, but Weary dispelled it. Zach's account suggests the group feared that something was watching them and that something troubling had occurred, without specifying a source for that suspicion.

The journal closes on an unresolved encounter: **[[Worlds/Astodan/People/The Fox|the Fox]] gave Jonks a coin**. Neither its significance nor the full context of this exchange is recorded.

> [!info]- Later clarification of the massacre (OOC provenance)
> Wiley recalls that Soren confessed to Diane, Grayson, Thornwick and Weary concerning the **orc village** attack. The player confirmed Soren as the confessor. We lack a timestamp for the confession and cannot safely place it in the investigation scene or assume every companion heard it.

> [!info]- Additional DM retrospective clarification (OOC)
> Wiley recalled that the body used to animate [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Jonks Jr|Jonks Jr.]] was **a demon**. Its exact kind and whether reanimation completed during Session 17 remain unverified.

## ✧ Questions & Mysteries

- **Attacker identified retrospectively:** Soren confessed to the orc village massacre, according to Wiley and the player. The precise moment of confession, full attack sequence and motive remain unknown.
- Was the blood-covered, red-haired figure actually connected to Jonks's former master?
- Who or what are the **Iron Shadows**, and did they have any part in the killings?
- Why did **the Fox** give **Jonks** a coin, and what significance does it have?
- Who cast **Silence** on the party, and what prompted the suspicion that they were being watched?
