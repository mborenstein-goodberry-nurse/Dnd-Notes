## Log

### ✧ After a Troubled Night

The party stirred in the cave where they had sheltered after escaping the [[Worlds/Astodan/Places/Tower of Icarus|Tower of Icarus]]. The night's dreams and visions had left several companions shaken. [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Thornwick|Thornwick]] woke screaming, warning [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Arthur Morgan|Arthur]] that **"she is coming."** He described a hag seizing and squeezing him in his dream, and spoke anxiously about needing to become stronger before she reached him.

[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Diane|Diane]] emerged pale, frightened, and unusually silent. [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Weary|Weary]] stayed close, guiding and reassuring her. She was not ready to discuss what had happened to her. The group resolved to continue toward the Dwarven Forge, hoping to find [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Drax|Drax]], whom Diane had met earlier, and obtain help with the relic recovered from [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Gideon|Gideon]].

### ✧ The Hemisphere and the Pillow

Before setting out, the party examined [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Mysteries/The Hemisphere of Gideon|Gideon's hemisphere]] more closely. The object was a substantial clockwork half-dome, its components shifting as though searching for something. **Purple light glowed inside it.** Along its flat surface were numerous tiny, leglike attachments that seemed designed to grip another surface or object. Its shape suggested that **another half might be missing**.

The hemisphere was too large to slip discreetly into a coat pocket. To learn how its underside behaved, a companion placed a pillow against it inside the [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Other/Aceso Aid|Aceso Aid]]. The little attachments gripped and worked into the fabric; removing the pillow would tear it. A rock placed against the opposite, rounded side simply fell away. For the moment, they left the pillow attached as a makeshift barrier and set the object on a shelf in the caravan. The group was careful not to allow the gripping side to touch anyone's chest.

Weary confirmed that he had already cast *[[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Nystul's Magic Aura|Nystul's Magic Aura]]* on the object during the preceding evening, intending to mask its magical nature from certain forms of detection. He kept track of when he would need to renew the protection.

### ✧ Toward Anvil's Flame

Diane produced the insignia Drax had given her: a **dragon pierced through the heart by a sword**. It was their best lead for identifying his guild. Thornwick also showed the others a mysterious symbol on his hand, associated in his mind with the hag from his vision. The group did not recognize it at once.

Traveling farther into the [[Worlds/Astodan/Places/Dwarven Forge|Dwarven Forge]], the party passed towns, merchants, craftsmen, and wagons carrying goods. The relatively peaceful, trade-focused surroundings came as a relief after their earlier ordeals. Nonetheless, the sight of numerous dragonborn and dragonkin among the traders intensified Diane's fear. She stayed close to Weary, who quietly helped her back into the caravan when she became overwhelmed.

Thornwick tried to speak with Diane about his own misfortunes. He said a hag had destroyed his Fey kingdom, banished him to the Material Plane, and fused a skull to his face. Diane was too shaken to respond at length. As the companions discussed their troubles, **Falatir's search for "shards" was mentioned aloud**, and [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Falatir|Falatir]] made clear that the loss of his love continued to weigh on him. This did not establish the woman's full identity, fate, or the purpose of the shards.

The travelers found banners matching Drax's crest near a roadside information kiosk. There, a dwarf named **Gornwick** directed them to the **[[Worlds/Astodan/Organizations/Mechanical Wonders|Mechanical Wonders]]**, the prestigious guild associated with Drax. He explained that *Founder* was an honorific given to particularly renowned craftsmen, and that the founders traveled frequently. He supplied directions to **3105 Acerwood Drive** (the address preserved in the original journal; the automated audio transcript is less clear).

### ✧ A Glove and an Unsettling Token

During a brief stop among the shops, Thornwick visited a doll or clothing seller with Arthur and obtained a small pair of **black leather gloves**. The exchange was lighthearted despite Thornwick's size and the unusual request.

On the way back to the caravan, Thornwick asked Arthur to examine something associated with his hand or mark for signs of magic. Arthur's examination led to a discussion of a **small iron, coinlike object described as a hag's tooth or token**, which could apparently allow its holder to converse with the hag. The exact physical form, ownership, and mechanics are difficult to recover from the recording. Thornwick tried mentally calling out to **Baba Eris Whitewood**. No spoken reply was heard, but he experienced a feeling of sorrow. The conversation did not establish whether this token was connected to the iron token seen near the cave in the preceding session.

### ✧ At the Guild Gates

After several more hours of travel through connected caverns, the company reached a grand district of marble roads, pillars, guild banners, and richly outfitted guards. A large iron gate led into the Mechanical Wonders' establishment. [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Falatir|Falatir]] watched for possible followers and did not spot anyone trailing the group.

Weary initially asked the gate attendants directly for Drax, unaware that this was considered an impolite way to approach a Founder. He apologized and restated the party's business: they had a **potentially important magical object** requiring specialized examination. A gate attendant attempted *[[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Identify|Identify]]* on the hemisphere; **Adrian**, a bronze dragonborn posted near the entrance, was later named as the guild contact to seek when the founders were unavailable. Weary removed the masking effect of *[[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Nystul's Magic Aura|Nystul's Magic Aura]]*. The hemisphere flared with such intense magical power that the attendant stopped casting, unable to determine its purpose. The guild admitted the party, examined the caravan for dangerous contents, and escorted them through workshops, forges, and displays of elaborate arms and armor.

### ✧ Drax, Thrax, and Gideon's Relic

The party reunited with **[[Worlds/Astodan/People/Drax|Drax]]**, a red dragonborn craftsman, who remembered his brief encounter with Diane at [[Worlds/Astodan/Places/Ingrail's Estate|Ingrail's Estate]]. He was introduced to Weary, her husband, and the other travelers. His brother **[[Worlds/Astodan/People/Thrax|Thrax]]**, a dark-scaled dragonborn with a scar and unusually colored eyes, also emerged from the workshop. Both brothers held the title of Founder. Drax demonstrated an improved *[[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Treasure/Sun Blade|Sun Blade]]* and handed it to Thrax to test; it remained **the brothers' equipment**, not a party acquisition.

Weary explained that the company had been sent by [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Queen Isabel|Queen Isabel]] of the Griffin Empire to seek help against the expanding Wastes. Their mission had led them to the Towers of Icarus and would eventually take them to the [[Worlds/Astodan/Places/Luciferian Caldera|Luciferian Caldera]]. At the tower, they had encountered Gideon and taken the hemisphere from his chest. Thornwick enthusiastically confirmed that **he** had pulled it free. Falatir introduced his own purpose more briefly: he was looking for a **lost love**.

Drax cautiously examined the object. Removing the pillow revealed shredded fabric around the grasping underside. He described the hemisphere as extremely old and seemingly dating to the **Equalizer War**, but could not confidently identify its function. He fitted a **metal plate** over the dangerous surface, providing a much safer way to carry it. A fuller examination would require specialized equipment not currently available to the guild.

### ✧ Duergar Raids and Baba Eris Whitewood

After consulting one another, the brothers explained the guild's predicament. The Dwarven Forge was under sustained pressure from **[[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Races/Duergar|duergar]]** raiders operating from the **Grey Mountains**. Mines and shipments had been attacked, research and specialized tools stolen, and relations with surrounding powers put under strain. The raiders carried an unfamiliar banner, and the brothers suspected that an unidentified outside entity was working with them.

When the brothers showed the party the banner, **Thornwick recognized its symbol as the mark seared into his own flesh**. He exposed the mark and identified his enemy as **[[Worlds/Astodan/People/Baba Eris Whitewood|Baba Eris Whitewood]]**, a hag from the Fey realm. He explained that she had ruined his kingdom, driven him from his home, and fused the skull to his face. The symbol strongly suggested a connection between his persecutor and the current attacks, though the group did not yet know the full arrangement between the hag and the duergar.

The brothers said they lacked the means to maintain a prolonged defense. They **offered to help investigate the hemisphere** if the adventurers would recover stolen equipment and help drive back the raiders. Confronting Baba Eris Whitewood might benefit both sides. They also mentioned a **powerful battleaxe stolen from one of the Dwarven Forge's armories**. If the party recovered it, they were welcome to keep it as spoils. No axe was recovered during this meeting.

The guild offered lodging for the night, opportunities to explore the district, and discounted crafting services. Drax and Thrax were willing to assist, but the hemisphere's deeper purpose remained unknown.

### ✧ Discretion and Equipment Upgrades

Once the meeting ended, the party argued over how openly information about their mission and the relic had been shared. Some feared that even trusted guild members might be overheard or infiltrated, making the hemisphere a target. Others pointed to the Forge's alliance with Queen Isabel and to the founders' apparent willingness to help. Drax **returned the hemisphere** to the party, now protected by its fitted metal plate; it was **not left in the guild's custody**.

Weary then approached Drax about improving his and Diane's equipment. The negotiated discounted prices were:

- **100 gp:** commission a **+1** enhancement (see [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Treasure/Armor, +1, +2, or +3|magic armor]]) for Diane's [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Equipment/Armor|scale mail]].
- **50 gp:** improve Weary's **bloodwell vial** by **+1**.
- **150 gp:** add one use of *[[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Hex|Hex]]* to Weary's vial, usable **once per long rest**, without requiring the proposed sorcery-point-based version. An upgrade to that more powerful version was discussed as a future possibility, but **not purchased**.

These commissions totaled **300 gp**. Diane and Weary pooled their money, and another companion provided the **10 gp** needed to reach the price. The party paid upfront and handed Drax the scale mail and vial, which he took toward an enchanting area. The recording does **not** show the enhancements completed or the items returned.

Thornwick, concerned about the possibility of facing Baba Eris Whitewood, separately asked about finding or commissioning a stronger weapon. The surviving recording ends during that discussion; no particular weapon or payment is confirmed.

## ✧ Questions & Mysteries

- [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Mysteries/The Hemisphere of Gideon|What is Gideon's hemisphere?]] What is its missing half, and what does it do? Can the Mechanical Wonders examine it once their equipment is recovered?
- **Who commands the duergar raids?** How directly is [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Baba Eris Whitewood|Baba Eris Whitewood]] involved, and why is Thornwick's mark displayed on their banner?
- **What is the hag-associated tooth/coinlike token?** Can it truly be used to speak with Baba Eris, and is it related to—or distinct from—[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Mysteries/The Fox's Token|the Fox's iron token]]?
- [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Mysteries/Falatir's Search|Why is Falatir seeking shards and a lost love?]] The party has heard these terms, but the full explanation remains unknown.
- **What happened to the stolen battleaxe and the guild's other equipment?** Could the party recover them while pushing back the duergar?
- **What will become of the commissioned enhancements?** The equipment was handed over, but its completion and return were not witnessed before the recording ended.
