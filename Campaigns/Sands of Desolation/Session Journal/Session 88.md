## Log
The door starts to close on us. [[Diane]] and [[Veldrin]] get stuck in the door 
**We decide on an official ruling: you cannot guide yourself** 
This entire area of hallway is spotless. We now realize that there is one very faint Will-O-Wisp. There is a route to the left, middle, and right 
Most of us tell the will o wisps our names. Grayson lies, and Phoebe lies 
**First room: 3 paths, we went left** 
The Will-O-Wisp follows us 
We spot a secret door on the right side 
**Another House Rule: Whoever casts [[guidance]] rolls the d4** 
Some of us enter the room and begin to get digested by a secretive [[Gelatinous Cube]] 
> [!summary | red] Roll Initiative!
> 1. [[Veldrin]] 
> 2. [[Phoebe]] 
> 3. [[Weary]] 
> 4. [[Diane]] 
> 5. [[Grayson]] 
> 6. Gelationous Cube 

[[Veldrin]] managed to blink out of it 
[[Phoebe]] also made it out of the cube 
[[Weary]] makes it out of the Cube 
[[Grayson]] is able to close the door 
**Another House Rule: Uses of healing give Diane Blessed Healer healing** 
The Medium sized whisp heals as Diane heals the party. Definitely not a whisp 
We find a large clean room. [[Diane]] and [[Weary]] check it. No traps, no cubes (2nd 34 perception in a row, go [[Diane]]!) 
Diane mentioned the healing to Grayson. He doesn't remember [[Xerac]] experimenting on the whisps. They also weren't called whisps, they were called anyone and everyone 
We get to a room with 4 tombs, labelled Heart, Mind, Body, Soul 
Anyone approaches us, they were expecting us 
What if this is an amalgamation room? 
Remember the body grafting tradition of the [[Gilded Lands]]...
We continue on. We approach a cross junction 
**At the cross junction, we go left** 
There is a large blast door, fully metal, like we saw before with the 2 holes 
Flavor time: [[Grayson]] keeps his bag of holding in his skull 
There is a sign! **Organ Storage/Collection** 
Anyone and Everyone function as watchers. They see everything 
Everything has a place 
They have pretty high health. triple digits perhaps... very unnatural for a will o whisp 
We crowbar it open. There is stuff in the room, we can't quite see into it 
Oh, the room is a chasm room with 2 crystals. The crystals are out of reach, but don't react to Weary throwing a shoe at it 
The door is magical, and very hard to open
