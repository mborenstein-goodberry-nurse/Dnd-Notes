## Log

### ✧ The Battle That Should Not Have Happened

The companions were still contending with the aftermath of the expedition to recover stolen mining equipment for the [[Worlds/Astodan/Organizations/Mechanical Wonders|Mechanical Wonders]]. The previous session had taken place without [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Diane|Diane]]; by the time she returned, the group was confronted with a much larger [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Races/Duergar|duergar]] force than expected. The old journal blames an unnamed companion's stumble for drawing them into a battle the DM had not intended them to fight at that scale.

The original notes describe the opposition as **fifty duergar**, apparently an incredulous estimate rather than a counted roster. Despite the unexpected fight, **the company defeated the duergar**. The journal records the outcome, but no reliable account survives of the terrain, individual combat turns, spells used, casualties, or exact victory conditions.

The mining equipment had reportedly been recovered. Whether the guild's property was already delivered, still held by the party, or awaiting transport is not stated. It should not be counted as personal treasure.

### ✧ The Coin and the Growing Suspicions

Attention then returned to a **mysterious coin** thought to be nearby. The original journal says everyone other than [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Falatir|Falatir]] showed their coins and that nothing incriminating was found among those displayed. Suspicion fell increasingly upon Falatir.

According to the journal, Falatir was concealing a coin that was associated with **a curse**. The notes also mention *[[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Vortex Warp|Vortex Warp]]* moving "it" away; they do not clearly identify the caster, the target, or precisely how the movement related to the coin. The party was also reportedly unable to compel truthful answers from Falatir through *[[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Zone of Truth|Zone of Truth]]*. The journal calls him **immune**, but records no reliable mechanical explanation for that result.

Whatever Falatir knew about the coin, the exchange further damaged the group's trust in him. This record does **not** establish that the coin was the token previously associated with [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Mysteries/The Fox's Token|the Fox]], the hag-related object discussed in Session 7, or the same object as any other earlier coin.

### ✧ Thornwick, Falatir, and the Wedding Ring

The dispute escalated until [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Thornwick|Thornwick]] and Falatir were nearly fighting. During the confrontation, the party cut off or forcibly removed **Falatir's wedding ring**. The original wording is unclear whether his ring finger was also severed, so that injury cannot be asserted confidently from the surviving note.

The ring was identified as a *[[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Treasure/Ring of Mind Shielding|Ring of Mind Shielding]]*. That revelation was significant in light of the attempts to question Falatir, but the journal does not establish precisely which of the ring's properties had been used, nor whether the ring caused the reported *Zone of Truth* outcome. Its final holder is not recorded; **the ring should not be added to another character's inventory on this evidence alone**.

The wedding ring also raised an obvious question about Falatir's past. He had spoken openly of searching for shards and a lost love during [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Session Journal/Cave Divers be Like|Session 7]], but the surviving Session 9 scene notes do not specify what he told the companions about that relationship.

### ✧ Grayson's Patron

Amid the same period of revelations, the journal identifies [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Xerac|Xerac]] as [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Grayson|Grayson Underwood]]'s patron. The original writer considered the disclosure unsurprising. The note does not preserve who revealed the information, how the party verified it, or any resulting bargain or confrontation. The original journal spells the name **"Zarach"**; the player has confirmed that this refers to **Xerac**.

### ✧ Falatir's Sacrifice

The confrontation culminated in a drastic choice by Falatir. The original journal describes him, in the writer's characteristically dark humor, as having **"committed sudoku via demon minotaurs."** This records an apparently intentional self-sacrificial act involving demonic minotaurs, but not its exact mechanics, the words exchanged, or what became of Falatir physically or spiritually.

The outcome was more complicated than simply declaring him dead. **Falatir was gone from the party after the event.** A large force of minotaurs remained, and the journal remarks on the absurdity of the company now having a *minotaur army*. It does not explain whether they were summoned, temporarily allied, under someone's command, or merely present. The company subsequently **took a long rest in the presence of that force**.

[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Isabelle|Isabelle]] is named in the original Session 9 summary and listed among its people. Those headings connect her to the sacrifice, but the scene notes do not preserve her introduction or confirm how much the group learned about her and Falatir during this session. The exact exchange and relationship must therefore remain outside this contemporaneous account until corroborated.

### ✧ Reporting to Queen Isabel

The group sent an update to [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Queen Isabel|Queen Isabel]], the queen who had commissioned the search for the relics. The original journal preserves the outgoing report:

> Mission Update: Towers of Icarus investigated. Reduced to one tower. Hemispherical artifact found, corrupting influence. Suppressing duergar for information therein from Mechanical Wonder Founders. Over.

The **25-word** phrasing and the journal's heading "Sending to Queen Isabel" strongly suggest use of *[[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Sending|Sending]]*, though the original does not explicitly identify the caster. The message accurately conveyed what the party could report at this stage: the former three towers were now a single [[Worlds/Astodan/Places/Tower of Icarus|Tower of Icarus]], a strange [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Mysteries/The Hemisphere of Gideon|hemispherical artifact]] had been recovered, and the group was involved with the duergar problem in hopes of gaining assistance from the Mechanical Wonders.

There is **no recorded reply** from Queen Isabel. The original Towers relic retrieval quest was already completed when the hemisphere was obtained in Session 5; this communication does not mark a second completion or establish that the object's purpose had become known.

## ✧ Questions & Mysteries

- **What was the hidden coin?** What curse, if any, was bound to it, and why did Falatir conceal it? Is it connected to any of the earlier tokens, or wholly separate?
- **What happened to Falatir?** His departure and sacrifice are witnessed, but the means and final state are not sufficiently recorded to call him simply *Dead*.
- **Who is Isabelle, and what did the party actually learn about her here?** The original summary names her, but the preserved event log does not reconstruct her introduction or the nature of Falatir's exchange. See [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Mysteries/Falatir's Search|Falatir's Search]].
- **Who or what are the demonic minotaurs?** Why were they involved, and what was their relationship to the party after Falatir's departure?
- **What does Grayson's link to [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Xerac|Xerac]] mean?** How did the party learn of his patron, and what does that allegiance entail?
- **What became of the ring and guild equipment?** The [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Treasure/Ring of Mind Shielding|ring]] was revealed but no transfer is documented. The [[Worlds/Astodan/Organizations/Mechanical Wonders|guild's]] mining equipment was recovered but not confirmed returned. What about the paid-for equipment enhancements commissioned in Session 7?
- **Can the Mechanical Wonders decipher the hemisphere, and did the queen receive the report?** Neither the relic's purpose nor Queen Isabel's reply is recorded here.
