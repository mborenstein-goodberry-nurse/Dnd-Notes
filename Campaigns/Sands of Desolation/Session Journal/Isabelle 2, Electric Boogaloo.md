## Log

### ✧ Isabelle Takes Falatir's Place

After [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Falatir|Falatir]]'s sacrifice and departure, [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Isabelle|Isabelle]] joined the traveling company. The old journal identifies her as **Falatir's wife**. It does not preserve the words of their reunion, the nature of the exchange, or what she and the companions were told about the soul-shard search.

The company learned that Isabelle could summon or command animal companions and possessed wildfire-related druidic abilities, including a primordial or wildfire companion. The original notes use several druid descriptions without resolving the exact character build. Her summoned beasts seemed distinctly uneasy around [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Thornwick|Thornwick]]; not all of her companions were animals, and the source does not explain the cause of their reactions.

A remark in the contemporary journal raises the possibility that Isabelle **was or had been a queen of [[Worlds/Astodan/Places/Sirvanna|Sirvanna]]**. It was recorded as a startled question, not a verified political history. This should not be conflated with [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Queen Isabel|Queen Isabel]], the ruler who commissioned the group's original expedition.

### ✧ Thornwick's Past Comes Into Focus

The journal's opening recap mentions that [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Arthur Morgan|Arthur]] and Thornwick had defeated a powerful opponent, that Thornwick now possessed an **axe**, and that he had learned he had a **son**. These appear under “Where we left off”; the player has assigned **the axe acquisition and the discovery of his son to Session 9**. They are retained here only as a recap, not newly occurring Session 10 events.

[[Worlds/Astodan/People/Baba Eris Whitewood|Baba Eris Whitewood]] was said to have Thornwick's son in her care and to be raising him against his father. She derided Thornwick as a bad role model. Thornwick's full memories of the child and his lost kingdom had not yet been restored: the opening of Session 11 would revisit those memories through a recovered signet ring.

Another brief entry states that [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Krieg|Krieg]] had become an imp and was described as a sidekick. The player recalls this as the in-world explanation for Alex no longer controlling the same Krieg, previously recorded as a tiefling PC. The exact permanence and mechanics of the transformation remain uncertain; his historical PC identity is preserved and his status is handled in the character note.

### ✧ The Rush Into Battle

Thornwick pushed ahead, and the company followed into a confrontation involving Baba Eris and a formidable beast. The journal refers to its mouth and calls it a **“gronulator”**; the following session's DM recap sounds like **“mouth of Grumulator.”** During Batch 4 review, the player identified the creature as a **[[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Bestiary/Mouth of Grolantor|Mouth of Grolantor]]**. Whether the DM used its standard statistics or modified the encounter remains unverified.

The original notes record **AC 17** for the hag and several observations of Isabelle's creatures reacting poorly to Thornwick. They do not preserve a reliable initiative order, turn-by-turn tactics, specific finishing blows, or the full effect of the hag's magic.

### ✧ The Hag Falls

The result is not in doubt. At the beginning of [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Session Journal/Amorphous Dreams|Session 11]], the DM explicitly recapped that the party had **defeated Baba Eris Whitewood**. The company stood in an altar area, badly beaten and bruised, amid the dead hag, the dead monstrous giant, and other fallen enemies. Some companions were searching the surrounding ruins, while others remained at the altar.

That recap corroborates the outcome of Session 10; it does **not** mean that the later actions of searching the corpse, handling the mysterious coin, and recovering Thornwick's family signet ring happened in this session. Those occur during the live Session 11 recording and belong in its journal.

## ✧ Questions & Mysteries

- Where was Thornwick's son, and how much of Baba Eris's account of his upbringing was true?
- What was the nature of Thornwick's axe, and why was it so significant?
- Was Isabelle truly a former queen of Sirvanna? What happened between her death, Falatir's search, and her return?
- Why did Isabelle's animal companions react so strongly to Thornwick?
- What precisely happened to Krieg, and did the “imp/sidekick” remark refer to the existing Krieg?
