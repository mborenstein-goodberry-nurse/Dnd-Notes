## Log

### Rejoining the others

The company was still within the [[Worlds/Astodan/Places/Luciferian Caldera|Luciferian Caldera]], near the rim of a crater where the previous expedition had shattered a great obelisk. [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Diane|Diane]], [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Weary|Weary]], and [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Jonks|Jonks]] caught up with [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Thornwick|Thornwick]], [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Isabelle|Isabelle]], and the shared body of [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Arthur Morgan|Arthur]] and [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Other/Damien Castellanos|Damien]]. The party learned how the other group had destroyed the lightning-wreathed structure and rescued an unidentified feminine form composed of blinding white light. Witnesses thought the figure sounded like the deceased [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Noisy Owlbear|Noisy Owlbear]], but no one could establish that she actually was Owlbear. Touching her had caused intensely **radiant** pain, and she had no visible facial features.

The collapse of the obelisk had stirred the fiends around the crater. The investigating party reported an army of demons or other fiends searching for those who brought it down. [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Jonks|Jonks]], Diane, and Weary also knew that **[[Worlds/Astodan/People/Cinderfang|Cinderfang]] had entered the Caldera** after his release and had not reappeared. When the companions checked the immediate surroundings, they saw no army directly on top of them, but the danger remained nearby. The party was still attempting to reach the [[Worlds/Astodan/People/The Sovereign|Sovereign]] and recover the [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Quest Board/Find the Hidden Relic in the Caldera (2 of 2)|Caldera relic]].

Wiley called for religion checks from [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Diane|Diane]], [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Weary|Weary]], and [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Jonks|Jonks]]. Jonks gained a point of exhaustion; Diane rolled particularly well. The opening discussion also confirmed that [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Weary|Weary]] possessed his full soul again, although visible red coloration had not simply vanished with its restoration. The meaning of the better religion result was not fully explained in the preserved dialogue.

### The woman of light

[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Diane|Diane]] sat beside the stranger to ask who she was. The woman could not remember her name or recognize the surrounding wasteland. She remembered leaving, chaos and screaming, meeting somebody, and **being split in half**. She could recall helping people, and later said that she had a husband. She had apparently been held in the obelisk for an uncertain length of time, but could not establish how long. She wanted to find out who she was.

[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Jonks|Jonks]] tried to identify the aura surrounding her. His initial inspection revealed ancient magic with a **fiendish component** and traces of **abjuration**. Upon examining her more closely, he detected necromancy at work in an unusual way: rather than raising a dead body, the magic appeared to be preserving or sustaining something. He learned that the figure was **not merely a person of light but a whole soul manifesting in a visible form**. He shared this finding with the group, while admitting he did not know what it ultimately meant. [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Diane|Diane]] could sense residual fiendish magic but could not infer the woman's alignment or motives from her featureless appearance.

The party considered whether the stranger might have been separated from a physical body, and whether one could be found or made for her. [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Diane|Diane]] considered [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Speak with Dead|Speak with Dead]], but the DM ruled that the spell could not target her because she was not a corpse. These were possibilities raised by the companions, not established solutions. The mysterious woman showed no clear recognition of [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Noisy Owlbear|Noisy Owlbear]] or anyone else.

[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Jonks|Jonks]] offered **"[[Worlds/Astodan/People/Luz|Luz]]"** as a temporary name, saying it meant *life* in Elvish. The woman did not recover her actual name. She agreed to accompany the companions if it might help her learn who she was, despite the danger ahead.

### What the ruined obelisk did

[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Jonks|Jonks]] recalled material from his former necromancer master and made a history check about the collapsed obelisk. He concluded that it had worked somewhat like a **lightning rod in reverse**, drawing beings from infernal or abyssal realms toward the material world rather than discharging lightning normally. This explained its role in bringing or attracting fiends, but did not establish that the luminous soul herself was a demon. The source and purpose of the obelisk, and why the soul had been held within it, remained unresolved.

### Jonks summons a familiar stranger

With the group uncertain whether [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Luz|Luz]] was safe, [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Jonks|Jonks]] attempted an experiment: if he summoned a demon, perhaps it or [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Luz|Luz]] would react as though they recognized each other. He cast [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Summon Greater Demon|Summon Greater Demon]] at fourth level, bringing forth a **tannaruk/tanarukk** wielding a large blade. [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Luz|Luz]] regarded it without recognition; the demon shielded its eyes from her brightness and seemed confused. The test did not reveal her identity.

[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Jonks|Jonks]] discovered he could not dismiss the demon as casually as he had imagined. The party debated concentration, the possibility of losing control, and the danger of attracting the nearby fiend army. Jonks sent it away, but it traveled toward the crater rim and soon moved beyond easy speaking distance.

Suddenly, [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Jonks|Jonks]] believed the demon was **[[Worlds/Astodan/People/Jonks Jr|Jonks Jr.]]**, the undead orc companion previously lost when a cavern collapsed into an infernal opening. Determined to recover him, Jonks asked to be launched toward the crater rim. A magically conjured [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Bigby's Hand|Bigby's Hand]] threw him with remarkable accuracy to the top of the wall, where he spotted the demon a short distance away. He called after it, and the creature turned, welcomed him, and carried him back. Their reunion was affectionate rather than hostile. [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Jonks Jr|Jonks Jr.]] remained completely loyal to his former companion, despite his apparent transformation into a demonic tanarukk.

Wiley then **confirmed outright** that this summoned tanarukk really was [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Jonks Jr|Jonks Jr.]], who had ended up in an infernal realm after the cavern collapsed. [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Jonks|Jonks]] learned the name **JJ** as a possible true-name handle, but the DM deferred exactly what that would mean for future summons. The party did not establish that Jonks could summon him at will without conditions. His loyalty was clear; the precise nature of his transformation was not.

### A diversion before the fiends arrive

As Jonks returned, movement and distant howls indicated that fiends were investigating the disturbance. [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Diane|Diane]] estimated that they might have roughly **five minutes** before the searchers reached the party, too little for the longer protective ritual she had considered: [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Forbiddance|Forbiddance]]. The group weighed hiding, running, and fighting.

[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Jonks|Jonks]] asked his newly recovered companion for another favor. He embraced JJ, apologized for his earlier loss, and sent him back up the crater wall to draw the patrols away. Still loyal to Jonks, JJ willingly carried out the diversion. He spoke to the fiends in Abyssal, and their movement suggested that he successfully led at least some of them away. How long the diversion would last, and whether Jonks could safely dismiss and later resummon him, remained unsettled.

The party had obtained a **short rest** amid its preparations, although fatigue was still discussed. The luminous woman continued to accompany them, and everyone returned to the problem of getting past the crater wall and down toward the [[Worlds/Astodan/People/The Sovereign|Sovereign]].

### Scouting the Caldera

The party considered several routes: flying or transforming into large birds, jumping with a last-second magic intervention, or finding a real walkway. [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Other/Damien Castellanos|Damien]] volunteered to scout the crater rim. He climbed up, concealed himself, and looked down into the crater within the Caldera. He spotted fiends patrolling the ring, including large horse-like creatures identified in the garbled audio with a name resembling **armanites**, as well as other fiend legions whose specific name is uncertain. The tanarukk recognized as [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Jonks Jr|Jonks Jr.]] could still be seen traveling among some of the fiends.

[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Other/Damien Castellanos|Damien]] also located a **structure near the destroyed obelisk**, apparently attached to a staircase or path descending into the crater. A larger structure remained visible deeper down. He saw no obvious replacement lookout tower or confirmed active searchlight, though this did not mean the path was safe. He conveyed what he had seen back to the others through a communication stone, and the company favored the physical path rather than a hazardous leap.

### Concealing [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Luz|Luz]] and moving out

Before approaching, the companions tried to conceal their conspicuously bright new acquaintance. A dark, hooded cloak provided by [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Weary|Weary]] failed: her radiance continued to shine through it. Weary then used his tiefling heritage to cast [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Invisibility|Invisibility]] on [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Luz|Luz]]. The spell **made her body invisible**, but a faint glow remained on the ground around her position, creating a residual risk of detection.

The group used a rope to climb to the rim of the crater, struggling with exhaustion and attempts to remain hidden. From the top they planned to creep along the wall toward the structure and its descending path, keeping close to the rock in the hope of reducing their visibility. The second March 28 recording ends **during the stealthy approach**, before confirming that they reached the structure or staircase, escaped the crater, or confronted its defenders. **The party remained within the Luciferian Caldera** at the session boundary.

## ✧ Questions & Mysteries

- Who is the woman provisionally called **[[Worlds/Astodan/People/Luz|Luz]]**? What happened when she was separated or "split in half," and who is her husband?
- Why was an entire manifested soul imprisoned in the obelisk, and what is the exact nature of the fiendish, abjuration, and preserving necromantic magic around her?
- Why did she sound like [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Noisy Owlbear|Noisy Owlbear]] to some companions, and was that resemblance meaningful?
- What were the obelisk's creators and intended purpose, beyond its apparent ability to draw fiends into the material world?
- What ultimately happened to **[[Worlds/Astodan/People/Jonks Jr|Jonks Jr.]]** after he loyally led the fiend patrol away? How did he become a tanarukk, and could [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Jonks|Jonks]] summon the same individual again under the DM's deferred ruling?
- Could the party safely conceal [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Luz|Luz]]'s residual glow and traverse the crater rim without being discovered?
- What lay at the lower end of the staircase near the ruined obelisk, and how could the party reach the [[Worlds/Astodan/People/The Sovereign|Sovereign]]'s stronghold?
