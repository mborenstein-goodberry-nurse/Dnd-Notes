## Log

### ✧ Beyond the First Idol

When the recorded account resumes, the party has **overcome the first [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Bestiary/Radiant Idol|Radiant Idol]]**. One companion attempted to collect a feather from its damaged wing but could not remove it. Although the immediate opponent was gone, an unnatural force still prevented the party from descending the entry stairs and leaving. Magical inspection suggested that the barrier was being maintained from **elsewhere**, rather than by the now-defeated idol. The adventurers concluded they might have to confront whatever controlled the towers rather than retreat.

Crossing an ornate bridge, they entered the **middle tower**, which contained another empty-looking pedestal under an invisibility field and an inscription recounting a celestial's fall from grace and punishment. A search disclosed that it, too, concealed a wing-clipped **[[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Bestiary/Radiant Idol|Radiant Idol]]**, this one presenting as female and exerting an intense enchantment or fascination upon an observer. The group resisted the urge to repeat the prior encounter and instead considered how to proceed. Diane sensed an overwhelming, frightening presence toward the central tower and was initially unable to face it. A companion's performance helped calm her enough to continue.

### ✧ The Watcher on the Ceiling

While searching the middle chamber, **Diane alone spotted a small golden eye** high above the party. Others repeatedly failed to see it despite her efforts to point it out. The eye moved along the ceiling, maintaining its distance as the group headed toward the bridge into the tallest tower. It appeared to slip into a concealed hatch near the central chamber. Diane checked the previous room but could no longer find it. What the eye was, who controlled it, and why Diane could perceive it remained unresolved. The players' joking speculation about being watched was not proof of its identity.

The party reached a heavy doorway at the far end of the bridge. After some difficulty attempting to force it open, a knock from *[[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Mage Hand|Mage Hand]]* caused the door to swing inward. Several companions moved toward the opening. The final part of **B08** reaches the doorway and the start of initiative, but does **not** contain the full confrontation that followed.

### ✧ Gideon the Judge

The original Session 5 journal and Google Docs account supply the scene that the available audio does not fully capture. Beyond the doorway, the adventurers confronted a massive, once-radiant figure seated on an ivory throne in an atmosphere of necrotic corruption and dread. His body was described as pale with purplish necrotic energy, and a **hemisphere was embedded in his chest**. He identified himself as **[[Worlds/Astodan/People/Gideon|Gideon the Judge]]** and pronounced the intruders condemned to die. He appeared to believe the party had come to steal the object embedded in him, though the adventurers themselves were not yet certain what they were expected to recover.

Combat began. The old journal records Gideon's formidable defenses, including an Armor Class of **22** and magic resistance, but the surviving audio does not independently verify the full initiative order, tactics, losses, or the final moments. **In the final stretch of the fight, [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Thornwick|Thornwick]] succeeded on a Strength check and physically tore the hemisphere from Gideon's chest.** This outcome comes from the original written Session 6 account and the player's direct retrospective confirmation; the check itself is not preserved on audio. By the next available recording, B09, Gideon is gone and the hemisphere is in the party's possession. Other missing combat details remain unverified.

**Consequences:** The party passed the second idol, confronted Gideon and recovered the hemisphere through Thornwick's successful Strength check. The ensuing aftermath and changes to the towers are documented in [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Session Journal/A Second Session has Hit the Towers|Session 6]]. Gideon's exact fate at the moment of extraction is not established by this missing recording.
