## Log

### ✧ The Pirate King's Private Audience

The company remains in [[Worlds/Astodan/Places/New Phyrexia|New Phyrexia]] after a chaotic night of Wild Magic. [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Weary|Weary]] is still **small and wet**, and his temporary **60-foot [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Dictionary/Truesight|Truesight]]** has not yet expired. He speaks privately with [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Graves Hellborne|Graves Hellborne]], the Pirate King, about what he has seen and about the dangers beyond the city.

Graves shows considerable familiarity with magic and immediately recognizes **a curse affecting Weary**. The surviving notes do not specify how Graves identifies or might remedy it. **Wiley subsequently identified this as Grayson's curse**, and the player explained that it reverses the face value of a rolled die for 24 hours before modifiers. Weary's Truesight also allows him to continue seeing the concealed **"backpack" or apparatus** that he noticed during [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Session Journal/The Session of Surge|Session 15]]. Weary recognizes the machinery as **a form of life support** through his Truesight, although **Graves never confirms this aloud**. Why Graves needs it, what condition it sustains, and how it works remain unknown.

### ✧ What Graves Says About the Wastes

The conversation turns to the [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Mysteries/The Necrotic Spires|necrotic spires]] and the dangers accompanying them. According to Graves, the **seas have become rougher** since the spires appeared. **Bands of undead** periodically emerge, most often at night, and **golems comb the desert collecting bodies**. He considers areas farther from the spires safer for travel, though this is his assessment rather than a guarantee.

Graves attributes the raising of the spires to **a warlock**, claiming that the magic required **a living soul uncorrupted by the dead**. He says the spell **cannot now be reversed**. He also reports that a great number of undead rose when the spires appeared, and that forces of the **Dominion** and [[Worlds/Astodan/Places/Sirvanna|Sirvanna]] intervened to put them down.

These are important leads, but the account is presented as **Graves's testimony** rather than independent proof. **Weary already knows the warlock was [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Warrick|Warrick]]**, because he personally witnessed Warrick casting the ritual. Weary deliberately keeps that firsthand knowledge from Graves. The account does not establish **whose uncorrupted living soul** was used, what the golems are doing with the bodies, or how much Graves himself knows about Warrick.

> [!note]- OOC reconstruction provenance and unresolved shorthand
> - **Primary source:** Original `A Nice Chat with the Pirate King.md` journal, Session 15.5. No matched recording or Zach photo is identified for this interlude. The journal is shorthand, not a transcript of Graves's exact speech.
> - **Private knowledge:** Weary is the only documented participant in this mini-session. Do not automatically treat these revelations as common party knowledge.
> - **Life-support identification (player clarification):** The player says the apparatus was very clearly a life-support system, but Graves **never confirmed it verbally**. Session 15's earlier recording showed machinery, tubes and an illusion; this interlude supplies Weary's more specific observation. The device's mechanism and reason for use are still unconfirmed.
> - **Later DM/player clarification:** Wiley identified this as **Grayson's curse**; the player explains that for 24 hours the curse reverses the **natural die face** prior to applying modifiers (e.g. on a d20, 17 -> 4). This is a confirmed homebrew mechanic, not proof of exactly when it was cast, which specific checks it affected, or whether Graves removed it. The contemporary "curses don't really last" shorthand should not override the clarified 24-hour duration.
> - **Unclear origin line:** After `Spell can’t be reversed now`, the original writes `Gained from trusting the dead`. The subject and meaning are not secure, so this is not turned into new world lore.
> - **Attributed history and private knowledge:** Graves's claims concerning the uncorrupted soul, inability to reverse the spell and Dominion/Sirvanna intervention are not independent proof. **Weary personally witnessed Warrick cast the ritual** and intentionally withheld the warlock's identity from Graves. Whether Graves knew whom he was describing remains open; the soul's identity is unknown.
> - **Assigned reconstruction date (player-approved):** The original journal was dated `04-21-2026`, inconsistent with Session 15 (`01-10-2025`) and Session 16 (`01-24-2025`). The player recalls the interlude happening within days after Session 15 and requested the following Sunday. `date: 01-12-2025` is therefore an assigned/provisional chronology date, **not a verified session timestamp**.
> - **Continuity:** The opening of Recording A04 starts Session 16 after a long rest, with the temporary Wild Magic surges gone. Do not place that rest before or within the interlude.

## ✧ Questions & Mysteries

- Why does **Graves need the life-support apparatus**, what condition does it sustain, might it have other functions, and what does he know about Weary's curse?
- **Whose uncorrupted living soul** was used in the ritual [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Warrick|Warrick]] cast, and what became of that person? How much did Graves know about Warrick?
- Why are **golems collecting bodies** in the desert, and who controls them?
- Grayson's **24-hour die-reversal curse** explains the luck shorthand; the exact time of its application and the separate reference to **"trusting the dead"** remain unknown.
