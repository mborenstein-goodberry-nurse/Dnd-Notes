## Log

### ✧ A Second Look at Thornwick's Son

Several days had passed since the disturbing encounter with the unnamed dark visitor on the mountain road. The company was preparing to leave the [[Worlds/Astodan/Places/Dwarven Forge|Dwarven Forge]] behind and enter [[Worlds/Astodan/Places/The Wastes|the Wastes]], with roughly half a day's travel remaining. [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Thornwick|Thornwick]] recounted the threat to his missing son. The companions briefly distracted themselves by trying, with [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Mage Hand|Mage Hand]] and physical demonstrations, to teach a little carrot creature named **Sprout** to backflip. Sprout failed to grasp the lesson.

Before setting out, [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Diane|Diane]] attempted [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Scrying|Scrying]] again on Thornwick's son, following the failed attempt during [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Session Journal/We Meet the Fox|Session 12]]. **This time the spell connected.** Diane alone could see and hear through the sensor, although she later told the others what she saw.

She discovered a **young fairy** with vibrant wings, apparently in his **early twenties**, wearing carefully made plate armor marked by runes and carvings. He was sharpening a blade with considerable focus. An **intelligent undead figure** entered: white-boned, with wings resembling an angel's and a great blade. The two spoke calmly in **Undercommon**, which Diane could not understand. From their manner, the young fairy seemed to be a **squire or aspiring knight**, possibly on a paladin-like path. The undead appeared to regard him favorably, without obvious hostility or intent to harm him. None of these impressions established the undead's name, title, moral alignment, or the exact terms of the young man's service.

The room seemed part of **a castle adapted to undead inhabitants**. It had little natural light or ventilation and one prominent wall light. The young fairy appeared accustomed to the dark and **well fed**, rather than neglected or imprisoned under visible duress. Diane could not identify the castle or tell where it was located.

Before the observation could continue, the winged undead **turned directly toward the invisible scrying sensor**, reached out, and **smashed it**, breaking Diane's connection. This suggested that the being could detect the spell's observer, but did not reveal how.

Diane reported that Thornwick's son was **alive** and apparently doing well physically. Thornwick showed relief, then anger and grief at the thought of his child growing up apart from him and developing loyalties to undead. Diane cautioned that a reunion might not be welcomed as readily as Thornwick hoped. He asked [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Grayson|Grayson]] if the description of a white-boned, winged undead noble or master sounded familiar. Grayson said he knew nothing of such a figure. **Neither the son's location nor the nature of his relationship with the undead was resolved.**

### ✧ The First Sands and a Problem with Wheels

After descending from the mountain passes, the party finally encountered the sands of the Wastes. Although the ground was unpleasantly coarse and heavy, the air and sand did not feel as oppressively hot as the group had expected. The caravan was another matter: **its wheels sank and dragged through the sand**, threatening to reduce travel to a crawl.

Off to the **northwest**, a large **necrotic spire** loomed over the landscape. The group had been able to see its dark uppermost tip from farther away, but now felt waves of unnatural energy radiating from it. The spires were relatively recent, having appeared about a year and a half earlier according to the conversation. Diane and Weary remembered being present when they manifested, but the recording explicitly indicates that **this detail was not shared openly with the party in that moment**. The characters had not traveled so close to a spire before.

Grayson, familiar with some travel in the Wastes, suggested that a vehicle would move far better with **treads** than with ordinary wheels. After the group worked through how such a contraption might be constructed, [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Weary|Weary]] cast [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Creation|Creation]] to conjure **leather treads**. His magic manifested through moving shadows, without a confirmed Wild Magic surge. The others lifted the caravan and struggled to fit the treads to both sides. Once secured, they enabled the cart to travel at **normal speed over the sand**. The recording later shows them wearing down; their longevity should not be assumed.

The party's protective supplies for transporting the dangerous **[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Mysteries/The Hemisphere of Gideon|hemisphere]]** were also counted: **two of the three protective sheets** supplied earlier remained. The precise reason the third was expended is not established by these clips.

Despite curiosity about the nearby spire, the group elected to make for **[[Worlds/Astodan/Places/New Phyrexia|New Phyrexia]]**, their intended destination, instead of diverting directly to the spire.

### ✧ An Unanswered Presence

While on the journey, Thornwick tried to address the mysterious presence associated with his **skull mask**, asking for information about his son. The DM described Thornwick privately sensing that presence **both in his mask and in a coin Grayson carried**. The presence appeared to register the question, but offered **no answer**. Grayson and the rest of the party were **not told about this connection** at the time. Whether the coin or mask connected to other mysterious objects remained unanswered.

> [!note]- ✧ Knowledge boundary — the visitor's identity
> The DM uses the name **[[Worlds/Astodan/People/The Fox|the Fox]]** during this exchange. The player recalls that **Thornwick likely knew the Fox's name in-character by this point**, and the transcript is consistent with him addressing the presence by name. This is a likely, not definitively verified, knowledge boundary **for Thornwick only**; do not give that understanding to the rest of the party. Grayson has not been shown to know about the mask/coin connection. The objects may be related to earlier coin/token mysteries, but should **not** be declared identical without further evidence.

### ✧ Near the Necrotic Spire

The party continued into increasingly warped terrain. The sands around the spire appeared **disturbed and unnaturally blackened** as necrotic energy spread through them. Weary checked his Wild Magic and felt that, closer to the spires, the odds were turning toward **neutral or harmful outcomes**. This was a warning about the local magical atmosphere, not a confirmed Wild Magic surge.

Diane was able to cast [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Goodberry|Goodberry]], but found the effort of drawing on **higher-level divine magic** unusually difficult in the surrounding darkness. The DM described her needing to concentrate on her holy symbol to draw out a spark of power. Diane prayed for guidance and received reassurance that her god's light still reached her despite the corruption. **The scene does not establish a permanent prohibition on higher-level spells.**

The leather treads were already looking worn. [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Diane|Diane]] and [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Weary|Weary]] stopped to use [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Mending|Mending]] on them. The party had not examined the stopping place for danger before stepping down from the caravan.

### ✧ Undead Beneath the Sand

Without warning, **undead erupted from the sands** surrounding the stopped cart, initiating a surprise attack. Among the attackers were:

- **Electrified zombies:** blue-tinted undead with **metallic, almost cybernetic augmentations** protruding from their backs; their blows delivered lightning as well as physical damage.
- **Skeletal bruiser:** an armored skeletal combatant wielding a heavy **warhammer**.
- **Necrotic skeletons:** blackened, nearly charcoal-like skeletons with **greenish glowing eyes and flames**, capable of casting spells.
- **Bone golem:** a huge amalgamation of bones and bodies whose hideous laughter inspired **fear**, and whose crushing strikes carried lightning damage.

The miniature figures used on the table did **not** reflect the creatures' actual forms; some were represented by orc minis, although the opposition was undead. When the electrified zombies attacked, one companion recognized the **same kind of augmentations** as enemies encountered roughly **one or two years earlier**. The augmentations resembled those **Diane and Weary encountered while traveling toward the [[Worlds/Astodan/Places/Resurgence|Resurgence]] in an earlier adventure**. Nothing established who created the present attackers.

The combat began badly for the caravan. The horses **scattered**, though the DM specifically kept them alive and out of the immediate fighting. The attackers overwhelmed the group with physical blows, lightning, and spells. A skeletal bruiser **was killed** in the first exchange. Poison failed to harm one of the undead; radiant damage was **not observed to deal extra damage** in that exchange, although it was not resisted.

The bone golem's dreadful laughter **frightened two characters** whose identities are not reliably discernible from the transcript. It struck Weary hard enough that he described being reduced to roughly **one-third of his health**. Two necrotic skeletons also cast at Diane and Arthur. Weary successfully used [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Counterspell|Counterspell]] to stop the spell targeting **Diane**; the spell targeting **Arthur** went through, inflicting **poison damage** without poisoning him.

**The recording ends as the opening surprise exchange concludes. The undead are still engaged and the party has not yet secured the area.** The next journal, [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Session Journal/Blueberry Weary|Session 14]], begins by describing combat in the Wastes; its outcome and any recovered necrotic bones belong there, not here.
