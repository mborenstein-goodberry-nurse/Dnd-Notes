## Log

### ✧ Getting Weary Back on His Feet

The company's day in [[Worlds/Astodan/Places/New Phyrexia|New Phyrexia]] begins with a practical problem: [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Weary|Weary]] is still inflated into the purple-blue ball produced by last session's Wild Magic surge. Attempts to lubricate him and maneuver him out of the caravan accomplish little until he uses magic to escape. Once on the street, his awkward, rounded body sends him rolling, and companions must stop him before he gets farther away. A sturdy **wheelbarrow** is found and pressed into service. [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Noisy Owlbear|Noisy Owlbear]], newly introduced to the adventuring company, takes an enthusiastic interest in his predicament.

Meanwhile, the temporary caravan treads Weary previously fashioned with [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Creation|Creation]] are deteriorating. Diane and the others decide to seek sturdier treads in the market, while another part of the group pursues the **[[Worlds/Astodan/Organizations/Phantom Blades|Phantom Blades]]** lead from the previous session. The markets lie within territory associated with the **Extortionists**, one of the Pirate King's five guilds. The group's navigation is less straightforward than hoped.

### ✧ A Face from the Blood Games

At a tavern along the way, companions unexpectedly encounter [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Joey|Joey]], the large, bearded overseer they remember from the time they were enslaved by Duke Ingrail. Joey seems pleased to see them, and claims **Graves Hellborne** has sent him to find them. The group recalls their ugly history with him, although he makes efforts to be sociable and offers drinks. The atmosphere is uneasy rather than an immediate fight.

While Joey is distracted, [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Amorpha|Amorpha]] uses sleight of hand to steal the **Brutalizers necklace** he wears. She also notices his bandaged arms and extensive scarring. When she later asks the Pirate King about the necklace, Graves estimates it could be worth **50–100 gold pieces**, depending on the buyer. The theft and necklace are witnessed in the recording; the original notes call the thief *“Alana,”* while the table audio repeatedly uses Alondra/Hayley for Amorpha's player. See the reconstruction review for that attribution.

### ✧ A Very Unusual Rescue

Elsewhere, another magical accident rapidly overtakes the search for treads. Weary triggers a Wild Magic surge that causes **gills to grow on his neck while he loses the ability to breathe normal air**. His companions hurry him to a fountain, then attempt to keep water around his head as he struggles for breath. A companion uses [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Mold Earth|Mold Earth]] to reshape the ground and create an improvised water-holding enclosure. [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Noisy Owlbear|Noisy Owlbear]] also tries to help with a metal helmet and smith's tools, although the attempt introduces considerable risk and confusion. [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Diane|Diane]] casts [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Cure Wounds|Cure Wounds]] to treat his injuries.

Weary eventually decides that enough is enough. He uses [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Dispel Magic|Dispel Magic]] to cancel the inflated body that made him a blueberry, but casting again sets off more Wild Magic. He shrinks to his normal proportions and gains a rapid healing effect. Another surge gives him an intense need to stay close to Owlbear, who helps move him along. He retains the gills for the time being; the exact duration of these overlapping effects is not settled here.

The original journal and Zach's dated notes separately report that a tread merchant tried to overcharge the group. Weary's sudden shrinking frightened the trader into accepting **Owlbear's acorn** as payment. The surviving audio establishes the search and a series of misadventures around it, but not the exact exchange; that account is preserved as contemporary written evidence rather than embellished with invented dialogue.

### ✧ Joey's Escort and the Pirate King's Secrets

Joey escorts the other companions to the **Pirate King's castle**, where they find [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Graves Hellborne|Graves Hellborne]] speaking to [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Grayson|Grayson]]. The two describe their discussion as **business** and intend to continue it later. Grayson suddenly departs by magic with **Creed**, surprising even Graves. The purpose of their arrangement remains unclear. Thornwick, very drunk by this stage, accompanies the group.

The visit also provides a significant answer to an older personal mystery. The DM explicitly tells [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Amorpha|Amorpha]] that she **recognizes Graves Hellborne as the person from her dreams**. She had previously dreamed of a scarred, pirate-like stranger calling himself the King of the Desert. That recognition does not explain why he visited her dreams or what he wants from her, but it establishes a direct connection. Graves's unusual schnauzer is confirmed to be named **Spore**.

Later, a Wild Magic surge gives **Weary [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Dictionary/Truesight|Truesight]] to a range of 60 feet**. He sees through part of the illusion covering Graves's upper body and notices a disturbing collection of **scars, tubes carrying reddish liquid, mechanical fittings, strange vents**, and equipment concealed beneath the King's outward appearance. The illusion also hides unusual armaments, including a dark rapier and an unfamiliar holstered device. Weary cannot tell whether the machinery is life support, augmentation, or something else. Graves notices Weary's reaction, studies him in return, and gives him a subtle acknowledgment. Weary does not draw the rest of the court's attention to what he saw.

### ✧ A Night of Wild Magic

In the court, the party's fascination with Wild Magic overwhelms any orderly audience. Thornwick spends part of the scene **miniature**, bathing in a large teacup. Later a surge imposes an alarming restriction: he must **touch an ally every minute** or fall unconscious, though an ally touching him restores consciousness at **one hit point**. The effect interrupts the conversation repeatedly as friends try to keep him awake.

[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Noisy Owlbear|Owlbear]]'s peyote pouch becomes a catalyst for further surges. Owlbear first **creates a duplicate**. When both versions are exposed to another surge, the duplicate becomes a **small turtle**, while the original gains the ability to manifest a **fire aura** that harms nearby creatures and protects him from fire. Other effects follow, including an apparent loss of familiarity with another person and a brief appearance of **butterflies and flower petals**. With nearly everyone talking over one another, some precise targets and durations remain difficult to establish from the surviving transcription.

Eventually the companions discuss settling down for the night, although **some temporary Wild Magic effects remain active when Session 15 ends**. Weary's 60-foot Truesight, notably, will still be in effect during the Session 15.5 interlude. The remaining temporary effects end when the party completes its long rest before Session 16. The original journal's reference to a “clean slate” is best understood as describing that later transition; it should not move the long rest into this session.

> [!note]- OOC evidence and continuity notes
> - **Personal knowledge:** Amorpha's identification of the dream visitor and Weary's observation of Graves's concealed anatomy are recorded as character-specific experiences, not common knowledge automatically granted to everyone present.
> - **Name correction:** The thief is called “Alana” in both historical note sets. The audio identifies Alondra/Hayley in the scene, the existing Party note identifies Alondra as **Amorpha's** player, and the player recalls Amorpha as the likely thief. Retain the theft under Amorpha, without inventing a separate character named Alana.
> - **The Pirate King's body:** The DM describes tubes, reddish fluid, scarring, and weapons, but neither identifies the source of the modifications nor states that he is undead. Avoid interpreting the imagery as proof of a specific creature type or machine.
> - **Tread merchant:** The acorn payment comes from the two written notes; no exact dialogue or amount has been invented for the missing recording segment.
> - **Surge expiration (player clarification):** Weary's Truesight and at least some other surges persist into Session 15.5. The temporary effects clear after the long rest preceding Session 16; do not move the rest to the end of Session 15 or assume every effect lasted equally long.
> - **Arthur and [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Other/Damien Castellanos|Damien]]:** No change is made to the confidential two-consciousness account learned in Session 12 or the unconfirmed Damien appearance in Session 14. This topic is reserved for the Batch 5 worldbuilding pass.
