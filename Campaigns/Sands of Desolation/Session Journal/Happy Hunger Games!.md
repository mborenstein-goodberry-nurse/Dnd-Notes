## Log

### ✧ Work for the Blood Games

After the failed attempt to resist capture, [[Duke Ingrail]]'s household treated the party as enslaved prisoners. The guards separated the captives into different work details. [[Weary]], [[Thornwick]], the minotaur, and other captives were marched to underground excavations under the supervision of **Joey**, **Jared**, and **Billy**. Diane was assigned to a more presentable group that prepared for and served a gathering of wealthy guests.

The slavers described the excavation as physical conditioning for a forthcoming **gladiatorial contest**. Favor with the overseers could mean equipment or other advantages when the captives were forced to fight. During work underground, a loud bell was followed by falling stone and stalactites that killed several captives. The surviving party witnessed slavers recovering body parts from the dead: they identified the practice as that of **skin grafters and flesh takers**, who acquired body parts for bodily augmentation. Joey made clear that failures and deaths were treated as acceptable parts of the trade.

### ✧ Diane and the Rose-Masked Woman

While serving a reception or masquerade at the estate, Diane attracted attention by performing her assigned duties particularly well. A powerful, elegantly dressed woman in a pale rose-colored **porcelain masquerade mask** beckoned Diane over. The woman discussed Weary's distinctive purple-and-red coloration and the damage to his soul, displaying knowledge of the couple's past. She said her agents had troubled the party on the road and referred to their killing one of her lesser beasts, apparently the basilisk from the Brutus Squad encounter.

The woman took a clear position of authority over Ingrail, who visibly feared contradicting her. When she learned the party was headed for the [[Towers of Icarus]], she offered a changed bargain: if Diane and the others **survived the coming tournament**, they could pass through the Gilded Lands toward the towers without further obstruction from her. She did not promise escort or additional assistance. The woman warned Diane against betrayal, gripped her chin with a metal claw-like fingernail ornament, and cut her. Diane privately called her **Rose**; neither her actual name nor her identity as [[Pleasure]] was established during the recorded exchange.

Afterward, Diane withdrew to collect herself. A **red dragonborn**, [[Drax]], approached and bandaged the wound, applying magical healing. He said he had already helped purchase the freedom of other captives and was seeking to free more, although he lacked the money to free Diane and her companions immediately. He identified himself as **Drax the Founder**, said he could be found in the [[Dwarven Forge]], and left Diane a sigil depicting a **dragon pierced by a sword** before guards arrived looking for him. His larger allegiances were not explained.

### ✧ The Arena at the Hallowed Bastion

The surviving prisoners spent several days at forced labor. Some earned limited favor or regained particular belongings for the contest: the recording specifically mentions Weary's bloodwell vial and Thornwick's belt; it does not establish that the entire party's equipment was returned. The captives were then marched back toward the [[Hallowed Bastion]], where the **18th annual Blood Games** were held in a subterranean arena. Some party members were reunited there; Arthur was not visibly participating with them.

The guards eventually removed the collars, manacles, and suppressors as the contestants entered a magically enclosed arena and were handed a small assortment of weapons. **Susie the Desecrator**, the reigning champion, appeared: an enormous, grotesque creature assembled from dragon flesh with ichor dripping from its body. Ingrail watched with a [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Bestiary/Manticore|manticore]] and [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Bestiary/Chimera|chimera]] at his side. The audio supplied for this batch reaches the start of the confrontation and initiative.

The historical session journal and original Google Docs subsequently record that the party **defeated Susie**. A **hydra** appeared, and a red lich named [[Xerac]] tore it apart, throwing the arena into turmoil. Those latter outcomes are retained as historical notebook evidence, **not** misrepresented as events present in the available Session 3 recordings. His motives and any connection to the [[Hallowed Bastion]] were not established in this session.

**Items and consequences:** Weary's bloodwell vial and Thornwick's belt were specifically restored for the contest; Diane received a dragon-and-sword sigil from Drax and was treated for a cut. The party's freedom and ability to reach the towers were still subject to Rose's conditional offer, not confirmed as secured at the close of the available audio.
