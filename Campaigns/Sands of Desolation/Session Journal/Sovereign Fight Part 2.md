## Log

### Holding the line against the Sovereign

The battle against **[[Worlds/Astodan/People/The Sovereign|the Sovereign]]** continued in his chamber in the [[Worlds/Astodan/Places/Luciferian Caldera|Luciferian Caldera]]. [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Arthur Morgan|Arthur]] summoned another copy of himself and went on the offensive. [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Thornwick|Thornwick]] threw his axe into the contest while [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Diane|Diane]] devoted much of her strength to keeping the wounded companions standing.

[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Weary|Weary]] suffered repeated wounds. Diane used powerful healing spells, including a fifth-level [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Cure Wounds|Cure Wounds]] and [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Mass Healing Word|Mass Healing Word]], to help keep him fighting. Amid the combat, Weary's wild magic erupted more than once, producing strange temporary effects. Zach's notes mention water breathing and unusual possibilities for restoring the fallen, but do not establish those as lasting new abilities. His attempts to strike back included a [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Chaos Bolt|Chaos Bolt]] and poison damage to which the Sovereign was immune.

The Sovereign struck back savagely. Both Diane and Thornwick fell during the struggle, and Weary himself was knocked down more than once. Arthur's direct assault drew legendary retaliation, and the notes record several others taking heavy hits. The fight repeatedly swung between desperate healing and renewed attacks.

### Companions, spirits, and shadows

[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Isabelle|Isabelle]] marshaled spirits in the fight and cast [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Aid|Aid]] on companions including Diane, Weary, and Thornwick. **[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Noisy Owlbear|Noisy Owlbear]]**, who had unexpectedly returned during the first part of the confrontation, fought beside them and summoned a spirit bear to assist with healing. He suffered an apparent decrease in Strength; Zach's notes question whether it was temporary. The manner in which Owlbear came back remains unknown.

Shadowy enemies also fought around the Sovereign. Zach credits Thornwick and a combatant transcribed as **Haley** with destroying shadow creatures. A dark blast from the Sovereign struck Isabelle and Owlbear. The notes then record an odd moment in which Arthur attacked Owlbear; the reason, any compulsion, and the consequences are not preserved, so it should not be taken as a confirmed deliberate betrayal.

### The Sovereign falls

The turning point came when **[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Thornwick|Thornwick]]** rose and, according to Zach's handwritten account, delivered the attack that killed **[[Worlds/Astodan/People/The Sovereign|the Sovereign]]**. The April 18 DM recap confirms the outcome: the Sovereign lay defeated beside one of the arches in the chamber, with a poisonous cloud above the wound Thornwick had opened in his chest. The company was badly injured, but it had prevailed.

The chamber contained **two large yellow spheres**. In the retrospective DM recap, one held the **life-associated hemisphere** of the engine the party had sought; **[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Weary|Weary]]** and **[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Noisy Owlbear|Noisy Owlbear]]** removed it. A hazardous interaction with its flat surface prompted it to be passed to [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Jonks|Jonks]], who was holding it at the opening of the next session. The other sphere held a suspended woman, while another luminous figure was working nearby. Their identities and the spheres' function remain unclear in this session's evidence.

The following session opens immediately in the aftermath of the battle, before the party has fully explored the chamber or learned the consequences of their victory.
