## Log

### An unwelcome homecoming

The party tried to rest after defeating the mound of flesh and reclaiming [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Other/Havoc's Sword|Hero's Bane]]. An incident involving the weapon awakened the sleepers, and their watch schedule continued with more interruptions than anyone wanted. During his watch, [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Jonks|Jonks]] noticed details that were familiar beneath the newer demonic markings. The volcanic crucible was **[[Worlds/Astodan/People/Cinderfang|Cinderfang]]'s lair**, once the home of the dragon who had enslaved him.

[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Jonks|Jonks]] found a crack or indentation in the floor at the place where [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Cinderfang|Cinderfang]] had crushed his wife, **[[Worlds/Astodan/People/Ursula|Ursula]]**. He clung to the spot, recognizing it as all he had left of her there. He woke the others and warned them that they were resting in his old master's home. The walls were marked with imagery and writing associated with demons that Jonks did not remember from his time in the lair. A recent large presence might have passed through, but the group could not verify where [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Cinderfang|Cinderfang]] was or whether he was still using the place.

### The inscriptions and Havoc's proposal

On a later watch, [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Weary|Weary]] found that the recovered sword seemed to make old Abyssal inscriptions easier for him to understand. The carvings repeatedly invoked **the [[Worlds/Astodan/People/The Sovereign|Sovereign]]**, called the **Unifier**, **Great [[Worlds/Astodan/People/The Sovereign|Sovereign]]**, and **Infernal Champion**. They referred to something called the **[[Worlds/Astodan/History/Equalizer War|Equalizer War]]** and suggested a connection to the **two Engines**, though the surviving audio does not securely establish the exact historical wording or relationship.

As [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Weary|Weary]] held the weapon, his attention slipped away from the room. He stood in a vision before an armored figure whose one half was immaculate and whose other half was grotesquely corrupted, with a massive clawed hand. The figure identified himself as **[[Worlds/Astodan/People/Havoc|Havoc]]**, an **Archdemon of Hostility**. Time passed differently in this exchange: what might feel long to them would seem only an instant to the other companions.

Havoc showed [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Weary|Weary]] a glittering blue fragment, shaped like a slice of a circle, and confirmed it was **one-fifth of Weary's soul**. He still held it. Havoc wanted to escape the sword and proposed two possibilities: **four additional fragments, each one-fifth of another person's soul**, or **the death of the [[Worlds/Astodan/People/The Sovereign|Sovereign]]**. He also claimed that **[[Worlds/Astodan/People/Iferit|Iferit]]** and **[[Worlds/Astodan/People/Withengar|Withengar]]** had escaped their respective predicaments and that he was the last one still confined. Havoc complained about having been passed to Weary's brother, and made clear that he was primarily interested in gaining his freedom. He promised Weary an opportunity to recover what he had lost, but no exchange or completed restoration was demonstrated in the recorded vision. Havoc said Weary could summon his attention simply by calling his name.

[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Weary|Weary]] returned to the room, where the others noticed his distracted state. With the group's rest sufficient for them to continue, and [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Jonks|Jonks]] eager to be away from [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Cinderfang|Cinderfang]]'s domain, the company decided to leave rather than remain for another watch. [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Diane|Diane]] and Weary were still described as exhausted during the departure.

### Through the ash wastes

The party descended enormous volcanic steps into a barren, smoke-choked wasteland, with dead trees, scattered ruins, and little sign of living vegetation. Something seemed to watch their movements, but no creature committed to an attack. In the haze they could make out a distant structure in the direction they believed led toward the deeper Caldera.

As they traveled, [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Weary|Weary]] explained the undertaking to companions less familiar with its purpose. The company was pursuing [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Quest Board/Find the Hidden Relic in the Caldera (2 of 2)|the hidden Caldera relic]], the second half of an ancient device: they already carried a **life-draining hemisphere**, and now needed its **life-associated counterpart**. They believed a powerful fiend—the **[[Worlds/Astodan/People/The Sovereign|Sovereign]]**—had long possessed that other half and used it for his own benefit. Discussion turned to how dangerous it might be to confront someone empowered by such an object, and whether theft or clever use of the hemisphere might succeed where a direct battle would not.

[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Weary|Weary]] also publicly named the recovered sword **Hero's Bane**. Tensions over the weapon lingered. Comments about [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Arthur Morgan|Arthur]] and [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Other/Damien Castellanos|Damien]] suggested that an additional presence might have surfaced in their shared body. No in-character introduction or recognition of this third individual is reliably captured. (The player's retrospective identification is preserved in the collapsed provenance, not presented here as party knowledge.)

### The warded gate

The travelers eventually reached a huge black, obsidian-like barrier radiating fiendish magic. Light cast upon it was swallowed by darkness. Contact revealed a magical wall rather than an ordinary opening, and [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Jonks|Jonks]] recalled seeing his second master, a necromancer, use fiendish objects to pass similar wards. The group considered using Hero's Bane as a key, but [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Weary|Weary]] said he could not simply leave the sword behind. Instead, they chose to test the **life-draining hemisphere**.

[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Jonks|Jonks]] exposed the hemisphere and cast or sent it against the barrier. Its effect drew the darkness inward until the gate crumbled and decomposed, clearing the route. The group then tried to protect the dangerous surface again. Jonks and [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Thornwick|Thornwick]] nearly fell into the hemisphere's grasp while handling it, escaping direct contact by a narrow margin. The metal sheet already used against the gate disintegrated; **two spare protective sheets** remained available after the group covered the object again.

### A hill of flesh

Past the barrier, the company could see a strange mound and a separate spiked tower. They chose to investigate the mound first to gain a view of the area. It was deeply unnatural: limbs, pulsing flesh and corrupted shapes protruded through the apparent earth. [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Diane|Diane]], already tired, lagged behind and was briefly caught by a grasping hand, but freed herself. From higher ground, the party could see more of the Caldera and its ruined, flesh-bound structures.

[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Jonks|Jonks]] was the first to recognize what was hidden within the hill. A huge red dragon's head rose beside him: **[[Worlds/Astodan/People/Cinderfang|Cinderfang]]**, his old master. The creature's tail, wings, body and portions of his face were embedded in a mass of demonic flesh beneath the dirt. Jonks reacted by emptying his [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Treasure/Wand of Fireballs|Wand of Fireballs]] into the exposed head at extremely close range. The blast severely burned the dragon but also hurt Jonks; he survived his own explosion by expending inspiration. [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Cinderfang|Cinderfang]] remained alive. After Jonks provoked him further, the dragon bit him.

The dragon seemed less interested in attacking the group than in obtaining relief from his imprisonment. The ground on which the company stood was partly **[[Worlds/Astodan/People/Cinderfang|Cinderfang]] himself**, trapped within and fused to the mound. The others learned more of [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Jonks|Jonks]]'s past: he had escaped [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Cinderfang|Cinderfang]]'s service after his wife was killed, only to be captured by a necromancer, whose spellbook he later stole before fleeing again.

### The fallen tyrant's offer

[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Linus Lightfoot|Linus Lightfoot]] eventually succeeded in magically **charming [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Cinderfang|Cinderfang]]** with [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Charm Monster|Charm Monster]], giving the group an opportunity to question him. The party heard that [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Cinderfang|Cinderfang]] had once been a feared red-dragon tyrant but was later displaced by another dragon, **[[Worlds/Astodan/People/Skylia the Inferno|Skylia the Inferno]]**. He had disappeared from wider notice and become ensnared in the Caldera's corrupting flesh. [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Cinderfang|Cinderfang]] downplayed responsibility for Ursula's death, claiming he had not intended to kill her; **that was his claim**, not established truth.

Asked what he would do if released, [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Cinderfang|Cinderfang]] declared that he would **kill the [[Worlds/Astodan/People/The Sovereign|Sovereign]] and burn the rest of the Caldera**. Some companions argued that his power could help the mission. [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Jonks|Jonks]]'s history with the dragon and [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Diane|Diane]]'s fear of dragons made the decision far less straightforward. The company also discussed whether any agreement, bargain or valuable offering could keep him from turning on them.

**The surviving March 7 audio stops during that negotiation.** Zach's eyewitness notes record that the group **released [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Cinderfang|Cinderfang]]** and **took a long rest near him**; the player independently remembers the release and believes the rest completed. The March 28 opening recap also confirms the release. Wiley later recalled **the party cutting Cinderfang's restraints** and negotiating **a deal through Jonks's past relationship**, though the terms and exact order of those actions are still unknown. Independently verified completion of that rest remains unknown. At this point [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Cinderfang|Cinderfang]] had stated his hostility to the [[Worlds/Astodan/People/The Sovereign|Sovereign]], but no binding alliance with the party is established. The session ends with the party near [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Cinderfang|Cinderfang]] at the approach to the Caldera.

## ✧ Questions & Mysteries

- Who is **the [[Worlds/Astodan/People/The Sovereign|Sovereign]]**, what occurred during the **Equalizer War**, and how do the two ancient Engines figure into that history?
- How and on what terms can [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Weary|Weary]] recover the soul fragment held by [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Havoc|Havoc]]? What would killing the [[Worlds/Astodan/People/The Sovereign|Sovereign]] actually release? (The subsequent restoration is not shown in this session.)
- What did Havoc mean when he said **Iferit and Withengar** had escaped while he remained imprisoned?
- What caused **[[Worlds/Astodan/People/Cinderfang|Cinderfang]]** to become fused into the corrupted hill, and what relationship does he have to the [[Worlds/Astodan/People/The Sovereign|Sovereign]]?
- Can [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Cinderfang|Cinderfang]]'s stated willingness to attack the [[Worlds/Astodan/People/The Sovereign|Sovereign]] be trusted? What consequences follow from releasing him?
- Did an unidentified third individual sharing [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Arthur Morgan|Arthur]] and [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Other/Damien Castellanos|Damien]]'s body become active here, and did anyone in the party recognize or speak with them?
- What is the true cost of activating or fully reuniting the hemispheres, particularly with only **two protective metal sheets** remaining?
