## Log

### A battle in the library

After the previous expedition's stealthy approach through the [[Worlds/Astodan/Places/Luciferian Caldera|Luciferian Caldera]], the company reached a library somewhere deeper inside the stronghold. The surviving recording begins **after entry**, with combat already underway. It does not show how the party crossed from the crater rim to this room. The company faced **worm-infested zombies** <span class="new-tag">new</span> among shelves approximately fifteen feet high. The combatants knew of at least one visible foe, while the shelving and their positions obscured others.

[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Thornwick|Thornwick]] took to the air to see over the shelves and attempted to strike an enemy with a thrown axe. [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Diane|Diane]] and [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Weary|Weary]] were engaged with another defender. Thornwick at first had only a partial view of the creatures and had to establish what he could actually see before attacking. The recording also contains a confused reference to shadowy demonic assailants, but the exact count and relationship to the worm-infested zombies cannot be established from the preserved conversation.

The short recording cuts off during Thornwick's attempted attack. Zach's dated notes supply the next clear outcome: the party **defeated the enemies in the library** and carried on farther into the Caldera. They record [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Jonks|Jonks]] attempting to burn the library, without saying whether he succeeded. A brief note about an unpleasant incident involving Weary preserves the table's humor but provides no reliably identifiable magical effect or lasting condition.

### The first confrontation with the Sovereign

The next identifiable episode is the encounter for which the session was named: the fight with **[[Worlds/Astodan/People/The Sovereign|the Sovereign]]** <span class="new-tag">new</span>. Zach's April 4 notes identify **[[Worlds/Astodan/People/Cinderfang|Cinderfang]]**, the red dragon who formerly enslaved Jonks, as a victim of the Sovereign. Player recollection clarifies the sequence: **the Sovereign killed Cinderfang before the company met him** and **used the dragon's skull as a weapon against the party during the confrontation**. The party did not witness Cinderfang's death; its exact date and manner are not established. Which attacks involved the skull and whether they occurred in this first portion or the next session are not preserved.

Another unexpected arrival changed the fight: **[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Noisy Owlbear|Noisy Owlbear]] returned**, despite having truly died during the company's earlier journey through the Wastes. Player recollection places the return shortly before or during the Sovereign battle, perhaps through a **hellish portal**. The portal's nature, who opened it, and whether this was a resurrection or another form of return remain unknown. Zach's brief note associates Owlbear with a sudden drop and fright, but the exact mechanical sequence is unclear. His confirmed participation continues in the next session.

The battle was not concluded in the surviving record of **Session 25**. The next dated set of Zach's notes is headed **“Sovereign Fight Pt. 2”**, placing the extended combat and its resolution in the following session rather than here.

> [!info]- DM retrospective clarification (OOC)
> Wiley recalled that [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Noisy Owlbear|Noisy Owlbear]] **fought his way through Hell** to return while the planes were temporarily merged. This is not evidence of a formal summoning or resurrection spell; avoid changing the other mechanics of the confrontation.
