## Log

### The orb and the changing mausoleum

Having passed the entrance riddle, the company continued into the [[Worlds/Astodan/Places/Infinite Mausoleum|Infinite Mausoleum]]. Its interior did not remain stable for long. Zach's May 2 notes say that **Grayson touched an orb, and the dungeon changed**. That attribution needs review: [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Grayson|Grayson]] was last seen disappearing during the exchange involving [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Havoc|Havoc]], and no intervening return is documented. The notes also do not establish whether this orb is the same object implicated at the end of [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Session Journal/To the Infinidungeon!|Session 28]] or the assembled life-engine.

[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Jingles|Jingles]] succeeded on an exceptional History check and seemed to recognize or understand the dungeon. Unfortunately, the particular information he learned is missing from the surviving notes. Before the group could progress much farther, **combat began**, and Jingles summoned **ten berserk warriors** to assist.

### A hydra-like dragon

The party fought several foes, including a **many-headed, dragon-like hydra** <span class="new-tag">new</span>. [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Noisy Owlbear|Noisy Owlbear]] killed two opponents whose identities Zach did not record. [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Mirage|Mirage]] rushed in and sliced a head from the hydra, while Jingles's summoned warriors surrounded the creature, attacked it, and severed another head. The hydra regrew heads, however, and attempted to unleash a breath attack. Mirage continued the assault, leaping at the hydra and taking another head.

Zach also records that Grayson was stabbed during the fighting, though the name shares the same continuity problem as the earlier orb interaction. [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Diane|Diane]] kept her distance because of what the notes call the **"D-word"**, likely referring to the dragon-like foe given her earlier traumatic experience, but that reading is not independently confirmed. [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Isabelle|Isabelle]] rode her steed into battle. Around this point, an enemy scored a critical hit, knocking a dragon-like combatant prone; the participants and target are not reliably identifiable from the handwriting.

### The seed box and a hostile double

During the encounter, Owlbear found a **box containing magical seeds and dirt**. Isabelle helped him with the seeds, but Zach questioned whether they would actually help. The notes then say that **a "bad guy" version of Owlbear** <span class="new-tag">new</span> was born. The proximity of the entries suggests a connection, but does not establish precisely how the double came into being, what it was, or whether the seeds caused it.

Jingles then apparently used [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Haste|Haste]] or a similar hastening effect and attacked the hydra. **The session ended with combat still underway**, leaving the hydra and Owlbear's new counterpart unresolved.

## ✧ Questions & Mysteries

- **Grayson or another character?** Zach explicitly assigns the orb touch and a stab wound to [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Grayson|Grayson]], although the historical record lists him as presumed dead after Session 23. Was Grayson actually present, or is this a mistaken name?
- **What changed in the mausoleum?** What did the orb interaction physically alter, and was this the same orb or apparent map effect mentioned at the end of Session 28? Do not equate it with the life-engine without confirmation.
- **What did Jingles learn?** His History result was excellent, but the specific dungeon knowledge he recalled is missing. What did the party hear?
- **Who or what was the hostile Owlbear?** Was it produced by the box of seeds, and what could the party actually tell about it? Its later role should not be assumed yet.
- **Unclear combat details:** Who were the two foes Owlbear killed? What creature was knocked prone after Isabelle's charge? Was Jingles casting [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Haste|Haste]], or merely acting with increased speed?
