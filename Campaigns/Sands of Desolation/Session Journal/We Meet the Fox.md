## Log

### ✧ The Missing Rings

The company awakened at the [[Worlds/Astodan/Organizations/Mechanical Wonders|Mechanical Wonders]] after its night of troubling dreams. [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Drax|Drax]] and [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Thrax|Thrax]] greeted the group with a serious accusation: **five rings had been stolen** from their collection. Although the brothers would accept compensation, they wanted the perpetrator to admit the theft, and arranged private questioning under [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Zone of Truth|Zone of Truth]]. [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Grayson|Grayson]] had already alerted them that something was missing.

The questioning unsettled the company. [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Diane|Diane]] and Grayson were treated as having alibis; [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Thornwick|Thornwick]], [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Arthur Morgan|Arthur]], [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Weary|Weary]], and [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Isabelle|Isabelle]] were among those questioned. Thornwick described his missing son, the hag, and the dark figure that had visited his dream; one of the brothers observed that the hag had not possessed the power to cause everything Thornwick attributed to her. [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Amorpha|Amorpha]] admitted an impulsive habit of taking things and searched her belongings. She found **three of the stolen rings**, which the brothers regarded as less important than the other two. She paid **100 gp** in compensation and was allowed to keep the three rings. **The remaining two rings were still missing.**

The quarrel ended sufficiently for everyone to share a meal. Grayson remained disguised, although the companions and the brothers were aware of the unnatural nature of his appearance. [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Isabelle|Isabelle]] also received the **steel defender** commissioned at the previous visit. The construct was bonded to her, while her living animal companions continued to react badly to Thornwick. He himself noticed that his body was becoming heavier, shorter, and less familiar.

### ✧ The Road Toward the Wastes

The Mechanical Wonders supplied provisions and feed for [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Diane|Diane]] and [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Weary|Weary]]'s caravan and horses, Dawn and Dusk, and confirmed that [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Mysteries/The Hemisphere of Gideon|Gideon's hemisphere]] had been secured for the journey with protective sheets meant to last for several days. The brothers urged the company to seek the missing complementary half of the rejuvenation engine.

They recommended travelling toward **[[Worlds/Astodan/Places/New Phyrexia|New Phyrexia]]**, a fortified outpost in [[Worlds/Astodan/Places/The Wastes|the Wastes]], where vessels might provide passage toward the [[Worlds/Astodan/Places/Luciferian Caldera|Luciferian Caldera]]. An overland route through the mountains was described as dangerous, with an alternative passage through the Wastes mentioned as well. The party prepared to leave [[Worlds/Astodan/Places/Anvil's Flame|Anvil's Flame]].

[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Diane|Diane]] offered [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Isabelle|Isabelle]] the option to leave the hazardous mission and begin a new life. Isabelle chose to remain, hoping the party's dealings with [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Falatir|Falatir]] and the entities involved in her return might help her recover her missing memories, discover the truth of her condition, and find her husband. She remained wary of several companions, particularly [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Thornwick|Thornwick]], whom the animals regarded with open hostility. The group debated how much trust could be expected from someone only recently returned to life.

### ✧ Identification, Ruined Wand, and the Cursed Axe

Before leaving the city, the company stopped at an unnamed dwarf wizard's establishment for magical identification. The rings brought for examination included a **Ring of Animal Influence**, which could invoke effects such as [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Animal Friendship|Animal Friendship]], [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Fear|Fear]] on a beast, or [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Speak with Animals|Speak with Animals]], and a **Ring of Protection**. Another ring was identified as a **Ring of Shooting Stars**, associated with [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Dancing Lights|Dancing Lights]], [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Light|Light]], and [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Faerie Fire|Faerie Fire]], among other effects. The precise ownership and charges of the rings should be checked against surviving inventories rather than assumed from the transcript.

[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Weary|Weary]] attempted to enhance the apparent value of a wand with [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Distort Value|Distort Value]], apparently hoping to outwit the examiner. Disagreement over identification fees followed. When Weary handled the unidentified wand instead of completing the paid examination, it crumbled into ash. The DM described it afterward as a **Wand of Fear**.

The wizard also revealed why [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Thornwick|Thornwick]] had begun to change shape: his ancient dwarven axe was cursed. A non-dwarf attuned to it would gradually acquire a dwarf-like appearance, eventually looking like a dwarf after seven days without changing racial abilities. The wizard explained that the effect could persist after attunement ended and required curse removal, rather than ordinary dispelling, to reverse. The axe also possessed hovering and recall properties. Its **exact name remains unclear** in the recording.

Thornwick then paid to have his own magical condition examined. When the wizard tried to study the power affecting him, an **explosion of necrotic energy injured the examiner**. The wizard warned that Thornwick had offended an exceptionally powerful being and that the mask's magic was not simply the hag's work. On the road, Thornwick explained the previous night's dark visitor to [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Diane|Diane]]. She cautiously treated his increasing dwarf-like stature using [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Greater Restoration|Greater Restoration]], reversing the physical transformation. The fused mask and the dangerous effect surrounding it were left alone. She also repaired his damaged hammock with [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Mending|Mending]]. During the examination, [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Weary|Weary]] and Thornwick traded minor magical pranks involving [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Prestidigitation|Prestidigitation]] and [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Druidcraft|Druidcraft]].

### ✧ The Failed Scrying

The company made camp along a rocky mountain pass on the way toward the Wastes, still several days from its destination. [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Weary|Weary]] established [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Mordenkainen's Private Sanctum|Mordenkainen's Private Sanctum]] around the camp for protection.

[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Thornwick|Thornwick]] asked [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Diane|Diane]] to locate his son through [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Scrying|Scrying]]. He provided a picture, but the spell could not establish a view of the child. Diane warned him that some form of interference or the son's location might be responsible; neither explanation was proven. **His son's location remained unknown at the end of this attempt.**

### ✧ First Watch — A Visitor of Living Darkness

[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Thornwick|Thornwick]] kept watch apart from the other campers, hovering on his axe and reflecting on the choices that had cost him his kingdom and son. [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Isabelle|Isabelle]] kept watch closer to the caravan with her animal companions and steel defender. The same inky-dark figure from Thornwick's earlier dream materialized beside him. Thornwick attempted to judge its nature and sensed **necromancy and power comparable to that of gods**.

The figure treated Thornwick's situation as a **game**, taunting him with the possibility of recovering his kingdom and reclaiming the title of king. It questioned his trust in his companions and hinted that his lost son was in danger. Rather than giving clear rules, it suggested that Thornwick was already playing and that time was running out. Threatened with losing his son, Thornwick agreed to play, though the nature and terms of any bargain were not clearly established.

The visitor then walked to Weary's sanctum and **cut through the magical ward without triggering its protections**. Isabelle's animals and defender froze with fear or supernatural restraint, and the figure prevented her from waking Arthur. Speaking to Isabelle, it claimed that Falatir had recovered **only two of five magical shards of her soul**. According to the visitor, Falatir tried to surrender his own soul as payment for hers; that bargain let Isabelle take form but did not make her whole. The figure also claimed that Falatir had entered a form of **servitude** and suggested he might yet be involved in returning her remaining fragments. Isabelle resisted its efforts to tempt her into another deal. She chose to continue with the party despite the uncertainty surrounding her husband, kingdom, and restoration.

The visitor restrained Isabelle with dark tendrils and vanished after further warnings. Its name was **not spoken to the characters** in the scene. Afterwards Isabelle confronted Thornwick about his apparent willingness to play along with the entity. Thornwick said his son's soul was at stake and felt he had little choice; Isabelle warned that accepting the rules of an unknown game might itself amount to a bargain. The damaged portion of the sanctum remained visibly carved away.

### ✧ Second Watch — An Old Vision Returns

[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Arthur Morgan|Arthur]] and [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Amorpha|Amorpha]] took the next watch. The party told them about the visitor, and they noticed the unnatural opening cut into the protected camp. Isabelle's steel defender had also been instructed to watch Amorpha for further thefts, which she noticed.

As rain and lightning passed over the mountain road, Amorpha briefly perceived a **pirate-like figure** in the distance, wearing a broad coat and carrying a slender blade. The figure disappeared with the next flash. Amorpha realized this was **a vision in her mind**, not an ordinary person standing on the road, and recognized the same presence from her earlier dream. The figure's name and purpose remained unknown. Arthur tried to speak kindly to her about her past thefts and offered to make a game of catching attempts to pickpocket him, rather than condemning her outright.
