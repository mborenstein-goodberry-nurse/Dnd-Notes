We start back up with the Shadowfell Party
There are 2 [[Nightwalkers]] guarding the spire we're heading to 
Those who rolled poorly on [[Perception]] see fellow party members as enemy imposters 
Aldrin tries to hit Waffles, starts talking it out

[[Arthur Morgan|Damien]] rolls [[Medicine]], and is able to remove them from [[Waffles]] 
Looks like a vampiric shell, with a pustule of blood instead of a shell 
We all have slugs, but Damien, Waffles, and Thornwick have the most 
We remove the slugs from ourselves 
Waffles tried to keep one to eat later. Aaldrin will remember this 
### The Path less Travelled By
We come across the crossroads. We see a left path and a right path. The left path looks interesting, We go right. In a cave, we find a cave with a pool of black ichor in the middle 
In the pool is a long white squared pillar 
The pillar is just a pillar 
Thornwick dips a toe in. It feels refreshing 
As some of us touch the pillar, we all fall [[Unconscious]] 
The pillar turns into the Fox 
He starts taunting us 
Thornwick's son was watching?! (Maybe a joke)
Is going to let us live because wants us to live for a bit longer 
He breaks Caspian and [[Thornwick]]'s wings, and breaks Waffles legs 
A bit of our soul breaks off as well, our connection to them 
The pillar is now rubble, the cavern begins to shake 
Various eyes open on the stalagmites and stalagtites. It's a room full of Darkmantles 
Aldrin puts me in his adventurers pack and runs 
Damien picks up Thornwick and runs 
Caspian nat 1s 
We make our way back to the fork and go down the left path like we should've
