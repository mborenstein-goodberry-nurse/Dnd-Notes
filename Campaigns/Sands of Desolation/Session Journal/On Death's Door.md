## Log

### ✧ The Hallowed Bastion

After resting near the frontier, the party traveled deeper into the [[Gilded Lands]]. They entered the [[Hallowed Bastion]], a remarkably well-kept necropolis of pale stone and purple roofs, with graves and tombs for both people and beasts. Undead were present but did not immediately attack; the grounds seemed to function as a passage or destination for the dead.

Will-o'-wisps guided the group through the graveyard. Along the way the party encountered a **minotaur** played by Charles, who said he had been killed along with others in a battle against an unusually powerful, unidentified figure and had appeared here afterward. His character's name was not clearly recovered in the supplied source.

At a junction the party saw paths associated with **nobles**, **game beasts**, and **lessers**. The wisps directed them toward the game-beast route. The group instead explored the noble route, where the markers and tombs were more elaborate. The wisps repeatedly warned them to return and leave the grounds, threatening death if they continued against their directions. [[Falatir]] described a sense of familiarity or a pull from the place. Elsewhere, the party saw a warforged worker handling the body of the [[Basilisk|basilisk they had killed]]. They learned that dead creatures from within the Gilded Lands could be brought here, though the reason and process remained unclear. The party ultimately left the necropolis.

### ✧ A Merchant at the Grand Exchange

Moving through the populated lands, the party reached the [[Grand Exchange]], a large urban market within the Gilded Lands. This was a wealthy but openly slaveholding, human-supremacist society; visibly nonhuman companions had reason to be wary. The group sought tea, coffee, and supplies and encountered a merchant who introduced himself as [[Duke Ingrail]]. He offered a night's lodging and dinner at [[Ingrail's Estate]], presenting himself as an unusually benevolent owner of enslaved people.

His household servants wore gleaming **brass collars** and appeared unnaturally content. Diane discreetly examined one. It seemed to be a powerful magical apparatus, with a rectangular component near the back of the neck, but the exact mechanism was not clear to her during the inspection. Ingrail became suspicious when he noticed her scrutiny. The guests also learned that Ingrail had connections to Sun Home or claimed familiarity with its faith, which unsettled Diane. A minotaur companion and Thornwick engaged in an arm-wrestling contest during the visit; Thornwick won.

### ✧ Dinner and the Ambush

The party accepted a meal at [[Ingrail's Estate]]. Diane warned the others that the collars might be dangerous and that Ingrail's pleasant manner should not be trusted. Ingrail later served drinks; the party was **poisoned**, and household guards and assassins moved to subdue them. The company resisted against **anti-magic restraints**, magical collars, and spells. Diane continued holding out after the other companions fell. Rather than continue a fight with no allies still standing, **she offered to surrender on the condition that the others be allowed to live**. [[Duke Ingrail]] accepted her terms. The party was subsequently taken captive and enslaved.

The available Session 2 recording ends before the surrender is resolved. Diane's negotiated terms come from a later firsthand player recollection; the party's captivity is corroborated by the Session 3 recap and historical notes.

**Encounters and conditions:** avoided open conflict in the necropolis; at [[Ingrail's Estate]], the party was poisoned and fought captors before Diane negotiated a surrender to protect the fallen party members. No permanent item gains are verified. The servants' collars are understood by the historical notes to impose magical domination, but Diane had not conclusively identified that effect on first inspection.

## ✧ Questions & Mysteries

- Why are dead creatures from throughout the Gilded Lands brought to the [[Hallowed Bastion]]? Who controls the process?
- Who killed the unidentified minotaur character and the others before he appeared in the necropolis?
- Why did the necropolis seem to call to [[Falatir]]?
- How do the collars and anti-magic restraints at [[Ingrail's Estate]] work, and how can the party escape?
- What happened to the party's belongings and [[Aceso Aid|caravan]] after the capture?
