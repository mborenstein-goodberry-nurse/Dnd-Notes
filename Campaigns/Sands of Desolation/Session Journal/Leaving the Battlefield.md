## Log

### The price of handling the engine

The company remained in the throne chamber of **[[Worlds/Astodan/People/The Sovereign|the Sovereign]]** in the [[Worlds/Astodan/Places/Luciferian Caldera|Luciferian Caldera]], surrounded by the aftermath of its difficult victory. The creature's body lay beside an opening in the chamber, with a poisonous cloud hanging over the wound that [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Thornwick|Thornwick]] had torn into its chest. The survivors still believed they had destroyed seven of its nine hearts or pods, but could not be certain this was the end of the threat.

[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Weary|Weary]] and [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Noisy Owlbear|Noisy Owlbear]] had recovered one of the two engine hemispheres in the preceding session's aftermath. Mishandling its flat surface had proved dangerous. As play resumed, **[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Jonks|Jonks]]** found the same flat side touching his hands; using his last inspiration, he flipped it before it pierced his skin. [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Diane|Diane]] was understood to have the other half. Neither hemisphere was assembled.

Diane cast [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Aura of Vitality|Aura of Vitality]] to heal her companions, who were still reeling from the fight. The chamber's other yellow sphere still contained the motionless body of a woman, while a radiant, disembodied woman labored nearby.

### The captive woman and the throne's controls

At the throne, [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Isabelle|Isabelle]] searched for something connected to her missing soul. A console bore three Infernal sigils: **release**, **contain**, and **expel**. Isabelle was able to read their meaning, and investigation suggested that the control might connect to the yellow spheres. She chose **release**.

The spheres softened and ran down into their bases, leaving the suspended woman's body to fall to the floor. The glowing figure approached it. [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Weary|Weary]] watched as she pressed into the body's forehead and slowly merged with it. The woman revived and introduced herself as **[[Worlds/Astodan/People/Wonder|Wonder]]**, wife of [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Warrick|Warrick]], Weary's brother. Wonder, who had previously been missing, was disoriented but eager to leave the chamber. In conversation she spoke of [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Gideon|Gideon]] as her patron and of his help in trying to restore her form. Her earlier glowing state and the exact circumstances of her separation from her body were not completely explained.

While others continued worrying about the Sovereign's remaining hearts, someone activated **expel**. The Sovereign's corpse descended into one of the holes beneath the chamber. An ornate box rose on a small pedestal, displaying a fragment of soul. [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Isabelle|Isabelle]] approached and took the fragment; it melted into her skin. She felt more whole and recovered partial memories, including an unsettling impression of having met the **Pirate King** at a gathering or ball. The timing and details of that memory remained confused, and something about one of the present companions still made her uneasy. Isabelle kept those impressions to herself.

[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Diane|Diane]] provided further healing to the injured. With the body recovered, the soul fragment reclaimed, and the halves of the engine in hand, the company could finally turn toward escape.

### An uneasy road out of the Caldera

The party debated what to do with the hemispheres. Some wanted to return them to the [[Worlds/Astodan/Places/Griffin Empire|Griffin Empire]] and consult a sage before experimenting with them. Others were curious about joining them immediately. For the moment, they remained **separate**. [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Thornwick|Thornwick]] also revealed a personal destination: he wished to go to the **City of Brass**, where he believed his son was. No final order of travel was settled.

The climb back to the crater's surface was tiring. Once outside, the companions were struck by the lack of fiends and demons: the forces they had previously seen massed around the Caldera were gone. The oppressive landscape was still barren, but the clouded sky seemed less choked with smog. [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Weary|Weary]]'s assessment suggested that the land's curse might slowly weaken with the [[Worlds/Astodan/People/The Sovereign|Sovereign]] defeated, although the time required was unknown.

[[Worlds/Astodan/People/Wonder|Wonder]] traced **Sylvan** runes on the ground and completed a summoning circle. Black shadow gathered and a heavily armed, shadowy **centaur** emerged. He introduced himself as **Nocturne Stormclaw** <span class="new-tag">new</span> (name as heard in the transcript; spelling needs verification), a guide whom Wonder's patron had helped her find. Thornwick was suspicious of the shadow magic and initially worried about a connection to [[Worlds/Astodan/People/The Fox|the Fox]]. Nocturne did not claim any loyalty to the Fox. The company eventually accepted his help in finding a route out.

### The halves divide the company

Exhaustion forced the companions to stop and rest near the outer crater wall. [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Leomund's Tiny Hut|Leomund's Tiny Hut]] gave the group shelter, though argument broke out over who could enter and who should hold each engine half. **[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Jonks|Jonks]]** increasingly pressed to put the hemispheres together, believing their power ought to be used. Others, including [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Weary|Weary]] and [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Diane|Diane]], argued that an ancient world-shaping device should not be assembled without understanding the risk. The company had not agreed to attempt it.

During the night, **[[Worlds/Astodan/People/Havoc|Havoc]]**, the archdemon once imprisoned in Weary's sword, appeared outside the camp. He was pleased by the Sovereign's defeat and the bloodshed the party had caused. Claiming their destruction of at least five of the Sovereign's hearts fulfilled a condition, Havoc offered a blessing to aid their escape: a fiend-like disguise or a hazardous teleportation whose destination he could not reliably control. The party favored the **disguise**. Havoc's motives remained his own; his apparent willingness to help was not an assurance of friendship.

The disagreement over the engine escalated. [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Jonks|Jonks]] attempted to obtain the hemisphere held by the shared body of **[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Arthur Morgan|Arthur]]**, [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Other/Damien Castellanos|Damien]], and [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Other/Soren|Soren]]. At first he cast [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Invisibility|Invisibility]] to try to bring his own half to it without consent. When that failed, a physical struggle followed, during which Jonks grappled the other character at very close quarters in an attempt to make him release the device. The body defended itself with dragon breath. [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Diane|Diane]], alarmed by the danger and the confrontation, cast [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Hold Person|Hold Person]] to stop the struggle. Weary took one hemisphere away from immediate reach.

The struggle continued despite the magical restraint. The recording associates **[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Other/Soren|Soren]]** with a forceful retaliation through a summoned gauntlet, badly injuring Jonks until he fell unconscious. A further change in the shared body's appearance preceded an attempt to stabilize him, which succeeded. The exact transitions among Arthur, Damien, and Soren during the exchange are not fully clear from the audio; those identities should not be treated as interchangeable. Diane remained frightened, [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Thornwick|Thornwick]] withdrew to cool off, and the camp was left shaken. **No hemisphere was successfully joined.**

### A guide through the mountains

By morning the DM confirmed the group had received a long rest, its acute exhaustion had abated, and Jonks was conscious again. The party's disagreement was less easily resolved. [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Havoc|Havoc]]'s promised magic was visible as a reddish glow around the travelers; although they could not perceive themselves as fully transformed, the protection was understood to make them appear like fiends while escaping the Caldera. The half [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Jonks|Jonks]] had held was now with **[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Weary|Weary]]**, and the half Diane had guarded had passed to the [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Arthur Morgan|Arthur]] / [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Other/Damien Castellanos|Damien]] / [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Other/Soren|Soren]] shared body.

The group set out with **Nocturne** guiding a route over the mountains rather than risking unreliable tunnels or the main entrance. They crossed ridges and descended toward greener land after a long, difficult trek. At last they emerged into **[[Worlds/Astodan/Places/Foo Fighters|Foo Fighters]] territory**, beyond the immediate reach of the Caldera. They had escaped the battlefield, but carried the divided engine and the unresolved effects of a bitter night with them.
