## Log

### An audience with Grimgor

The company began the day in a tavern in [[Worlds/Astodan/Places/Foo Fighters|Foo Fighter territory]], still carrying injuries and spent magic from its recent troubles. The tavern's other patrons cleared out as several local leaders arrived. Their principal speaker introduced himself as [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Grimgor|Grimgor]], the grand chieftain. He wore a striking headband that the party recognized as a **[[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Treasure/Headband of Intellect|Headband of Intellect]]**; one of his companions quietly cast an unidentified **evocation** spell during the introductions, but the group could not establish its purpose.

The company introduced itself under the traveling name **Aceso Aide** and explained that it had been sent by the [[Worlds/Astodan/Places/Griffin Empire|Griffin Empire]] to investigate the [[Worlds/Astodan/Places/Luciferian Caldera|Luciferian Caldera]]. Grimgor said that demons and devils had formed an alliance, but that the Foo Fighters had defended their territory against fiendish incursions for a very long time. He allowed the travelers to proceed. If they returned through his lands, however, he wanted them checked for demonic or devilish influence. He also warned that their enemies might take deceptive forms.

Grimgor knew that some of his guards had been killed in the group's earlier fighting. Rather than retaliating, he acknowledged their demonstrated strength and advised that they not wield it carelessly. He offered an escort toward the western gate and a chance to recover. He mentioned local smiths and a northern mausoleum where unusual equipment had been found, although remaining there too long could lead to getting lost. The travelers agreed to spend some time resupplying in town rather than immediately heading west.

### Zoff's curious merchandise

The party split its attention between local inquiries, cleaning and tending the caravan, and shopping. **[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Grayson|Grayson]]** visited a shop run by [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Zoff|Zoff]], an elderly-seeming goblin with no legs who moved using his arms and a wheeled stool. When Grayson asked about magical goods, Zoff showed him what he called a **[[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Treasure/Deck of Many Things|Deck of Many Things]]**. The authenticity of the deck was not verified, and the possibility of using it alarmed the others.

Grayson bargained with Zoff and ultimately paid **75 gp for both the deck and a magical hand axe**. Zoff said the axe came from the northern mausoleum. Neither item's magical properties were demonstrated before the sale. Grayson also produced a small metal ball, asking whether Zoff could make more of it. The merchant could not identify it with certainty and directed him again toward the mausoleum. With the purchase complete, the party regrouped.

### A night of strong drink

The travelers went westward and found lodging in a smaller, shabby settlement. [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Weary|Weary]] bought a large bottle of the local drink from an ogre at the inn and was drawn into a contest with an orc. Another member of the party, appearing as an orc, cast **[[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Friends|Friends]]** and deliberately stirred up the challenge. A crowd began gathering as the first drinks proved punishingly strong. **The surviving recording does not reveal who won the contest**, and the next recording resumes with the group continuing its Caldera plans.

### The assistant at the wooded approach

After further travel, the group approached the shaman's retreat through an unusually fruitful and verdant stretch of woodland. A hooded, heavily bandaged figure sat meditating beside a path. He presented himself as the shaman's assistant and seemed unwilling to disclose anything about his appearance. When the party pressed him, he uncovered his face: **[[Worlds/Astodan/People/Warrick|Warrick]]**, Weary's estranged younger brother.

[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Weary|Weary]] approached, and old grievances resurfaced immediately. Warrick still bore the broken horn from their childhood confrontation. They accused each other of violence, abandonment, and failures of family loyalty; [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Other/Havoc's Sword|Havoc's Sword]], which had taken part of Weary's soul, remained an especially painful subject. The brothers also spoke of their missing parents. Weary said their letters had simply stopped arriving and that, when he returned home, their belongings were left behind. Neither brother supplied a verified explanation for the disappearance. As the argument unfolded, the surrounding trees seemed to close in, and the party detected magic within them.

The group divided for a time. **[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Weary|Weary]] and [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Diane|Diane]] stayed with Warrick**, who returned to meditating, while the others went farther into the grove in search of the shaman.

### Guidance from Narci

The visitors met [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Narci|Narci]], a shaman named in Zach's handwritten account. She referred to herself as a faithful servant of **Vigor**. When [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Isabelle|Isabelle]] asked about her incomplete return from death, Narci said that she had come back too early and that pieces of her were still missing. She touched Isabelle's forehead, restoring some of her memories and allowing her to recall her past more clearly, but she made clear that Isabelle was not yet whole.

Narci offered counsel to the others as well. She could not immediately provide everything they sought and said she would need to consult Vigor; they could return to her after the task ahead. She advised **[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Amorpha|Amorpha]]** that her confusing dreams would eventually lead her toward a path and cautioned her about stealing from the wrong person. Her conversation with **[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Thornwick|Thornwick]]** centered on change, responsibility, and whether his hopes of reconnecting with his son required becoming the father the boy needed. These were words of guidance, not fulfilled promises.

Asked about the Caldera, Narci described **two approaches**. The open route through the mountains was crowded by fiends and orcs who had succumbed to corruption. A second route, a tunnel, was **currently stable** but might collapse, close, or injure those passing through. The cart might not fit safely. The group had her indicate the approaches on its map. She also spoke about the inevitability of change, leaving its moral direction and consequences to the travelers' choices rather than promising any certain outcome.

Time behaved strangely in the grove. The conversation seemed to last only a short while to those inside, but when they returned to the others, **many hours had elapsed** and evening had arrived.

### Warrick's account of the spires

While the others met Narci, [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Weary|Weary]] and [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Diane|Diane]] continued talking with Warrick. Confronted about the [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Mysteries/The Necrotic Spires|necrotic spires]], Warrick **claimed** that he had bargained with a fiend who promised him power in return for opening a gateway to Hell. He said the spell supplied to him had not been what he believed it was, and that he raised the spires **by accident**, not deliberately. According to his account, the ritual required a living person; he did not identify who that was. These statements explain **Warrick's version** of the event, not an independently demonstrated mechanism.

He also described possession of [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Other/Havoc's Sword|Havoc's Sword]], the cursed weapon once bound to Weary. The weapon had whispered to him, calling Weary an unsuitable host. Warrick said it could not claim part of his soul because he did not own it, and that he had since lost the sword. He said his wife, **[[Worlds/Astodan/People/Wonder|Wonder]]**, was missing; a book had apparently supplied information about her, but the brothers corrected an initial claim of her death to **"lost."** What actually happened to her remains unknown. When the missing sword came up again, Warrick gave a cryptic warning **not to search for it**, saying it would find Weary.

The brothers' anger softened enough for Weary to offer help if Warrick ever found a way to break free of his patron. Warrick did not say that he was free of the pact. They parted without a fight, although neither had resolved their distrust or found their family.

### Toward the tunnel

Back together, the travelers discussed Narci's routes. They favored the mountain tunnel over the exposed route into the Caldera, while recognizing that either could be dangerous. They also decided not to divert to the distant northern mausoleum. The party set off to **camp near the tunnel and enter it the following morning**. The session ended with the Caldera investigation still ahead of them.
