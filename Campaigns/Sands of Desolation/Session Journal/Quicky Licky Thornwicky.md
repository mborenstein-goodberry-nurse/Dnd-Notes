### Recap 
We are headed to the Northern Spire to destroy it as it is less defended 
We noticed on the way up a giant mushroom, almost the same size as the spire itself 
We know not of the myconoids who live there, but we are headed that way 
On our way we met Krell, a giant raven creature with his shield guarding slick, who have decided to assist us so we can get into the [[City of Brass]] Ozzie and Damien got very concerned for Jonks's well being 
Jonks is now fully devout to the raven queen, his fox corruptions are gone. He is now a kobold with raven bits, and a second raven head 
Also we leveled up to 17!!! 
### The Quicky Licky 
We are on the outskirts of the Quicky Licky, myconids are looking at us 
Fungus isn't supposed to grow here, so any of them (let alone a big one) is unheard of 
We have to convince Krell to come with us towards the mushroom 
I roll a natuere check: 
The mushroom is magical 
No idea how it got into the shadowfell 
It is producing myconoids, which is common for them 
Should we take a lick we gain haste until the end of the day 
The area we're in is one big flat wasteland, nothing really between us and the mushroom 
As we get within 50 ft of them, the air has gotten super thick. We feel the spores attach themselves to the back of our lungs, Very heavy air, hard to breath 
We hear in our heads "**Who dares approach the Quicky Licky?*** " 
We are indeed speaking with the Quicky Licky 
We try to express our peacefulness, the Quicky Licky sees through us and into our heads 
There is a pressure, then it recedes, and the mushrooms make a path for us 
"***You may take a lick of the quicky licky***" it says 
We are gathered up and we are guided to the volva 
We taste the mushroom, Waffles is no stranger to this life, he can taste it 
We all are [[Haste]]d 
The Quicky Licky did have a favorite licker
It doesn't know how it got here 
It hates us being greedy, and us asking for more 
We reach the Spire faster than the rest of the party
