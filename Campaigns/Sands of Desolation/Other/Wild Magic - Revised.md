# Wild Magic - Revised

**Class:** [[Sorcerer]]  
**Subclass:** Wild Magic - Revised (homebrew)  
**Source:** *DC Wild Magic Bundle*, v2.0, by The Dungeon Coach — Sorcerer: Wild Magic - Revised (p. 17), Wild Magic Surge Tips (p. 3), d300 surge table (pp. 5–9).  
**Campaign:** [[Sands of Desolation]] · **Character:** [[Weary]]

*This campaign uses a revised version of the [[Wild Magic|standard Wild Magic sorcerer]] subclass. Its features below **replace**, rather than add to, the standard subclass progression. Rules are summarized here for table use; consult the original PDF for full text and the 300 surge effects.*

## 1st Level

### Wild Magic Surge

Once per turn, when you cast a spell of **1st level or higher**, make a **Surge Check** with your current **Surge Die**.

- Your Surge Die begins as a **d12**. A roll of **1** triggers a Wild Magic Surge.
- Each **Sorcery Point spent on Metamagic** increases the trigger range by 1 for that casting. For example, spending 2 Sorcery Points triggers a surge on a **1–3**.
- When a Wild Magic Surge occurs, the die erodes one stage: **d12 → d10 → d8 → d6 → d4** (minimum d4).
- Finishing a **[[Long Rest]]** restores **one die stage**, rather than resetting it immediately to d12.
- When a surge occurs, use the **d300 surge procedure** below.

> [!tip] Surge Die progression
> | Most stable | | | | Most unstable |
> | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
> | **d12** | **d10** | **d8** | **d6** | **d4** |
>
> Track the **current** die on the character sheet. It is not automatically assumed to be d12 at the start of a session.

### Tides of Chaos

You can gain **[[Advantage]]** on one attack roll, ability check, or saving throw. After using this feature, it normally becomes available again after a **Long Rest**; however, **making a Surge Check immediately restores its use** (even if the check does not trigger a surge).

## 6th Level

### Bend Magic

As a **[[Bonus Action]]**, you can bend nearby magic for **1 minute**. During this time, make a Surge Check whenever magic is used within **120 feet** of you, including spells, magic items, or other effects the DM treats as magic.

- Any surge triggered through **Bend Magic** uses the **Good** result from the d300 surge table.
- The feature **ends when a surge is triggered** through it.
- You can activate Bend Magic **once per Long Rest**.

## 14th Level

### Controlled Chaos

Your control over surge outcomes improves:

- When you roll the d20 to determine surge quality, **only a 1 is Bad**. Results **2–15 are Neutral**; **16–20 are Good**.
- When casting a spell, you may spend **1 Sorcery Point** to **skip the Surge Check**.

This changes the outcome categories; it does not otherwise replace the d100 roll that selects a surge-table row.

## 18th Level

### Sorcery Surges

When a spell you cast triggers a Wild Magic Surge, you can apply **up to 3 additional Sorcery Points' worth of effects for free** to that spell or, where possible, the Wild Magic Surge effect. Modifications to surge effects depend on **DM approval**.

## d300 Wild Magic Surge Procedure

The revised subclass uses the source's **100 rows × 3 possible outcomes** rather than the standard Player's Handbook d100 table.

1. When a surge triggers, roll **d100** to choose a row and **d20** to choose its outcome category.
2. Use **Bad (1–5)**, **Neutral (6–15)**, or **Good (16–20)**, unless a feature specifies a category or changes these ranges.
3. Look up the full result in *DC Wild Magic Bundle*, **pp. 5–9**. The GM determines unspecified durations, saving throw DCs, and other effect details as appropriate to the situation (see p. 3).

| Feature / Situation | How it affects a surge |
| :--- | :--- |
| Ordinary surge | d20 **1–5 Bad**, **6–15 Neutral**, **16–20 Good** |
| **Bend Magic** (6th level) | Surges triggered through this feature are **Good only** |
| **Controlled Chaos** (14th level) | d20 **1 Bad**, **2–15 Neutral**, **16–20 Good** |

> [!important] Full surge table
> The 300 individual results are **not reproduced** in this note. Use the original *DC Wild Magic Bundle*, version 2.0, pages 5–9 for those effects. This avoids confusing the revised table with [[Wild Magic|the standard subclass's surge table]].

## Campaign Context

[[Weary]] uses this homebrew subclass in *Sands of Desolation*. His childhood experiences with magic suppression and the resulting catastrophic release are part of his **established character backstory**; the homebrew PDF does **not** define a rule that unused magic accumulates over time.

See [[Weary - Character Reference]] for the character-specific quick reference and his current features, once confirmed against his character sheet.
