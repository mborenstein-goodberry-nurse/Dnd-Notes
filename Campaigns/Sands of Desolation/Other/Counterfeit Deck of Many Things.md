### [[Counterfeit Deck of Many Things]]

**Type:** `=this.subtype`  
**Rarity:** Unknown / not applicable (nonmagical)  
**Status:** `=this.status`

#### Features/Abilities

- **None demonstrated.** The cards were revealed to be a **nonmagical fake** in [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Session Journal/Into the Fiery Butthole|Into the Fiery Butthole]].
- Do **not** use the effects, rarity, or procedures of the genuine [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Treasure/Deck of Many Things|Deck of Many Things]] for this counterfeit.

### Notes

- **[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Session Journal/Travel & Lore for Weary|Travel & Lore for Weary]]:** [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Grayson|Grayson]] purchased an allegedly magical deck and an unidentified enchanted hand axe from [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Zoff|Zoff]] for **75 gp total**. At purchase, authenticity was unknown.
- **[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Session Journal/Into the Fiery Butthole|Into the Fiery Butthole]]:** A draw exposed the deck as a **joke/counterfeit** without detectable magic.
- **[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Session Journal/A Mound of Flesh|A Mound of Flesh]]:** Grayson damaged or tore up at least part of the fake deck during the living mound fight. Its final physical condition is uncertain.

*This campaign-specific note describes Grayson's fake purchase. The Wanderer's Guide entry remains the campaign-independent rules reference for a genuine deck.*
