#### Features/Abilities

- A powerful magical sword that dealt unusually high damage in the original Astodan-series campaign; its exact statistics are unknown.
- The weapon housed [[Havoc]], a malicious entity that whispered to [[Weary]], urged him to hurt people, and progressively consumed his soul.
- Havoc prevented Weary from explaining its influence to [[Diane]]. Weary could not rid himself of the sword.

### Notes

At the climax of the first campaign, [[Wonder]] pulled the sword away from Weary. A red mark from his **right hand to his right eye** remained, signifying a wound to his soul. The sword's whereabouts after that moment are not established by the pre-campaign account.
