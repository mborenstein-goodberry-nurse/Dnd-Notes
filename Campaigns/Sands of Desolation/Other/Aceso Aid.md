# Aceso Aid

#### Overview

**Transport Medium:** `=this.transport`  
**Transport Type:** `=this.transportType`  
**Status:** `=this.status`  
**Summary:** `=this.summary`

[[Diane]] and [[Weary]] share a large traveling cart called **Aceso Aid**, a cottage-like home on wheels. The old notes also use the spelling **Aceso Aide** for the sign on its roof. The player tentatively remembers that the couple received the cart **after their marriage**, during the year between campaigns, but the giver, exact date, and method of acquisition are **not established**.

#### Features

**Exterior:** A strong dark-wood frame with larger rear wheels, faded white walls and a brick-red roof. Shuttered windows have hanging planters. There is a larger window near the elevated driver's seat and a back door with a folding stepladder. Lanterns hang over the driver's seat and at the rear. Cooking utensils, buckets, and pots hang outside; the roof carries firewood, tents, and bedrolls. A counter folds out for dining.

**Interior:** Warm red decor and a worn rug give the otherwise crowded space a homey feeling. Crates and foraged supplies sit under the front window. A tail-friendly couch, blanket, coat rack, shelves of jars and keepsakes, a bookcase, writing supplies, and a wall-mounted bunk provide living and working space.

**Horses:**
- **Dawn** — [[Diane]]'s pinto horse.
- **Dusk** — [[Weary]]'s dark bay horse.
