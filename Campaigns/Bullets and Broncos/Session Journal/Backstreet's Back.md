> 4. Theseus

Within 25 feet we lose 10 feet movement, and we roll a con save against [[Proficiency Bonus]] in ranged attack rolls 
Crazed Wildfire Traits: 
- Cooling. Wildfire has a cooling gauge of 60 health. If Wildfire takes a total of 80 cold damage he will cool off some and stop being able to use his "Lava Belch" ability. This same requirement is met if he is reduced to half health, whichever happens first. He will then gain the action "Roiling Flame." At 150 cooled health or one quarter remaining health, Wildfire will calm down. 
- Heat Barrier. Wildfire's exothermic body heat is intense enough to create a super-heated area-of-denial that spreads out in a 50 foot radius with compounding effects: Within five feet creatures take 2d6 fire damage at the start of their turn. Within 15 feet non-magical clothing and gear ignite dealing 2d4 fire damage at the start of their turn (to cease this effect creatures must either wait one minute or use their action to snuff the flames). Within 25 feet Heat blisters start to form and their movement speed is reduced by 10 feet. Within 50 feet creatures are blinded by the light and cannot add their proficiency bonus to ranged attack rolls. 
- Illumination. Wildfire glows a bright reddish-orange that cascades the surrounding area with bright light in an 80-foot radius and dim light for an additional 60-feet. 
- Multiattack. Wildfire may attack twice with his hooves. 
- Super-heated Skin. Whenever a creature strikes Wildfire with a melee attack, they are subjected to taking 3d6 fire damage.
