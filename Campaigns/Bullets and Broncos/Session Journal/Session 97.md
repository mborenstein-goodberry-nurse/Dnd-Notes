## Log
Introduction messager from Andrew: 
> Towards the end of your stay in the ruins of Zaropia, a raiding party from The Mad-dog Gang arrives to ransack the place. The party is led by Dober, Blackdog’s war chief. In the fray the cart is stolen along with Gusty and for some reason he can’t be summoned back. Inside the cart was all of the party’s food and water, not to mention the Haversack which holds the bulk of your funds.  
If this wasn’t bad enough 8 earth monks and an earth priest were kidnapped. It’s imperative that you follow the plot and save what was lost! From 6:30 to 8ish Saturday roleplay amongst yourselves as you make the trek across the Southern Mountain Range. You must hunt and forage along the way as the earth temple can only supply you with a days worth of food due to losses. During this time I’ll answer questions that I can while I work
