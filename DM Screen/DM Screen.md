## ✧ Combat

> [!tip] Your Turn at a Glance
> **Movement up to speed + 1 action.** You may also use **1 bonus action** if a feature or spell permits it, interact with **1 object for free** in normal circumstances, and take **1 reaction** when its trigger occurs (recharges at the start of your next turn).
>
> Movement can be split before and after your action. A round represents about **6 seconds**.

## ✧ Core Rules

### Ability Checks and DCs

| Very Easy | Easy | Medium | Hard | Very Hard | Nearly Impossible |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **5** | **10** | **15** | **20** | **25** | **30** |

- **Checks:** d20 + ability modifier + proficiency bonus (if proficient); success when the total **meets or exceeds** the DC.
- **Advantage / Disadvantage:** Roll 2d20, use higher / lower. Multiple sources don't stack; one of each cancels out. [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Gameplay/Advantage and Disadvantage|Full rules]]
- **Passive checks:** 10 + the normal modifiers (with +5 for advantage or −5 for disadvantage). [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Gameplay/Abilities/Abilities#Passive Checks|Passive Checks]]

### Cover and Positioning

| Cover | Benefit |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Half** | +2 AC and Dexterity saving throws |
| **Three-quarters** | +5 AC and Dexterity saving throws |
| **Total** | Cannot be targeted directly by attacks or spells; area effects may still reach the creature |
