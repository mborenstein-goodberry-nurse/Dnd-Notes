# Undercommon

**Typical speakers:** Merchants and inhabitants of the Underdark, including many drow and other subterranean peoples  
**Script:** Elvish

Undercommon is a shared language used across parts of the Underdark, particularly in trade and communication between communities with very different native tongues. Its value lies in allowing otherwise unrelated peoples to negotiate, exchange information, or simply understand one another underground.

Undercommon uses the Elvish script, but it is separate from [[Elvish]] and [[Sylvan]]. Its reach and local variations depend on the campaign setting.
