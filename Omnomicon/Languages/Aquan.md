# Aquan

**Typical speakers:** Water elementals and other creatures associated with elemental water  
**Script:** Dwarvish (the script used for [[Primordial]])  
**Language family:** [[Primordial]]

Aquan is the water-associated dialect of [[Primordial]], traditionally connected to the Elemental Plane of Water. It may be heard among water elementals and other beings whose homes and cultures are closely tied to elemental seas and depths.

**Mutual understanding:** Creatures speaking Aquan can communicate with speakers of [[Auran]], [[Ignan]], and [[Terran]], the other Primordial dialects.
