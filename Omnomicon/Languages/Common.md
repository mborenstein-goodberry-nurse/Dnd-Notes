# Common

**Typical speakers:** Humans and people of many different ancestries  
**Script:** Common

Common is a widely shared language used for trade, travel, diplomacy, and everyday conversation. It allows people from different communities to communicate without sharing an ancestral language.

The precise history, accent, and reach of Common can vary from one campaign setting to another. A common tongue does not erase local languages, which may remain important to family, tradition, and identity.
