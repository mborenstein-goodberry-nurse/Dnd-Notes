# Orc

**Typical speakers:** [[Races/Orc|Orcs]]  
**Script:** Dwarvish

Orc is the traditional language of orc communities, carrying their stories, names, oral histories, and cultural traditions. Its presence in a region can reflect settlement, trade, travel, or longstanding local ties, rather than conflict alone.

Orc commonly uses the Dwarvish script, but it is a separate language from [[Dwarvish]].
