# Abyssal

**Typical speakers:** Demons and other creatures of the Abyss  
**Script:** Infernal

Abyssal is the language most closely associated with demons and the Abyss, a realm of chaos and destruction. It is encountered in demonic writings, rituals, names, and communication with creatures from that plane.

Although demons and devils are bitter enemies, Abyssal uses the same written script as [[Infernal]]. Knowing one language does not automatically grant an understanding of the other.
