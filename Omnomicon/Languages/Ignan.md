# Ignan

**Typical speakers:** Fire elementals and other creatures associated with elemental fire  
**Script:** Dwarvish (the script used for [[Primordial]])  
**Language family:** [[Primordial]]

Ignan is the fire-associated dialect of [[Primordial]], traditionally connected to the Elemental Plane of Fire. It is spoken by fire elementals and may be encountered among other beings whose origins or cultures are bound to elemental flame.

**Mutual understanding:** Creatures speaking Ignan can communicate with speakers of [[Aquan]], [[Auran]], and [[Terran]], the other Primordial dialects.
