# Celestial

**Typical speakers:** Celestials, including angels and other beings of the Upper Planes  
**Script:** Celestial

Celestial is the language associated with the divine and the Upper Planes. It may appear in sacred inscriptions, celestial names, religious texts, and communication with otherworldly servants of good-aligned powers.

It is distinct from the everyday languages used by worshippers; a priest or devotee does not necessarily know Celestial simply by following a deity.
