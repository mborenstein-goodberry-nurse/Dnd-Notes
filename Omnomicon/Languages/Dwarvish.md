# Dwarvish

**Typical speakers:** [[Dwarf|Dwarves]]  
**Script:** Dwarvish

Dwarvish is the traditional language of dwarven communities, heard in their homes, halls, histories, and crafts. It is an important link to the traditions of dwarven clans and the records they preserve.

Its writing system is also used to record several other languages in the standard D&D language tables, including [[Giant]], [[Gnomish]], [[Languages/Goblin|Goblin]], [[Languages/Orc|Orc]], and [[Primordial]]. Sharing a script does not mean the spoken languages are mutually intelligible.
