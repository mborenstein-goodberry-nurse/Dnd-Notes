## Session 7 — Cave Divers be Like (journal date: 2024-11-02)

### Evidenced chronological beats

1. The party awakens after disturbing nighttime experiences. Thornwick wakes screaming about a hag and her warning that 'she is coming.' Diane is visibly shaken; Weary helps her. Do not automatically ascribe all private dream scenes to all party members' in-character knowledge.
2. The party inspects the hemisphere recovered from Gideon. It has clockwork-like changing parts, an inner purple glow, attachment-like 'legs' on its flat surface, and apparently lacks another half. Testing against a pillow demonstrates a damaging grasping/drilling effect. The party discusses securing and concealing it, including previously cast magical concealment.
3. At the Dwarven Forge, the party seeks the founder Drax and the Mechanical Wonders. A dwarf at the information kiosk directs them toward the founders' establishment; the handwritten journal calls him **Gornwick**, while audio automatic transcription is inconsistent. The party passes through connected caverns to a prestigious district and is received by the guild.
4. They meet Drax and his brother Thrax. Drax examines the hemisphere, removes the damaged pillow, and makes a safer metal plate covering. He cannot fully investigate its workings without specialized equipment.
5. Drax and Thrax describe a siege by duergar from the Grey Mountains that has disrupted mines, equipment, shipments and research. The enemy flies an unexplained banner, which Thornwick recognizes as matching the mark on his flesh. He connects it to **Baba Eris Whitewood**, whom he says destroyed his Fey kingdom, banished him, and fused a skull to his face.
6. The brothers offer aid with the hemisphere and craftsmanship if the party helps recover lost equipment and push back the attackers. They also mention a powerful stolen battleaxe that may be kept as spoils if recovered. This is an offered opportunity, not confirmed loot.
7. A party disagreement develops over revealing sensitive information and the hemisphere's location to people not yet proven trustworthy. Drax returns the hemisphere rather than retaining it.
8. The party discusses improvements to equipment. The later part of B12 describes a **300 gp** arrangement: enhancing Diane's scale mail and Weary's bloodwell vial, plus a once-per-long-rest hex-related property, with a 10 gp contribution by another party member. The exact item effects, enhancement delivery, and future inventory state warrant a dedicated review before permanent loot metadata is changed.
