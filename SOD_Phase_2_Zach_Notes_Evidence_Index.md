# Sands of Desolation - Phase 2: Zach Photo Evidence Index

Source: `Zach's notes.zip`, 42 photographs of handwritten notes (IMG_8024 through IMG_8065). This is a source crosswalk, not a canonical reconstruction. Names and some details are provisional handwritten readings. No original file has been changed.

## Findings

- Dated photo pages match **22 existing session journal entries**, spanning **January 3 to September 5, 2025** (not uninterrupted).
- Of those, **17** journals have blank/placeholder session logs; these photos preserve direct first-hand narrative evidence for future migration.
- These are NOT 22 newly discovered sessions, and do not substitute for recordings; original photographs are a separate evidence source.
- The missing numbered journals **24, 31, 34-36, 38, 43-44, and 46** are still not directly recovered from these photographs.
- Recording 4 (January 17, 2025) is tentatively linked to Session 16, while Zach's Session 16 page **and** the historical journal list January 24, 2025. Flag chronology discrepancy; do not silently force a match.
- The photo for Session 19 and recordings 5-8 share February 16, 2025, an important cross-source chronological anchor.
- One picture, `IMG_8064`, shows character/reference-sheet information and cannot be assigned an independent session date from this view.

## Handling recommendations

1. Preserve the original HEIC photographs and exact image numbers; this index points back to source images.
2. Treat photo dates as dated firsthand notes, not independent proof of the session's actual calendar occurrence. Cross-check with existing journals/calendar/recording audio metadata.
3. When the 26 unidentified older recordings arrive, test against early journal chronology separately. Zach's notes begin January 3, 2025, so they are primarily helpful for the later unrecorded period, not necessarily 2024.
4. During migration, distinguish eyewitness description, quoted dialogue, uncertainty, and party theories. Never treat a player's humorous or speculative remarks as established world facts.
