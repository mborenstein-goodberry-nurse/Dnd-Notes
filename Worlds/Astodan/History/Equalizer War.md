# Equalizer War

## Historical overview
The **Equalizer War** was a global conflict centered on the ancient **Scorpinox Empire**, according to Wiley's original pre-session world briefing. The source also uses **Scorpinosis** for the empire; the preferred spelling is not yet verified. The empire was ruled by **three emperors** and renowned for wealth, advanced technology, and extraordinarily powerful magic. Its center was the **[[Worlds/Astodan/Places/City of Rust|City of Brass]]**, now historically known as the City of Rust.

Wiley's pre-session account says the empire intended to share its accomplishments with the wider world, while other powers feared how great its capabilities had become. Conflict spread across the world and continued for **approximately one thousand years**, with immense magic unleashed throughout the struggle.

A catastrophic spell ultimately destroyed the empire, reducing its heartland to ash and dust. The devastated territory became associated with **[[Worlds/Astodan/Places/The Wastes|the Wastes]]**, from which undead poured. Much of the empire's earlier history was lost.

## Chronology and naming
- **Duration:** About **1,000 years of warfare**. This is the length of the conflict, **not** a confirmed age for its conclusion.
- **End:** Described as **centuries ago**, with no precise date or year established.
- **City name:** **City of Brass** is the ancient original name; **City of Rust** was already common in campaigns preceding Sands of Desolation. Learning the old name was a historical discovery, not a new renaming in this campaign.
- **Empire name:** **Scorpinox / Scorpinosis** are both in the recovered notes; do not arbitrarily pick one as DM-confirmed spelling.

## Later events and artifacts
- In [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Session Journal/Cave Divers be Like|Session 7]], Drax tentatively dated [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Mysteries/The Hemisphere of Gideon|Gideon's hemisphere]] to the Equalizer era.
- In [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Session Journal/Amorphous Dreams|Session 11]], Drax identified it as one half of a rejuvenation engine. **There is no evidence yet that the engine was created for, or used in, the Equalizer War.**
- The more recent ritual associated with [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Warrick|Warrick]] and the necrotic spires described in [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Session Journal/Session 0|Session 0]] is a **separate, later event**. It should not be confused with the original devastation of the ancient empire.

## Source provenance and knowledge boundaries
> [!warning]- Historical briefing, revisions, and in-character knowledge
> **Primary source:** Player's own “Pre-Session Wiley Notes,” transcribed from the original pre-campaign conversation and supplied during Batch 4 review (November 2026). These are eyewitness notes of the initial setting briefing, **not a guarantee that every world detail remained unchanged**.
>
> **Retrospective confirmation:** The player confirms that prior campaigns already used *City of Rust* and that the earlier name *City of Brass* was discovered as historical lore. The exact discovery session is unknown.
>
> **Knowledge distinction:** This note is a cross-campaign **historical reference**, not proof that every character in Sands of Desolation knew all these facts. Weary's prior-campaign access to forgotten history is referenced in the original briefing; the specific knowledge Diane, Weary, or the party shared at each in-character point must be established independently.
