## Description

Grimgor is the **grand chieftain** encountered by the party in [[Worlds/Astodan/Places/Foo Fighters|Foo Fighter]] territory. He came to meet the travelers at a tavern with four other local leaders, wearing a [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Treasure/Headband of Intellect|Headband of Intellect]] that the party recognized. No fuller physical description, age, or private history has been reliably preserved.

He presented himself as **measured but security-minded**. Although he knew the adventurers had killed some of his guards in an earlier clash, he chose to speak with them rather than renew the fighting. He recognized their strength, cautioned them against abusing it, and insisted on safeguards against fiendish influence. These are observed actions, not evidence of an established personal friendship.

    if (value == null) return [];

    if (!value) return null;

        ? value.path
        : String(value)
            .replace(/^!?\[\[|\]\]$/g, "")
            .split("|")[0];

## Campaign History & Knowledge

- **[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Session Journal/Travel & Lore for Weary|Travel & Lore for Weary]] (Session 19):** Grimgor arrived with four other leaders at the tavern where the party was staying. The group explained its mission from the [[Worlds/Astodan/Places/Griffin Empire|Griffin Empire]] to investigate the [[Worlds/Astodan/Places/Luciferian Caldera|Luciferian Caldera]]. Grimgor said the Foo Fighters had resisted demons and devils for a long time and reported that those foes were now cooperating. He permitted the party to travel through his territory, offered an escort toward the western gate, and required a check for demonic or devilish influence if they returned. He also spoke of local smiths and a dangerous northern mausoleum where unusual equipment had reportedly been found.

## Additional Details

**Role:** Grand chieftain among the Foo Fighters. The precise extent of his authority over individual clans, settlements, and the four leaders accompanying him is not established.

**Disposition toward the party:** He allowed passage despite their earlier conflict with guards. His willingness to negotiate should not be inflated into unqualified trust or an alliance.

**Open details:** The identity of his companions, the spell one of them cast during the meeting, and the accuracy of Grimgor's reports about fiends and the mausoleum remain uncertain.
