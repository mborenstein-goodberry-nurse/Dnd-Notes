# Luz

> [!info] Historical identity — [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Wonder|Wonder]]
> **Luz** was the provisional name used for [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Wonder|Wonder]] while she appeared as a disembodied luminous soul. This note preserves the party's discoveries **before her identity was revealed in Session 27**. For her current status, attitude, relationships, and later history, see [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Wonder|Wonder]].
>
> This is a **reference note**, not a second person or active NPC entry.

## Description

**"Luz"** is the provisional name offered by [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Jonks|Jonks]] to an unidentified feminine figure made of intense white light, rescued from an obelisk in [[Worlds/Astodan/Places/Luciferian Caldera|Luciferian Caldera]]. She could not remember her true name or origin. She recalled leaving, screaming, meeting someone, being split in half, helping people and having a husband. Her physical contact could cause painful radiant effects.

Magical examination by Jonks revealed traces of fiendish magic, abjuration and unusual necromancy sustaining her form. He identified her as an **entire soul manifesting visibly**, rather than a normal corporeal person. The party could not determine her alignment or why she had been imprisoned in the obelisk. Some companions reported that her voice resembled the late [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Noisy Owlbear|Noisy Owlbear]]; **this is only a resemblance, not an identification**. She accepted the group's invitation to travel in search of her identity.

A cloak and [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Spells/Invisibility|Invisibility]] could not fully conceal the residual glow she cast upon the ground.

## Campaign History & Knowledge

- **[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Session Journal/Free of the Sword|Free of the Sword]]:** An unidentified luminous figure is recovered during the investigation of the obelisk; the later session begins with the groups rejoining.
- **[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Session Journal/A Soul of Light|A Soul of Light]]:** Diane questions the stranger, Jonks investigates her magical nature and coins "Luz" as a temporary name. The party decides to take her along and attempts to conceal her radiance during the approach into the Caldera.

- **[[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Session Journal/Leaving the Battlefield|Leaving the Battlefield]] (Session 27 — identity revealed):** The luminous soul enters the body released from the Sovereign's yellow sphere. The revived woman identifies herself as [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Wonder|Wonder]], wife of [[Worlds/Astodan/People/Warrick|Warrick]]. **Luz was a temporary name for Wonder in her disembodied state, not a distinct person.** The mechanisms of splitting, magical confinement and rejoining remain unclear.

## Additional Details

> [!warning]- Identity boundary
> The true identity of Luz was **unknown to the party through Session 24**; the identity was revealed in Session 27 through reunification with Wonder's body. The early voice resemblance remained insufficient evidence on its own. Track open questions at [[Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Mysteries/Who Is Luz|Who Is Luz]].
> "Luz" is a **temporary name**, not the name recovered from her memories.
