# Spellcasting Focus

**Category:** Spellcasting equipment

A spellcasting focus is an object through which a magic-user shapes and channels their spells. A focus might be a crystal sphere, a living-wood staff, a sacred emblem, or an instrument whose notes seem to linger longer than they should.

## Rules

When a class feature permits a particular focus, the caster may use it instead of a spell's ordinary material (M) components.

- **Components with an explicit cost** must still be supplied.
- **Components consumed by the spell** must still be supplied.
- A focus does not replace verbal (V) or somatic (S) requirements.
- The caster must hold or otherwise use the focus as their class rules specify. A hand holding the focus can also perform somatic components when the spell requires both somatic and material components; a somatic-only spell still needs a free hand unless another rule says otherwise.

## Common Focuses

| Focus | Typical users |
| --- | --- |
| Arcane focus (wand, orb, rod, crystal, or staff) | [[Sorcerer]], [[Warlock]], [[Wizard]] |
| Druidic focus (mistletoe, sacred wood, or natural totem) | [[Druid]] |
| Holy symbol (amulet, emblem, or reliquary) | [[Cleric]], [[Paladin]] |
| Musical instrument | [[Bard]] |

A cleric or paladin may use a holy symbol by holding it, wearing it visibly, or displaying it on a shield. Other classes may receive different options through their own features (for example, artificers).
