# Component Pouch

**Category:** Adventuring gear  
**Typical cost:** 25 gp  
**Weight:** 2 lb.

A component pouch is a small, weather-resistant belt pouch divided into compartments for the powders, feathers, fragments, and other little objects used in spellcasting. For a traveling mage, it is an unassuming collection of extraordinary necessities.

## Rules

A component pouch can supply a spell's ordinary **material (M) components** without requiring the caster to track each inexpensive ingredient separately.

- It **does not replace** a component with a specific listed monetary cost.
- It **does not replace** a component that the spell consumes; provide such a component for each casting.
- It does not replace verbal (V) or somatic (S) components.
- The caster must have a free hand to access the pouch. The same hand can perform the somatic component of a spell that also requires material components.

Unlike many types of [[Spellcasting Focus|spellcasting focus]], a component pouch is not limited to a particular spellcasting class.
