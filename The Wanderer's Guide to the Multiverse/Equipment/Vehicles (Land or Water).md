# Vehicle Proficiencies

This overview describes the ways adventurers learn to guide a conveyance across the ground, over water, or through the sky. For specific information, consult the individual entries:

| Proficiency | Typical Craft | Rules Context |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[Vehicles (Land)]] | Carts, carriages, wagons, chariots | Standard 2014 vehicle proficiency |
| [[Vehicles (Water)]] | Rowboats, longships, sailing ships | Standard 2014 vehicle proficiency |
| [[Vehicles (Air)]] | Airships and other flying craft | Setting-specific or DM-defined |

**Land and water are separate proficiencies.** If a background grants both, the character has both; proficiency with one doesn't automatically provide the other.

The Eberron [[House Agent]] background uses the wording **vehicles (air and sea)** for House Lyrandar. That option involves both skyborne and seagoing craft, and is distinct from the two generic categories listed in the 2014 *Player's Handbook*.

## Using Vehicle Proficiency

When a difficult maneuver requires an ability check, a character proficient with the relevant kind of vehicle can add their proficiency bonus. Competence also includes basic maintenance, inspections, and repairs. Familiarity with watercraft may include customary seamanship, rigging, nautical hazards, and weather signs.

Related skills can support this knowledge: [[Arcana]] for unusual magical mechanisms, or [[Investigation]] and [[Perception]] to uncover defects, tampering, and concealed details.

*Xanathar's Guide to Everything* provides optional tool-proficiency guidance for land and water vehicles, including these example tasks:

| Example Task | Suggested DC |
| --- | ---: |
| Navigate rough terrain or waters | 10 |
| Assess the condition of a vehicle | 15 |
| Take a tight corner at high speed | 20 |

Its vehicle-handling guidance also describes adding vehicle proficiency to a vehicle's AC and saving throws when piloting it, where the DM is using those rules. This is **supplemental guidance**, not a blanket bonus to the character's own AC or saving throws.
