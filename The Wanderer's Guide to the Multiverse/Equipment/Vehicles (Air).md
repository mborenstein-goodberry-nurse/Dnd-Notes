High above the roads and sea lanes, airborne vessels turn the sky into a route for merchants, explorers, and daring adventurers. Airships, enchanted barges, and other flying craft may be powered by magic, bound elementals, ingenious machinery, or other wonders of a particular setting.

## Proficiency

**Vehicles (Air)** is **not a standard standalone proficiency listed in the 2014 Player's Handbook**: its general vehicle categories are land and water. A campaign that features airships may nevertheless use an air-vehicle proficiency or a setting-specific equivalent, at the DM's discretion.

For example, the House Lyrandar option of the Eberron [[House Agent]] background refers to **vehicles (air and sea)**. In that setting, this represents familiarity with both airborne and seagoing vessels. It should not be mistaken for a proficiency granted to every character who can operate a land or water vehicle.

## Common Airborne Craft

- **Airship:** A crewed flying vessel used for long journeys, transport, or exploration; its operation depends on the campaign setting.
- **Elemental airship:** A magical craft associated with Eberron, where bound elemental power contributes to flight.
- **Flying barge or sky skiff:** A smaller craft that might rely on enchantment, sails, or supernatural currents.

Some items, such as a [[Carpet of Flying]], provide flight without necessarily being treated as a vehicle requiring this proficiency. Similarly, riding a flying creature is not automatically the same as piloting an airship.

## Using Air Vehicles

Airborne travel may involve maintaining altitude, navigating storms, correcting a dangerous bank, bringing a vessel alongside a tower, or making an emergency landing. When an ability check is appropriate, the DM selects the ability and whether air-vehicle proficiency applies. [[Navigator's Tools]] may help plot a course, while [[Arcana]] may help someone understand an enchanted engine.

**There is no universal 2014 core-rules price, speed, or vehicle statblock for all airships.** Use the statistics and special operating rules of the vessel and campaign setting in question. A character able to fly by spell or magic item does not automatically gain airship proficiency.
