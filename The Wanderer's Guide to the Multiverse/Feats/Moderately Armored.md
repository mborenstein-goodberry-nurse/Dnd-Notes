Source: Player's Handbook

_Prerequisite: Proficiency with [[Light Armor]]_

You have trained to master the use of [[Medium Armor]] and [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Equipment/Shield|Shield]], gaining the following benefits:

- Increase your [[Strength]] or [[Dexterity]] score by 1, to a maximum of 20.

- You gain proficiency with [[Medium Armor]] and [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Equipment/Shield|Shields]].
