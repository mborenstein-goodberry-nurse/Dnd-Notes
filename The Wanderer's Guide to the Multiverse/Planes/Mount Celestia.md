# Mount Celestia

**Nature:** A sacred mountain of ascent, virtue, and disciplined compassion.  
**Typical inhabitants:** Celestials, devoted petitioners, and champions of righteous causes.

## Overview

Mount Celestia rises through seven heavenly layers, each expressing ideals of justice, goodness, and spiritual growth. A pilgrimage here is as much a test of character as a physical climb. Gleaming heights, solemn guardians, and places of contemplation evoke a paradise guided by duty rather than ease alone.

## For Travelers

A traveler may find refuge and wisdom, but kindness does not mean that every request is granted. The plane values truthful intentions and the difficult work of doing good. Its position in the Great Wheel places it between [[Arcadia]] and [[Bytopia]].
