# Nine Hells

**Nature:** Nine infernal layers governed by iron hierarchy, ambition, and binding bargains.  
**Typical inhabitants:** Devils, archdevils, damned souls, and infernal servants.

## Overview

The Nine Hells are a structured dominion of punishment, authority, and calculated evil. Each of the nine layers has its own character and rulers within an infernal order built on rank, obligation, and the pursuit of power. Unlike the demons of the [[Abyss]], devils use procedure and contracts as readily as armies.

## For Travelers

A clever traveler can sometimes negotiate passage or aid, but every provision deserves scrutiny. A bargain that seems perfectly reasonable may be designed to cost far more than it grants. The Hells lie between [[Gehenna]] and [[Acheron]], and [[Infernal]] is their signature language.
