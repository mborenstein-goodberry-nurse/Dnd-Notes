# Plane of Air

**Nature:** A boundless sky of winds, cloud-seas, and floating islands.  
**Typical inhabitants:** Air elementals, djinn, and creatures that thrive on wind and flight.

## Overview

The Plane of Air is an immense aerial world where there may be no reliable ground beneath a traveler's feet. Cloud banks, storms, and wandering masses of stone or other material interrupt the open sky. Near regions touched by other elements, islands and settlements can exist amid the winds; deeper realms are dominated by air itself.

## For Travelers

Flight or an equally dependable means of travel is extremely useful. Sudden gusts, impossible heights, and violent storms pose obvious hazards, while a change in wind can separate a party from its intended destination. The language [[Auran]] is associated with the realm.
