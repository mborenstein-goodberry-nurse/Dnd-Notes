# Astral Plane

**Nature:** An immense silver expanse associated with thought, spirit, and journeys to divine realms.  
**Typical inhabitants:** Astral travelers, gith, and creatures that roam between the Outer Planes.

## Overview

The Astral Plane is frequently imagined as a nearly weightless sea of silver light, with distant motes and drifting fragments breaking its vast emptiness. It offers passage toward the [[Planes#Outer Planes|Outer Planes]] in the broader cosmology, though it does not follow the familiar geography of the mortal world. Thought and intention seem closer to the substance of travel here than roads or maps.

## For Travelers

The [[Astral Projection]] spell sends an astral form away from its resting body, traditionally joined to that body by a silver cord. Portals and color pools can lead to other planes. A traveler should expect unusual movement, strange encounters, and a journey measured by more than ordinary distance.
