# Bytopia

**Nature:** A paradise of honest effort, community, and meaningful work.  
**Typical inhabitants:** Good-aligned souls, celestial visitors, artisans, and farmers.

## Overview

Bytopia is known for two facing layers, as though two immense landscapes were suspended above one another. Mountains, forests, farms, and workshops embody the satisfaction of building something worthwhile and sharing its benefits. Its beauty is less that of a royal palace than of a flourishing village where everyone contributes.

## For Travelers

Visitors often discover that the rewards of the realm are inseparable from labor, generosity, and mutual aid. Its ideals combine goodness with responsibility and place it between [[Mount Celestia]] and [[Elysium]] in the Great Wheel.
