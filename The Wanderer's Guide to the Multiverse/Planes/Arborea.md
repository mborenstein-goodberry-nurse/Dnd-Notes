# Arborea

**Nature:** A realm of beauty, strong feeling, and fiercely celebrated freedom.  
**Typical inhabitants:** Eladrin, celestial beings, and spirits of revelry and inspiration.

## Overview

Arborea is filled with dramatic landscapes, wild forests, crashing seas, and glorious revels. Joy, anger, art, love, and rivalry can all be felt more keenly here, as though the plane gives every emotion a voice. Its grandeur is generous and alive, but rarely quiet or predictable.

## For Travelers

An invitation to a feast can be as consequential as a challenge to a duel. Those who respect the freedom and feelings of others fare better than those who seek to tame the realm. Arborea neighbors [[Beastlands]] and [[Ysgard]].
