# Plane of Earth

**Nature:** An enormous realm of stone, crystal, pressure, and buried riches.  
**Typical inhabitants:** Earth elementals, dao, and creatures that tunnel through solid rock.

## Overview

On the Plane of Earth, vast expanses of rock replace the open horizons of familiar worlds. Caverns, veins of crystal, seams of metal, and shifting faults create routes through an otherwise impenetrable domain. Border regions can contain more recognizable terrain, but farther into the plane there is little relief from stone and crushing weight.

## For Travelers

Finding a safe passage is the chief challenge. Travelers may need tunneling magic, guides, or carefully maintained portals, and underground routes can change without warning. The elemental dialect [[Terran]] is closely associated with this plane.
