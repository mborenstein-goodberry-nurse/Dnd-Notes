# Abyss

**Nature:** A seemingly bottomless collection of demonic realms and unrestrained destruction.  
**Typical inhabitants:** Demons, demon lords, and creatures corrupted by abyssal power.

## Overview

The Abyss is not a single wasteland but a multitude of horrific layers, each capable of reflecting the whims and cruelties of a demon lord. One realm may be a poisoned swamp, another a ruin-strewn battlefield, and another a place whose very geography seems to hate its visitors. Chaos and malice define the plane more than any shared terrain.

## For Travelers

No bargain with a demon should be mistaken for security. Routes between layers can be treacherous, and a place that offers refuge may conceal a worse peril. The Abyss sits between [[Pandemonium]] and [[Carceri]]. Its language is [[Abyssal]].
