# Pandemonium

**Nature:** A labyrinth of darkness and unending, maddening winds.  
**Typical inhabitants:** Exiles, fiends, and creatures accustomed to tunnels and relentless noise.

## Overview

Pandemonium is a network of enormous caverns and twisting passages scoured by winds that never seem to fall silent. The howling can drown out conversation and wear down a traveler's resolve. Its darkness hides both vast empty chambers and dangerous beings who have retreated into the depths.

## For Travelers

Finding shelter and keeping companions together can be as important as defeating hostile inhabitants. The plane's very atmosphere is oppressive, without requiring any single monster to be present. It lies between [[Limbo]] and [[Abyss]].
