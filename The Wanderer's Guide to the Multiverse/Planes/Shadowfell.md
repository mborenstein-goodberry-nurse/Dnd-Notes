# Shadowfell

**Nature:** A dim echo of the mortal world, haunted by loss, silence, and lingering dread.  
**Typical inhabitants:** Undead, shadow creatures, and beings adapted to the plane's gloom.

## Overview

The Shadowfell resembles a faded and unsettling reflection of places on the [[Material Plane]]. A thriving town might appear as abandoned ruins; a healthy forest may become a maze of leafless trees. Muted colors, oppressive skies, and a sense of exhaustion lend the plane a reputation for sorrow and foreboding.

## For Travelers

Shadow crossings are sometimes found in tombs, battlefields, or other places marked by death. The landscape can be physically dangerous, but despair and the temptation to surrender hope are just as memorable as any monster. Individual campaign settings may give the plane additional domains or rulers.
