# Acheron

**Nature:** An endless theater of regimented war and grinding conflict.  
**Typical inhabitants:** Soldiers, warlike spirits, and other beings bound to conflict.

## Overview

Acheron is often imagined as a vast battlefield of enormous iron cubes drifting through empty space. Armies march across their surfaces while the clash of weapons echoes between colliding masses of metal. Here, order is not peaceful; it is the discipline of command, formation, and relentless struggle.

## For Travelers

Avoiding the path of an army can be more important than winning a skirmish. Travelers face militarized territories, hazardous crossings, and commanders who see strangers as potential recruits or enemies. Acheron stands between [[Nine Hells]] and [[Mechanus]].
