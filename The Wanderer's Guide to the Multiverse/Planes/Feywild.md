# Feywild

**Nature:** An enchanted echo of the mortal world, where wonder and peril share the same path.  
**Typical inhabitants:** Fey, archfey and their courts, and other creatures drawn into faerie realms.

## Overview

The Feywild reflects familiar landscapes through a lens of heightened beauty and strange emotion. Forests may grow more vibrant, rivers may sparkle with impossible colors, and the border between a celebration and a deadly bargain can be perilously thin. Fey courts, ancient pacts, and stories older than mortal kingdoms shape much of the plane.

## For Travelers

Fey crossings sometimes connect particular sites in the [[Material Plane]] to their uncanny counterparts. Customs and promises matter, and what seems like a trivial gift or an innocent invitation may carry unexpected weight. Time and memory can behave strangely in some Feywild adventures, at the DM's discretion.
