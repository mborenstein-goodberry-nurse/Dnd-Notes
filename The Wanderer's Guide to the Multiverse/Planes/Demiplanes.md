# Demiplanes

**Nature:** Small realities whose boundaries and laws can be radically unlike those of larger planes.  
**Typical inhabitants:** Varies: inhabitants may be nonexistent, imprisoned, or entirely unique.

## Overview

A demiplane is a bounded extradimensional realm with its own characteristics. Some are formed by powerful magic; others are remnants of larger realities, sealed sanctuaries, or strange accidents of creation. A demiplane might contain a single chamber, a secluded estate, or an environment whose limits defy normal geography.

## For Travelers

Access is often deliberately restricted. Certain demiplanes can be reached by portals or magic, including the [[Demiplane]] and [[Gate]] spells in appropriate circumstances. Because each realm has its own origin, travelers should not assume that the same physical laws, inhabitants, or exits apply to two different demiplanes.
