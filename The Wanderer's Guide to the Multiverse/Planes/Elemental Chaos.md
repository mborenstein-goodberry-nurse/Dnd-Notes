# Elemental Chaos

**Nature:** The unstable reaches where the primal elements collide and transform.  
**Typical inhabitants:** Powerful elementals and creatures able to survive violent elemental upheaval.

## Overview

Beyond the more recognizable edges of the four Elemental Planes lies a tumult in which fire, water, air, and earth are mixed and torn apart. Mountains may splinter into storms, oceans may boil against islands of magma, and the landscape can be rebuilt by forces that do not obey mortal expectations.

## For Travelers

The Elemental Chaos is less a destination with fixed borders than a reminder that elemental reality can be unpredictable. Movement, shelter, and even the shape of the environment can change abruptly. The four elemental dialects of [[Primordial]] echo the powers that contend here.
