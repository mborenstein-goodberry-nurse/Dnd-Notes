# Negative Plane

**Nature:** A source of consuming darkness and life-draining power.  
**Typical inhabitants:** Few living inhabitants; undead and necrotic forces are often associated with it.

## Overview

The Negative Plane embodies a force that diminishes vitality and draws warmth and life away. It is commonly associated with necrotic energy, deathly stillness, and the power that can animate undead creatures. Where its influence seeps into other realms, living places may seem drained of color and strength.

## For Travelers

This is not simply another gloomy country to explore. In many interpretations, the plane is fundamentally hostile to living creatures, and surviving direct exposure requires extraordinary protection. Its destructive aspect contrasts with the radiant abundance of the [[Positive Plane]].
