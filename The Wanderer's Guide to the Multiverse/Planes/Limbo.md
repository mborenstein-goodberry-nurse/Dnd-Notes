# Limbo

**Nature:** A sea of raw possibility where form is temporary and will can shape matter.  
**Typical inhabitants:** Slaadi, githzerai, and creatures accustomed to unstable reality.

## Overview

Limbo has no dependable landscape. Fire, stone, water, and countless other substances whirl together and separate again, forming fleeting islands of order amid endless change. Beings with exceptional mental discipline can sometimes impose a temporary shape on this chaos.

## For Travelers

Travelers who rely on fixed landmarks may lose their way almost immediately. Concentration, allies, and secure refuges can matter more than provisions. Limbo lies between [[Ysgard]] and [[Pandemonium]] and is associated with the strange culture of the githzerai.
