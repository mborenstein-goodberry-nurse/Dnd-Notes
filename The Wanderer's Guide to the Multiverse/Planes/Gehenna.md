# Gehenna

**Nature:** A treacherous realm of steep volcanic slopes and calculated cruelty.  
**Typical inhabitants:** Yugoloths, fiends, and ambitious schemers.

## Overview

Gehenna is famous for mountainous, volcanic landscapes with little level ground. Paths cling to enormous slopes, settlements survive in precarious places, and the threat of falling may be constant. Its harsh surroundings suit a realm where self-interest and merciless bargains flourish.

## For Travelers

Travelers should plan for unstable terrain as carefully as for hostile negotiations. A promised service may be given only at the price of another, less visible obligation. Gehenna stands between [[Hades]] and [[Nine Hells]].
