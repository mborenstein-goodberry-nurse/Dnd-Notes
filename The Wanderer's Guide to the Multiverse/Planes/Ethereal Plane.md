# Ethereal Plane

**Nature:** An unseen, mist-filled passage beside the world and between the Inner Planes.  
**Typical inhabitants:** Ethereal wanderers, ghosts, and creatures capable of entering the mists.

## Overview

The Ethereal Plane lies alongside the [[Material Plane]] and the Inner Planes. In the Border Ethereal, a traveler can perceive a ghostly version of the nearby world while remaining separated from most ordinary physical interactions. Farther out, the Deep Ethereal becomes a rolling expanse of colored mist and strange passages.

## For Travelers

Spells such as [[Etherealness]] can bring travelers into this realm. Some effects, particularly those involving magical force, can interact with the Border Ethereal despite its separation from the Material Plane. The mists also provide routes toward other parts of the multiverse, although navigation is rarely straightforward.
