# Arcadia

**Nature:** A landscape of harmony, cooperation, and carefully tended order.  
**Typical inhabitants:** Orderly communities, celestial beings, and spirits drawn to shared ideals.

## Overview

Arcadia is a realm of cultivated fields, symmetrical orchards, broad avenues, and communities striving for collective well-being. It offers peace through cooperation, duty, and dependable institutions. Its beauty is measured and deliberate rather than wild or spontaneous.

## For Travelers

The question for a traveler is often where cooperation ends and conformity begins. Visitors who challenge established order may find polite resistance instead of overt hostility. In the Great Wheel, Arcadia stands between [[Mechanus]] and [[Mount Celestia]].
