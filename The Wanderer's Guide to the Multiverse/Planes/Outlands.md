# Outlands

**Nature:** A balanced crossroads for the ideals and powers of the Outer Planes.  
**Typical inhabitants:** Travelers from many planes, petitioners, merchants, and planar intermediaries.

## Overview

The Outlands occupy a distinctive place in the Great Wheel: a neutral realm surrounded by the other Outer Planes. Its distant communities and gate-towns are associated with portals leading toward neighboring realms of law, chaos, good, and evil. At its center rises the Spire, above which the extraordinary city of Sigil is often described as floating.

## For Travelers

The Outlands reward curiosity, negotiation, and an understanding that different philosophies can meet without agreeing. Sigil, the City of Doors, is an important planar destination but is a city rather than a separate plane. The exact routes and traditions of its gateways can vary between settings.
