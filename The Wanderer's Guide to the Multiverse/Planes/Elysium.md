# Elysium

**Nature:** A land of deep peace, compassion, and refuge.  
**Typical inhabitants:** Benevolent spirits, celestial beings, and souls seeking rest.

## Overview

Elysium embodies goodness without a need to demand strict order or complete freedom. Gentle rivers, welcoming valleys, and quiet communities offer relief from struggle. The legendary River Oceanus winds through its pastoral realms, carrying travelers across places where generosity comes more naturally than suspicion.

## For Travelers

The greatest danger for a restless adventurer may be a reluctance to depart. A peaceful life here can feel more compelling than any unfinished quest. Elysium lies between [[Bytopia]] and [[Beastlands]] on the Great Wheel.
