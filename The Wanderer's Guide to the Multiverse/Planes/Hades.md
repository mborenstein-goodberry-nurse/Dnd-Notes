# Hades

**Nature:** A colorless realm that slowly erodes hope and purpose.  
**Typical inhabitants:** Yugoloths, lost souls, and other beings shaped by despair.

## Overview

Hades, also known as the Gray Waste, is defined by bleak expanses in which color, enthusiasm, and even the desire to act seem to fade. Its landscapes can include lifeless fields, harsh settlements, and roads used by mercenary fiends. Evil here is not always loud or furious; it can be indifferent and exhausting.

## For Travelers

A traveler needs more than weapons to endure the Waste. Companionship, purpose, and a reason to keep moving may be as valuable as provisions. Hades lies between [[Carceri]] and [[Gehenna]].
