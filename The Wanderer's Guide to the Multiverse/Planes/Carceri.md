# Carceri

**Nature:** A prison-plane of betrayal, confinement, and seemingly hopeless escape.  
**Typical inhabitants:** Exiles, imprisoned spirits, fiends, and jailers.

## Overview

Carceri is commonly depicted as a sequence of isolated, prisonlike realms where distance itself can become a sentence. Bleak landscapes, far-separated spheres, and hostile captors make even a successful escape from a cell feel like the first step in a larger imprisonment. Betrayal and suspicion shape the realm's reputation.

## For Travelers

A way into Carceri is often far easier to find than a way out. Trust, freedom, and passage are precious currencies for those trapped there. The plane lies between [[Abyss]] and [[Hades]] on the Great Wheel.
