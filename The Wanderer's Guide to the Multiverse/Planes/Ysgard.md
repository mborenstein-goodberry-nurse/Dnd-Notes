# Ysgard

**Nature:** A realm of valor, contests, and stories told around roaring fires.  
**Typical inhabitants:** Heroic spirits, warriors, giants, and beings drawn to glorious challenges.

## Overview

Ysgard is a plane of soaring mountains, immense halls, and rugged landscapes where bravery and the joy of struggle are held in high esteem. Floating earthbergs and legendary battlefields give its scenery the scale of epic poetry. In many stories, warriors who fall in honorable contests rise again to celebrate their deeds.

## For Travelers

The plane can reward boldness while punishing recklessness. Not every battle is a matter of hatred: rivalry, fellowship, and the pursuit of a worthy challenge are central to its character. Ysgard sits between [[Arborea]] and [[Limbo]].
