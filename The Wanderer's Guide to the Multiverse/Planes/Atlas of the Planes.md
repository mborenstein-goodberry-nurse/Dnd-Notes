# Atlas of the Planes

*There are roads that lead to cities, doors that lead to kingdoms, and a few particularly ill-advised thresholds that lead out of reality altogether.*

This atlas collects the best-known planes of existence in the **D&D 5e Great Wheel** tradition. It is a setting-neutral reference: a particular campaign may omit, rename, rearrange, or reinterpret any of these realms. For the broader explanation of planar travel and cosmology, see [[Planes]].

## Mortal Worlds and Their Echoes

- [[Material Plane]] — the many worlds where ordinary adventures begin
- [[Feywild]] — a radiant, dangerous reflection of the mortal world
- [[Shadowfell]] — its dim and haunting counterpart

## Transitive Planes

- [[Ethereal Plane]] — mist-shrouded passages beside the world
- [[Astral Plane]] — a silver realm of mind and spirit

## Inner and Elemental Planes

- [[Plane of Air]] — endless skies and storm-tossed islands
- [[Plane of Earth]] — stone, crystal, pressure, and caverns
- [[Plane of Fire]] — burning seas and volcanic kingdoms
- [[Plane of Water]] — immeasurable oceans and shifting currents
- [[Elemental Chaos]] — the turbulent meeting of the elements

## Fundamental Energies and Other Realms

- [[Positive Plane]] — overwhelming radiance and vitality
- [[Negative Plane]] — necrotic power and consuming emptiness
- [[Demiplanes]] — small, self-contained realities with their own rules

## Common Ways Between Planes

- [[Plane Shift]] — direct travel to another plane
- [[Gate]] — opens a passage to another plane
- [[Astral Projection]] — astral journeys beyond the body
- [[Etherealness]] — entry into the Ethereal Plane
- [[Demiplane]] — access to an extradimensional chamber

> [!note] For the Dungeon Master
> These are descriptions, **not extra game mechanics**. Planar hazards, time effects, portals, and traversal methods should follow your campaign's chosen rules. The Outer Planes are presented in the classic Great Wheel arrangement; other settings can use a different cosmology.
