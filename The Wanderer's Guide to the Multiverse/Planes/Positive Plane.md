# Positive Plane

**Nature:** A source of radiant vitality and the raw force of life.  
**Typical inhabitants:** Few ordinary travelers; beings associated with life and radiant power may be linked to it.

## Overview

The Positive Plane represents life-giving energy in its most concentrated form. Rather than a familiar landscape, it is often described as an overwhelming radiance that nourishes growth and vitality across the multiverse. Its influence may be reflected in healing magic and in places where life flourishes beyond reasonable limits.

## For Travelers

Unfiltered life energy need not be safe merely because it is life-giving. A visit can be as hazardous as exposure to any other fundamental force, and the precise effects depend on the campaign's rules and cosmology. It stands in thematic opposition to the [[Negative Plane]].
