# Material Plane

**Nature:** The crossroads of mortal lives, where matter, magic, and competing cosmic forces meet.  
**Typical inhabitants:** Mortals and the creatures of ordinary campaign worlds.

## Overview

The Material Plane encompasses the many worlds where most adventures begin. Its lands can be remarkably different from one setting to another: a bustling city, an isolated wilderness, or an entire planet scarred by ancient magic can all belong to the same broad category of existence. Unlike the more extreme planes, it is not governed by a single element or moral principle.

## For Travelers

Other planes often cast reflections into the Material Plane. Fey crossings, places touched by shadow, elemental rifts, and ancient portals can place extraordinary destinations only a step away from familiar ground. The exact arrangement of worlds and their connections is a choice for each campaign setting.
