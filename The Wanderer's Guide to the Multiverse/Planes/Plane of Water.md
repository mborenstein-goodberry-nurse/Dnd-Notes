# Plane of Water

**Nature:** A world-spanning ocean, with depths and currents beyond mortal seas.  
**Typical inhabitants:** Water elementals, marids, and aquatic creatures.

## Overview

The Plane of Water is dominated by a seemingly endless sea. Its border regions may contain islands, pockets of air, and the ruins or strongholds of aquatic civilizations, but much of the plane is open water without a dependable surface or seabed. Underwater currents can carry travelers into enormous, unfamiliar expanses.

## For Travelers

Breathing underwater, moving against powerful currents, and keeping a sense of direction are central challenges. The plane is not necessarily uniformly hostile, but its scale rewards careful preparation. [[Aquan]] is the dialect of [[Primordial]] most associated with its inhabitants.
