# Mechanus

**Nature:** A cosmic machine of perfect order and relentless regularity.  
**Typical inhabitants:** Modrons and beings devoted to law, patterns, and precise systems.

## Overview

Mechanus is a world of vast interlocking gears whose motions reflect an ideal of universal order. Structures, paths, and societies can function with astonishing precision. Its most famous inhabitants, the modrons, are associated with routines and hierarchies that appear baffling to visitors accustomed to improvisation.

## For Travelers

Rules here are not mere customs; they can feel like the organizing principle of reality. Travelers may prosper by understanding procedures and keeping agreements, but careless disruptions can have disproportionate consequences. Mechanus lies between [[Acheron]] and [[Arcadia]].
