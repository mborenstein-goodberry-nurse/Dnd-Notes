# Beastlands

**Nature:** Untamed wilderness in which the natural world is exalted.  
**Typical inhabitants:** Beasts, awakened or intelligent animals, and nature-aligned spirits.

## Overview

The Beastlands is a realm of immense forests, rivers, and open wilds where nature takes precedence over cities and kingdoms. Animals may be unusually perceptive or able to speak, and its environments express the vitality of the hunt, the seasons, and life beyond cultivated walls.

## For Travelers

A visitor who treats the wilderness only as a resource may quickly become unwelcome. Respect for its creatures and rhythms is a better guide than any road. This Outer Plane stands between [[Elysium]] and [[Arborea]].
