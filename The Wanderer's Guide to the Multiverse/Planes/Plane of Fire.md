# Plane of Fire

**Nature:** A realm of flame, smoke, molten landscapes, and relentless heat.  
**Typical inhabitants:** Fire elementals, efreet, and other creatures at home in flames.

## Overview

The Plane of Fire is often pictured as a land of burning skies, seas of fire, and rivers of molten stone. Its more habitable border regions may sustain settlements, trade routes, and the famous City of Brass, while distant reaches become increasingly hostile to life that cannot withstand elemental fire.

## For Travelers

Heat and exposure are immediate concerns. Safe passage generally demands powerful protection, suitable magic, or help from beings native to the plane. Efreet and other powerful residents can offer assistance, but their bargains may create dangers of their own. [[Ignan]] is the elemental dialect of fire.
