# Elvish

**Typical speakers:** [[Elf|Elves]] and members of some communities closely connected to them  
**Script:** Elvish

Elvish is the ancestral language of elves, preserved through stories, songs, scholarship, and long-lived cultural traditions. Its spoken and written forms may vary between elven peoples and settings.

The Elvish script is also used for [[Sylvan]] and [[Undercommon]] in the core D&D language tables. These remain distinct languages, even when their writing looks familiar.
