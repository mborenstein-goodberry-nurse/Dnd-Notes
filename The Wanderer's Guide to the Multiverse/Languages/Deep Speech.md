# Deep Speech

**Typical speakers:** Certain aberrations, including aboleths and cloakers  
**Script:** No standard script listed in the core language tables

Deep Speech is an alien language associated with aberrant creatures and the strange places beyond ordinary mortal experience. Adventurers might encounter it in communications among such beings or in writings tied to unsettling, otherworldly knowledge.

Not every aberration speaks Deep Speech, and its lack of a standard listed script does not prevent a creature or cult from recording it using magical symbols or another writing system.
