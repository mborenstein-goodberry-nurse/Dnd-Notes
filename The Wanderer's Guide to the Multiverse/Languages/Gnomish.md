# Gnomish

**Typical speakers:** Gnomes, including [[Deep Gnome|deep gnomes]]  
**Script:** Dwarvish

Gnomish is the traditional language of gnomes, used in their communities, stories, and records. It may also be found in writings about inventions, craftsmanship, and the varied interests of gnomish scholars and artisans.

Gnomish uses the Dwarvish script, though it is a separate language from [[Dwarvish]] and cannot be understood simply by recognizing its letters.
