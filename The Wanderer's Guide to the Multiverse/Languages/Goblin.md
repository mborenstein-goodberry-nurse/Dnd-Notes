# Goblin

**Typical speakers:** Goblinoids, including [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Races/Goblin|goblins]], [[Hobgoblin|hobgoblins]], and [[Bugbear|bugbears]]  
**Script:** Dwarvish

Goblin is a common language among goblinoid peoples. It can help different goblinoid groups communicate, despite differences in their customs, social organization, and ways of life.

Goblin is commonly written using the Dwarvish script, but remains distinct from [[Dwarvish]] as a spoken language.
