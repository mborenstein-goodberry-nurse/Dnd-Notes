# Infernal

**Typical speakers:** Devils and some creatures tied to the Nine Hells, including many [[Tiefling|tieflings]]  
**Script:** Infernal

Infernal is the language most strongly associated with devils and the Nine Hells. It may be encountered in infernal names, contracts, correspondence, and rituals, particularly where devils bargain with mortals.

[[Abyssal]] uses the Infernal writing system as well, but the two languages are distinct. Being able to read Infernal letters does not by itself reveal the meaning of an Abyssal text.
