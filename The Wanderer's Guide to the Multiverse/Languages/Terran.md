# Terran

**Typical speakers:** Earth elementals and other creatures associated with elemental earth  
**Script:** Dwarvish (the script used for [[Primordial]])  
**Language family:** [[Primordial]]

Terran is the earth-associated dialect of [[Primordial]], traditionally connected to the Elemental Plane of Earth. It may be heard among earth elementals and other beings tied to stone, soil, and the depths of elemental realms.

**Mutual understanding:** Creatures speaking Terran can communicate with speakers of [[Aquan]], [[Auran]], and [[Ignan]], the other Primordial dialects.
