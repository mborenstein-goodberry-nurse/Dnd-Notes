# Gith

**Typical speakers:** [[Githyanki]] and [[Githzerai]]  
**Script:** Tir'su

Gith is the shared language of the githyanki and githzerai, peoples whose histories diverged after escaping enslavement by mind flayers. Both groups speak their own dialects, reflecting separate cultures and longstanding disagreements, but can generally understand one another.

Its distinctive writing system, **tir'su**, arranges letters in circular patterns. Githyanki and githzerai traditions differ in how they arrange those symbols, making even written language a sign of their cultural divide.
