# Auran

**Typical speakers:** Air elementals and other creatures associated with elemental air  
**Script:** Dwarvish (the script used for [[Primordial]])  
**Language family:** [[Primordial]]

Auran is the air-associated dialect of [[Primordial]], traditionally connected to the Elemental Plane of Air. It is used by air elementals and may be encountered among other beings native to airy or wind-swept elemental realms, including some [[Aarakocra]].

**Mutual understanding:** Creatures speaking Auran can communicate with speakers of [[Aquan]], [[Ignan]], and [[Terran]], the other Primordial dialects.
