# Draconic

**Typical speakers:** Dragons, [[Dragonborn]], and some other draconic creatures, such as [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Races/Kobold|kobolds]]  
**Script:** Draconic

Draconic is the language of dragons and many creatures connected to them by ancestry or culture. It is associated with ancient dragon traditions and appears in draconic names, records, and lore.

Scholars and spellcasters may study Draconic to read writings preserved by dragons or by people who learned from them. Its use is not limited to dragons alone.
