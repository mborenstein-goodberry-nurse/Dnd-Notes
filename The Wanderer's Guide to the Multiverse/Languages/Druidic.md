# Druidic

**Typical speakers:** [[Druid|Druids]] and others explicitly taught their secret language  
**Script:** Hidden signs and messages; no standard alphabet specified

Druidic is the secret language of druids, passed among those who follow druidic traditions. Beyond speech, it can convey hidden messages that trained readers recognize but outsiders may overlook. A carved mark, carefully arranged symbol, or other subtle sign can carry meaning to someone who knows the language.

## Rules (2014 D&D 5e)

You know Druidic, the secret language of druids. You can speak the language and use it to leave hidden messages. You and others who know this language automatically spot such a message. Others spot the message's presence with a successful DC 15 Wisdom (Perception) check but can't decipher it without magic.
