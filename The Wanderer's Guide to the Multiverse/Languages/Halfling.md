# Halfling

**Typical speakers:** [[The Wanderer's Guide to the Multiverse/Races/Halfling|Halflings]]  
**Script:** Common

Halfling is the traditional language of halfling communities, spoken among families and passed down through local stories and customs. Its importance can be easy to overlook because many halflings also speak [[Common]] when dealing with outsiders.

Halfling uses the Common writing system, but is a language in its own right rather than a dialect of Common.
