# Primordial

**Typical speakers:** Elementals and other creatures native to or influenced by the Elemental Planes  
**Script:** Dwarvish

Primordial is an ancient language family associated with the elemental forces that shape the multiverse. In D&D, it includes four elemental dialects:

- [[Aquan]] — water
- [[Auran]] — air
- [[Ignan]] — fire
- [[Terran]] — earth

**Mutual understanding:** Creatures that speak different Primordial dialects can communicate with one another. The dialects are associated with different elemental realms, but are not treated as completely separate languages for this purpose.
