# Sylvan

**Typical speakers:** Fey creatures, including many inhabitants of the Feywild  
**Script:** Elvish

Sylvan is the language of many fey beings and is closely associated with the wonders, customs, and dangers of the Feywild. Travelers may encounter it in conversations with fey creatures, inscriptions in enchanted places, or tales passed between those who dwell beyond the mortal world.

Sylvan is written with the Elvish script, but it is distinct from [[Elvish]]. Knowing one does not automatically grant fluency in the other.
