### Astral Shard

*Wondrous item, rare (requires attunement by a sorcerer)*

A crystal infused with the essence of the Astral Plane. It can be affixed to a Tiny object or carried as a spellcasting focus while held or worn.

When the wielder uses a Metamagic option while casting a spell, the shard allows the wielder to teleport immediately after the spell is cast to an unoccupied space they can see within **30 feet**.

*Source: Tasha's Cauldron of Everything, p. 120 (2014-era D&D 5e).*
