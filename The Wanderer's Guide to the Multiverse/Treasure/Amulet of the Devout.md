### Amulet of the Devout

*Wondrous item, uncommon (+1), rare (+2), or very rare (+3) (requires attunement by a cleric or paladin)*

A jeweled holy symbol that enhances spellcasting. While worn, it grants a bonus to spell attack rolls and spell saving throw DCs, determined by the item's version:

| Version | Rarity | Bonus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| +1 | Uncommon | +1 |
| **+2** | **Rare** | **+2** |
| +3 | Very rare | +3 |

The wearer may also use **Channel Divinity** once without consuming a normal use of that feature. This extra use becomes available again at the next dawn.

*Source: Tasha's Cauldron of Everything, p. 119 (2014-era D&D 5e).*
