# Truesight

**Category:** Special sense

Truesight is a supernatural form of perception that reveals realities concealed from ordinary eyes. It is always limited to the range specified by the creature, ability, or spell granting it.

## What It Reveals

Within that range, a creature with truesight can:

- See through ordinary and magical darkness.
- Perceive invisible creatures and objects.
- Recognize visual illusions for what they are and automatically succeed on saving throws against those illusions.
- Perceive the true form of a shapechanger or a creature altered by magic.
- See into the [[Ethereal Plane]].

Truesight is not X-ray vision: it does not, by itself, grant the ability to look through solid walls, see unlimited distances, or know every secret.

## In Play

The spell [[True Seeing]] grants truesight for a limited duration. Some other spells, such as [[Blur]] and [[Mirror Image]], specify how truesight changes their effects.
