# Blindsight

**Category:** Special sense

Blindsight allows a creature to perceive its surroundings within a stated radius without relying on ordinary vision. The mechanism varies: a bat might use sound, while a dragon or an elemental might sense its environment in a very different way.

## How It Works

- **Range matters.** A creature perceives its surroundings this way only within the distance specified in its feature or stat block.
- **Sight is not required.** Darkness, invisibility, and other effects that interfere specifically with normal sight do not necessarily prevent the creature from perceiving nearby targets through blindsight.
- **It is not omniscience.** Blindsight does not automatically allow perception through solid barriers or into another plane, and it does not grant every ability associated with [[Truesight]].
- **Specific limitations still apply.** A feature based on echolocation, for example, may fail when the creature is deafened, if its description says so.

## In Play

Spells such as [[Blur]] and [[Mirror Image]] explicitly account for creatures able to perceive without sight. Check the individual feature or spell to determine how the interaction works.
