# Unarmed Strikes

**Category:** Combat rule

An unarmed strike is an attack made without a weapon: a punch, kick, headbutt, elbow, horn strike, or some other bodily blow. It counts as a **melee weapon attack** under the 2014 rules, but the body part used is not itself a weapon.

## Standard Rules (2014)

- **Attack roll:** Make a melee attack using Strength, adding proficiency bonus because all characters are proficient with unarmed strikes.
- **Damage on a hit:** 1 + Strength modifier [[Bludgeoning|bludgeoning damage]], unless another feature provides a different calculation.
- **Reach:** Normally 5 feet, unless a trait or feature modifies it.
- **Special features:** The [[Monk]] class, fighting styles, and certain species traits can alter the damage or other properties of an unarmed strike.

A [[Satyr]], for example, can use its horns to make unarmed strikes and uses its Ram trait's damage instead of the standard amount.

### Important Distinction

An unarmed strike is a melee weapon **attack**, but is not usually an attack **with a weapon**. Features that require an actual weapon may therefore behave differently; always check their exact wording.

*Rules basis: 2014 D&D 5e. The 2024 rules handle unarmed strikes differently.*
