# Ghoul

**Creature type:** Undead  
**Size:** Medium  
**Challenge rating:** 1  
**Armor Class:** 12  
**Hit Points:** 22  
**Speed:** 30 ft.

## Description

Ghouls are ravenous undead that haunt graves, ruined crypts, battlefields, and other places where the dead are left undisturbed. Unlike a shambling zombie, a ghoul moves with disturbing speed and purpose, lunging at living prey with jagged teeth and filthy claws. Hunger often drives its behavior, though necromancy can bind it to a master's will.

## Abilities and Behavior

**Notable traits:** A ghoul can see in darkness, is immune to poison damage, and cannot be charmed, exhausted, or poisoned. Its most feared attack is its **claws**, which can paralyze a creature that fails a DC 10 Constitution saving throw; a creature can repeat that save at the end of each of its turns. **Elves and undead are exempt from the claws' paralysis effect** under the 2014 rules.

A paralyzed target is particularly vulnerable to nearby attackers, making a group of ghouls dangerous even to a well-armed party. See [[Paralyzed]].

## Adventurer's Notes

The 6th-level spell [[Create Undead]] can animate up to three Medium or Small humanoid corpses as ghouls when cast at night. These ghouls obey the caster's commands for 24 hours; maintaining control requires another casting before that period ends. Higher-level spell slots can create or maintain additional ghouls, or certain stronger undead as specified by the spell.

The creature's statistics do not change just because it was animated by *Create Undead*. For the exact number controlled, spell components, and command rules, consult [[Create Undead]].
