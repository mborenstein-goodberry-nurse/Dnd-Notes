# Chimera

**Creature type:** Monstrosity  
**Size:** Large  
**Challenge rating:** 6  
**Armor Class:** 14 (natural armor)  
**Hit Points:** 114 (12d10 + 48)  
**Speed:** 30 ft., fly 60 ft.

## Description

A chimera is a fierce winged creature whose body combines features of a lion, goat, and dragon, with a head belonging to each. The dragon head can breathe fire while the other heads savage enemies up close.

## Abilities and Behavior

**Three attacks:** Its 2014 Multiattack makes a bite, horn, and claw attack. Each attack is **+7 to hit**; its bite and claws each deal **11 (2d6 + 4)** damage of their respective types, and its horns deal **10 (1d12 + 4) bludgeoning damage**.

**Fire Breath (Recharge 5–6):** The dragon head breathes fire in a **15-foot cone**. Creatures in the area make a **DC 15 Dexterity saving throw**, taking **31 (7d8) fire damage** on failure or half as much on success. When available, the chimera may use this breath in place of its bite or horns during Multiattack.

**Mobility and senses:** A chimera flies and has darkvision, making it dangerous both in open space and in close quarters.

## Adventurer's Notes

Spread out to avoid several allies being caught in its fire breath, and remember that its recharge determines how often the breath can be used. A particular chimera in a campaign may have altered statistics or abilities at the DM's discretion.
