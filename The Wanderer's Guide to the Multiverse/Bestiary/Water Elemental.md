# Water Elemental

**Creature type:** Elemental  
**Size:** Large  
**Challenge rating:** 5  
**Armor Class:** 14  
**Hit Points:** 114  
**Speed:** 30 ft.; swim 90 ft.

## Description

A water elemental is a towering surge of living liquid that can flatten itself into a current or gather into a crushing wave. It may defend sacred springs, coastal ruins, or portals to the [[Plane of Water]].

## Abilities and Behavior

**Notable traits:** Fluid movement through tight spaces, high swimming speed, and a Whelm ability that can engulf and restrain victims, threatening them with drowning.

## Adventurer's Notes

Especially dangerous in flooded chambers or on ships. A [[Circle of the Moon]] druid may eventually adopt this form with Elemental Wild Shape.
