# Earth Elemental

**Creature type:** Elemental  
**Size:** Large  
**Challenge rating:** 5  
**Armor Class:** 17  
**Hit Points:** 126  
**Speed:** 30 ft.; burrow 30 ft.

## Description

An earth elemental is a walking mass of stone, soil, and grinding mineral. It is often slow to change course, yet terrifyingly persistent once roused, breaking obstacles through strength rather than finesse.

## Abilities and Behavior

**Notable traits:** Tough natural armor, tremorsense, movement through unworked earth and stone, and exceptional effectiveness against objects and structures. Associated with the [[Plane of Earth]].

## Adventurer's Notes

Its ability to move through stone and break fortifications can radically alter a dungeon encounter. [[Circle of the Moon]] druids may gain access to this form through Elemental Wild Shape.
