# Crocodile

**Creature type:** Beast  
**Size:** Large  
**Challenge rating:** 1/2  
**Armor Class:** 12  
**Hit Points:** 19  
**Speed:** 20 ft.; swim 30 ft.

## Description

Crocodiles are patient aquatic ambush predators found along rivers, marshes, lakes, and tropical waterways. Their stillness can make them nearly indistinguishable from drifting logs until their jaws close around an unsuspecting victim.

## Abilities and Behavior

**Notable traits:** Can hold its breath for an extended time; its bite can grapple and restrain a target. The creature is considerably more mobile in water than on land.

## Adventurer's Notes

A standard [[Wild Shape]] example for level 4 druids in the 2014 rules; swimming forms become available at that level under the normal progression.
