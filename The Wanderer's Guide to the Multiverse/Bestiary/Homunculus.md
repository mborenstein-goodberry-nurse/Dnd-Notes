# Homunculus

**Creature type:** Construct  
**Size:** Tiny  
**Challenge rating:** 0  
**Armor Class:** 13 (natural armor)  
**Hit Points:** 5  
**Speed:** 20 ft., fly 40 ft.

## Description

A homunculus is a miniature artificial creature shaped through alchemical and magical craft, often resembling a strange winged humanoid with sharp little teeth. Though physically frail, it can serve its creator as a scout, messenger, laboratory assistant, or watchful companion. Its loyalty is reinforced by a supernatural bond rather than by ordinary training.

## Abilities and Behavior

**Notable traits:** A homunculus understands the languages of its creator but cannot speak. Through its **Telepathic Bond**, it can communicate with its master and convey what it senses while both are on the same plane of existence. It has darkvision, immunity to poison damage, and immunity to the charmed and poisoned conditions.

Its bite deals little damage, but its venom can poison a victim. On a badly failed Constitution saving throw, the victim can also fall unconscious for as long as that stronger poisoning effect persists. See [[Poisoned]] and [[Unconscious]].

## Adventurer's Notes

The 6th-level spell [[Create Homunculus]] fashions a homunculus from special materials and the caster's blood. A homunculus made by that spell is a faithful companion, **dies if its creator dies**, and can benefit from a temporary transfer of the caster's vitality after a long rest, subject to the spell's limits. A caster can have only one homunculus created in this way at a time.

The creature's normal statblock begins with 5 hit points; the hit-point changes described in *Create Homunculus* are specific to that spell, not a permanent change to the standard creature statistics. For components, restrictions, and the vitality-sharing procedure, see [[Create Homunculus]].
