# Basilisk

**Creature type:** Monstrosity  
**Size:** Medium  
**Challenge rating:** 3  
**Armor Class:** 15 (natural armor)  
**Hit Points:** 52 (8d8 + 16)  
**Speed:** 20 ft.

## Description

A basilisk is a heavily built, many-legged reptilian monstrosity whose stare can gradually turn a living creature to stone. Although it moves slowly, its dangerous gaze and powerful bite make direct confrontation hazardous.

## Abilities and Behavior

**Petrifying Gaze:** A creature that begins its turn within **30 feet** of the basilisk while they can see each other may be forced to make a **DC 12 Constitution saving throw**. On a failure, it becomes [[Restrained]] as it begins turning to stone. At the end of its next turn, it repeats the save: success ends the effect; failure leaves it [[Petrified]] until cured by [[Greater Restoration]] or another suitable magical effect.

A creature that is not surprised can avert its eyes at the start of its turn to avoid the gaze, but then cannot see the basilisk until the start of its next turn unless it looks back and makes the save. In bright light, a basilisk that sees its own reflection within 30 feet may target itself with its gaze.

**Bite:** A heavy bite deals piercing damage and additional poison damage. In the 2014 stat block, the attack is **+5 to hit**, dealing **10 (2d6 + 3) piercing** plus **7 (2d6) poison** on a hit.

## Adventurer's Notes

The first failed save does **not** cause immediate permanent petrification: it initially restrains the target, with another saving throw at the end of the target's next turn. Looking away can prevent the initial gaze, but makes it harder to fight a creature you cannot see. For the exact gaze wording and creature actions, use the stat block below.
