# Bandit

**Creature type:** Humanoid  
**Size:** Medium  
**Challenge rating:** 1/8  
**Armor Class:** 12  
**Hit Points:** 11  
**Speed:** 30 ft.

## Description

Bandits are opportunistic highway robbers, raiders, smugglers, and other people who survive by force or intimidation. They are not a distinct species: a bandit stat block can represent many different folk, allegiances, and motives.

## Abilities and Behavior

**Notable traits:** Light armor and straightforward melee or ranged attacks. An individual bandit is a relatively minor threat; a prepared group with cover or terrain advantage can be far more troublesome.

## Adventurer's Notes

Useful in ambushes and road encounters, particularly alongside the [[Surprise]] rules. Bandits can bargain, flee, or surrender, unlike many mindless monsters.
