# Zombie

**Creature type:** Undead  
**Size:** Medium  
**Challenge rating:** 1/4  
**Armor Class:** 8  
**Hit Points:** 22  
**Speed:** 20 ft.

## Description

A zombie is a corpse animated by necromantic power rather than restored life. Zombies seldom show cunning or self-preservation; they follow crude commands or the instincts imposed by the magic that animates them.

## Abilities and Behavior

**Notable traits:** Slow movement, basic melee attacks, and Undead Fortitude, which can sometimes keep a zombie moving after what should have been a finishing blow.

## Adventurer's Notes

A common early-game undead foe. The [[Circle of the Spores]] druid can create a zombie through its Fungal Infestation feature, subject to that feature's rules.
