# Manticore

**Creature type:** Monstrosity  
**Size:** Large  
**Challenge rating:** 3  
**Armor Class:** 14 (natural armor)  
**Hit Points:** 68 (8d10 + 24)  
**Speed:** 30 ft., fly 50 ft.

## Description

Manticores are winged predators with leonine bodies, humanlike faces, and tails bristling with sharp spikes. Their ability to fly and attack at range makes them dangerous even to opponents who try to keep their distance.

## Abilities and Behavior

**Tail spikes:** A manticore can fire spikes from its tail, making three ranged attacks in one Multiattack. Under the 2014 rules, a tail spike attacks at **+5 to hit** (range **100/200 ft.**) and deals **7 (1d8 + 3) piercing damage** on a hit. It has **24 spikes**, replenished after a long rest.

**Close combat:** A manticore can instead make a bite attack and two claw attacks. It can fly, has darkvision, and speaks Common, so it may threaten or bargain with travelers as well as attack them.

## Adventurer's Notes

Its flight and long-range spikes reward seeking cover and limiting its line of sight. Remember that the manticore's own description and statistics are general 2014 D&D rules: the appearance of a creature in a campaign does not guarantee the DM has used an unmodified stat block.
