# Gelatinous Cube

**Creature type:** Ooze  
**Size:** Large  
**Challenge rating:** 2  
**Armor Class:** 6  
**Hit Points:** 84  
**Speed:** 15 ft.

## Description

A gelatinous cube is a near-transparent mass of corrosive ooze that slowly sweeps dungeon corridors clean. Coins, bones, and lost equipment may hang suspended inside its body like specimens in glass.

## Abilities and Behavior

**Notable traits:** Difficult to notice while still, able to engulf creatures, and capable of paralyzing prey. Its nearly perfect fit in a dungeon passage makes it an especially memorable obstacle.

## Adventurer's Notes

An excellent example of why even a seemingly empty corridor may be dangerous; pair it with [[Surprise]] and exploration clues rather than relying only on an unseen attack.
