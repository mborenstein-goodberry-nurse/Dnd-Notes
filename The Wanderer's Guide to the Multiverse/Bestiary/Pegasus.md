# Pegasus

**Creature type:** Celestial  
**Size:** Large  
**Challenge rating:** 2  
**Armor Class:** 12  
**Hit Points:** 59  
**Speed:** 60 ft.; fly 90 ft.

## Description

A pegasus is a majestic winged horse associated with freedom, noble purpose, and the open sky. It is a celestial being rather than a mundane animal, and it is generally portrayed as wary of cruelty and reluctant to serve an unworthy master.

## Abilities and Behavior

**Notable traits:** Great aerial mobility, keen perception, and a strong preference for flight over cramped ground combat. Its presence often signals a blessing, a rare alliance, or a chance at extraordinary travel.

## Adventurer's Notes

Despite its horse-like appearance, a pegasus is a **celestial**, not a beast, so it is not an ordinary [[Wild Shape]] option. Refer to [[Travel Pace]] when using one as a mount.
