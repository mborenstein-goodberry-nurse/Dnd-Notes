# Awakened Tree

**Creature type:** Plant  
**Size:** Huge  
**Challenge rating:** 2  
**Armor Class:** 13 (natural armor)  
**Hit Points:** 59  
**Speed:** 20 ft.

## Description

An awakened tree is a great tree given the ability to move and act through powerful nature magic. At rest it might look like any other ancient oak, pine, or willow. When threatened, its roots pull free of the soil, its trunk bends, and its branches strike with the force of falling timber. Some are independent beings; others serve as guardians of sacred groves.

## Abilities and Behavior

**Notable traits:** While motionless, an awakened tree is indistinguishable from an ordinary tree (**False Appearance**). It attacks with a long-reaching **slam**, can resist bludgeoning and piercing damage, and is vulnerable to fire damage. Its formidable strength makes it an effective guardian, though it moves slowly.

An awakened tree created by the spell [[Awaken]] can speak one language its creator knows. This is different from a tree animated temporarily as a grove guardian by [[Druid Grove]].

## Adventurer's Notes

The 6th-level spell [[Druid Grove]] can animate **up to four trees** within its warded area. These guardians use the awakened tree's statistics, but **cannot speak**, bear druidic symbols on their bark, and **cannot leave the protected grove**. They defend the area against intruders who aren't exempt from the spell's effects and follow the caster's spoken commands within the grove. When the spell ends, their animating magic fades and they take root again if possible.

For the ward's duration, exceptions, and other effects, see [[Druid Grove]]. For a sentient tree created by a separate, longer-lasting spell, see [[Awaken]].
