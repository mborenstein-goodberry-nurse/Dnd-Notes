# Giant Eagle

**Creature type:** Beast  
**Size:** Large  
**Challenge rating:** 1  
**Armor Class:** 13  
**Hit Points:** 26  
**Speed:** 10 ft.; fly 80 ft.

## Description

Giant eagles are intelligent, formidable birds that soar above mountain ranges and remote wilderness. Often portrayed as proud and discerning rather than simple predators, they can become allies, messengers, or watchful guardians of high places.

## Abilities and Behavior

**Notable traits:** Exceptional eyesight, fast flight, and attacks with both beak and talons. Giant eagles speak their own language and understand some Common and Auran.

## Adventurer's Notes

A standard flying [[Wild Shape]] example for level 8 druids in the 2014 rules. It is also an excellent scout or aerial travel encounter.
