# Glabrezu

**Large fiend (demon), chaotic evil — CR 9 (5,000 XP)**  
**Armor Class:** 17 (natural armor) · **Hit Points:** 157 (15d10 + 75) · **Speed:** 40 ft.

## Combat Reference

**Four-attack routine:** Two pincers and two fists; alternatively, two pincers plus one innate spell. Its pincers can grapple Medium or smaller opponents, holding as many as two targets at once.

**Spellcasting (Intelligence; DC 16):** At will: [[Darkness]], [[Detect Magic]], [[Dispel Magic]]. Once per day each: [[Confusion]], [[Fly]], [[Power Word Stun]]. It has advantage on saving throws against spells and magical effects.
