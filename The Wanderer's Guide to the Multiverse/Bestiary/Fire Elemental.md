# Fire Elemental

**Creature type:** Elemental  
**Size:** Large  
**Challenge rating:** 5  
**Armor Class:** 13  
**Hit Points:** 102  
**Speed:** 50 ft.

## Description

A fire elemental is flame given purpose: a shifting silhouette of cinders, heat, and hungry light. It can turn a confined battlefield into a spreading hazard simply by moving through it.

## Abilities and Behavior

**Notable traits:** Sets creatures and flammable objects alight through contact, can enter an enemy's space, and is vulnerable to the extinguishing effects of water. Associated with the [[Plane of Fire]].

## Adventurer's Notes

Its fire form makes clustered enemies and flammable surroundings particularly dangerous. [[Circle of the Moon]] druids can eventually assume this form through Elemental Wild Shape.
