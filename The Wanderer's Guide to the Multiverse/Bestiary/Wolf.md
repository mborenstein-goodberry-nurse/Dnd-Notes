# Wolf

**Creature type:** Beast  
**Size:** Medium  
**Challenge rating:** 1/4  
**Armor Class:** 13  
**Hit Points:** 11  
**Speed:** 40 ft.

## Description

A wolf is a persistent pack hunter of forests, grasslands, and cold wilderness. Wolves depend on keen senses and coordinated pursuit rather than sheer size, and they are most dangerous when several can surround their prey.

## Abilities and Behavior

**Notable traits:** Keen Hearing and Smell, Pack Tactics, and a bite that can knock a target prone. The creature's ability to fight beside allies makes positioning especially important.

## Adventurer's Notes

A standard example for [[Wild Shape]] at druid level 2 in the 2014 rules (subject to the feature's other requirements).
