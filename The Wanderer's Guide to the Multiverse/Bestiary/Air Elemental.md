# Air Elemental

**Creature type:** Elemental  
**Size:** Large  
**Challenge rating:** 5  
**Armor Class:** 15  
**Hit Points:** 90  
**Speed:** fly 90 ft. (hover)

## Description

An air elemental is a living vortex of wind and swirling pressure. It has no fixed anatomy and can shift from an almost invisible current to a violent, roaring storm in an instant.

## Abilities and Behavior

**Notable traits:** Remarkable mobility, the ability to pass through small openings, and a whirlwind that can disrupt or fling creatures. A strong thematic inhabitant of the [[Plane of Air]].

## Adventurer's Notes

A 2014 [[Circle of the Moon]] druid can assume an elemental form at the level specified by its Elemental Wild Shape feature; this is not a normal beast-form option.
