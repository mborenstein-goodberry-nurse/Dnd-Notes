## Source integrity / provenance policy

- Use each original recording identifier with a **batch prefix**. One session may have multiple recording segments; one recording may span OOC chatter or an adjacent session transition.
- Keep original TXT/SRT pairs and original photo images untouched. When multiple transcription versions exist, prefer the most recent, retaining older readings for ambiguous words.
- Distinguish contemporaneous journals, original audio (and automatic transcripts), Zach's perspective, and retrospective player recollections. Do not merge contradictions silently.
- Use the historical 2024-09-21 calendar anchor for Session 1, but do not assume weekly attendance, one recording per session, or dates taken from iCloud bulk uploads.
- **All assignments in this document are evidence claims**, not updates to your Obsidian session files. Reconfirm and migrate journals in 3–5-session batches.
