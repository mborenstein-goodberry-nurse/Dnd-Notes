# Batch 5 — Arthur & Damien Supplemental Biography Review

## Purpose and Scope

This is a narrow, **no-framework-change** worldbuilding patch prepared from `Batch 5 Initial.zip` and the approved reconstructions through Session 15. It creates an independent biography for Damien while retaining only one Party Member roster entry for the physical body. **No Soren biography**, Party Member template change, Dataview modification, or earlier/later Session Journal edit is included.

## Files

1. `Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/The Party/Arthur Morgan.md` — minimally revised; links the two identities, adds a compact shared-body reference, and puts retrospective/spoiler information in a collapsed OOC callout.
2. `Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Other/Damien Castellanos.md` — new `type: Other` supplemental character biography, not an NPC or a second PC roster record.
3. This review document, for provenance and review.

## Alias handling

- Removed `Damien`, `Damien Castellanos`, and `Damian` from Arthur's **frontmatter aliases**; the first and third now belong to Damien's biography (Damien Castellanos resolves by its filename).
- Existing `[[Damien]]` links in later legacy notes should therefore resolve to Damien's own biography, as intended. Their narrative meaning has **not** yet been reviewed.
- Existing explicit `[[Arthur Morgan|Damien]]` links still target Arthur's roster note. These are left as-is until those later sessions are reconstructed; changing them now would be a premature global migration.
- `Soren`, `Soren Hellborne`, `Damien/Soren`, and `Arthur/Damien/Soren` remain **temporary legacy aliases** on Arthur so later links keep resolving. This is only link compatibility; it is **not** evidence of Soren appearing by Session 15. We can migrate them chronologically later.

- **Session 12:** Arthur reveals Damien to **Diane and Weary privately**; their actual origin is unexplained. Source: approved reconstructed `We Meet the Fox` journal, supported by B22–B23 provisional continuation.

The campaign note-taker confirms Arthur is a **changeling**; `race: Human` is left untouched pending the user's preferred public/spoiler metadata convention.
