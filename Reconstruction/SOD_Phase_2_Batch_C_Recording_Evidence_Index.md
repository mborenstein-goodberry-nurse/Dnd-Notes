## Historical discrepancies and limitations

- The source Session 23 journal is dated **03-14-2026**, which conflicts with surrounding 2025 journals and these 2025 phone recordings. Treat it as a likely date typo, **not a corrected fact**; verify against the calendar during Phase 3.
- Session 24's **journal is missing**, not its recording evidence. Preserve both C05 and C06 so a new Session 24 note can be authored after review if warranted.
- There is **no Batch C original audio recorded after April 25, 2025**. This final batch does not directly fill Sessions 29–55; Zach's contemporaneous notes and original journals remain vital for that period.
- Partial audio must not be represented as full-session coverage. In particular C06 (~8 min) and C07 (~4 min) are fragments; C01+C02 and C03+C04 are split-session recordings.
- The 2026 transcription prompt can misrecognize proper nouns, so quote uncertain names with their original wording when preserving evidence. Do not infer identities from phonetic similarity alone.
