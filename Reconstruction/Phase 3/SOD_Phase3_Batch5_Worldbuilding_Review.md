# Sands of Desolation — Batch 5 Worldbuilding Patch Review

**Checkpoint:** End of Session 15 (January 10, 2025). This patch does not reconstruct Session 15.5 or assume future revelations.

## Decisions adopted

- **Grayson:** `Departed` as of Session 15. He leaves with Creed for his own business, is seen at Graves Hellborne's court, and departs by magic. A future return can restore `Active` in that later session. This is not an assertion that the character never returns.
- **Noisy Owlbear:** standard Party Member (Zach Glass, also Grayson's player), introduced Session 14. Race, class, and level are not fabricated.
- **Phantom Blades:** one new Organization note in Astodan, with Damascus kept as a provisional name. No other guild notes yet.
- **Unseen Hand and Calum:** remain mentions only; no standalone notes.
- **Amorpha's visitor:** identity Graves Hellborne confirmed in Session 15; mystery `Partially Answered`, motives unresolved.
- **Necrotic Spires:** preserve reported three-Nightwalkers-per-spire number and newly witnessed Nightwalkers; unresolved.
- **The Fox's Token:** possible related coin/mask clue, not a confirmed identical object.

## Privacy, knowledge, and chronology

- Graves's concealed machinery is a **private Truesight observation by Weary**; the rest of the party is not automatically aware.
- Arthur's apparent Damien form in Session 14 is **unconfirmed**. The approved Arthur/Damien separate biographies and aliases are left untouched.
- Wearing a Brutalizers or Phantom Blades emblem is **not by itself proof of guild membership**.
- The market and castle are described within New Phyrexia; no new Place files or map coordinates are invented.
- The Party/People/Locations infoboxes in approved Session Journals are not rewritten; source journals are not included in this patch.
- No changes are made to vault framework, macros, templates, non-Batch-5 sessions, or Session 91.
