## Written-only facts requiring placement, not invention

1. **Grayson's dice deal:** Both dated independent note sets say he wins a dice contest against Graves, is freed, and later drinks with the party. This is not recorded in A04. A04 briefly has the characters wondering why Grayson did not leave with them. **Player review needed for timing:** before departure, parallel to journey, or after escape? As of the end of S15 Grayson was `Departed`; a return should be established only at its actual point.
2. **Creed:** Both sources say `Creig/creig is dead`, which likely refers to **Creed**, seen with Grayson at Graves's court in S15. Spelling and exact circumstances are not independently spoken in A04.
3. **Wedding ring:** Both notes have `Ate ben's wedding ring` alongside Grayson lines. Which character ate it and whose ring is meant **are not securely readable**. Leave literal shorthand in the review instead of inventing an incident.
4. **Nightwalkers & Spire:** Written notes report Nightwalkers visible as party leaves and a Spire radiating necrotic energy. The recording shows a distant Spire but **does not independently record those Nightwalker sightings or identify the storm creature as a Nightwalker**.
5. **Before the escape:** Written notes say Ben cast **Pass Without Trace** on the cart (likely a player-name notation; in-world caster uncertain), that the storm may be attracted to sound (flagged `?`), Thornwick wakes the encounter, Weary deafens the group, and Thornwick deploys an **Immovable Rod** on the pursuer. Do not claim sound attraction or a specific deafening spell as established.
6. **Border:** Written sources agree the company reaches Foo Fighter territory and the storm cannot pass the border. Diane gets a message not to trespass, with sender and delivery unrecorded. **Distinct from the earlier written-to-Diane / blank-to-Owlbear note** at the Pirate King's castle.
7. **Weary/Grayson's orb:** Zach's final line is *`Possibility to have weary surge through Grayson's orb absorb the arcana?`*—a question or potential plan, not a completed action or confirmed mechanic.
