## Interpretive gaps and provenance risks

1. **Who received the natural-20 acclaim?** Zach wrote in first person “I nat 20'd twice & the Chief thinks I'm orc Jesus” and a cult was starting, while the page also says “Jonks nat 20s.” The two lines may refer to the same or different characters. Do **not** assign the emerging cult to Jonks or Grayson without confirmation. The exact handwritten cult name looks ambiguous (possibly “Gilgamesh” or another similar word); omitted rather than canonized.
2. **What was Jonks given?** The line following “Jonks nat 20's” looks like “gets given 1 body” but the last word is not fully secure. Do not count a body, companion, or item as an established gain. Its wording is preserved in the Log as an ambiguous historical artifact.
3. **The killer / Jonks's master:** Page 8027 says Jonks *thinks* the killer is his old master. No source confirms it, and no source proves whether one or multiple attackers carried out the slaughter. Details such as how the vision was produced or who cast it are absent.
4. **Silence, tracking and timing:** Photo 8028 says “Silence cast on party / Weary dispells”, “Something is on us & watching”, “something has happened”. This supports an incident of magical Silence with Weary removing it, but not the caster, a confirmed tracker, why the spell was cast, or exact point on the route.
5. **The Iron Shadows / potion maker:** Zach ends page 8027 with a question about the Iron Shadows possibly killing a potion maker. This is *speculation*, not a confirmed murder by them. Whether it connects to the unnamed yuan-ti potion merchant in S16 is **not established**.
6. **The Fox and coin:** Only the original historical journal directly mentions that the Fox gave Jonks a coin. This is important but could describe a physical meeting, an exchange at a distance, or an offer. Do not assign a function or a bargain yet.
7. **Other shorthand:** “Arthur counter spells me — fucker” supports a counterspell but not the precise target; “Dian lore dumps — smth about fire” lacks details; “Weary pulls a 'Sherlock Helms' put Bag” is not interpretable; “Weary & Issobell watch me flee — creepers” does not identify the fleeing person or reason; “Haley got Juice” may be a name but no independent identity is established. These phrases are not elevated to unsupported narrative facts.
8. **Village architecture:** One line suggests “town's burned outside but inside is good”, another “barn is only left standing?” while the original says the barn is ordinary with two bodies. Avoid claiming that the barn was definitively the sole surviving building.
9. **Party list:** Carried forward the likely group from S16, adding Isabelle because Zach explicitly mentions her in S17. Other members' specific actions/attendance are not independently established. Noisy Owlbear is **not** included because the player confirmed he actually died during S16. Confirm attendance if it later matters.
10. **Potions:** Zach says “Arthur & Grayson get potions” but gives no amount, type, seller, or cost. Count as a limited acquisition without creating named loot.
11. **Northward movement:** Zach says the company left town to the north, as did a group of orcs. His “take hobbits to Mordor” wording is a joke, not a literal objective or setting fact.
