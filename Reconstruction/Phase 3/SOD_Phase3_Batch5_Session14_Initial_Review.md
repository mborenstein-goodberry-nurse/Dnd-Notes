## Player review — decisions and retained uncertainties

1. **Blueberry Weary — confirmed:** A **Wild Magic surge inflated Weary into a ball**. He was already mostly purple, hence "blueberry." The user recalls the mechanism; no direct Session 14 recording preserves the roll. The beginning of Session 15 confirms the continued state.
2. **Grayson, Creed, Xerac — confirmed:** Grayson left with Creed. "Lich Diddy" was the table's running joke for **Xerac**, whose name they had temporarily forgotten. No new lich note.
3. **New faces:** **Noisy Owlbear** is a new player character played by **Zach Glass**, who also plays Grayson. **Calum** is named as a rogue in the Session 15 recap, and the user believes Calum was a PC/party member but cannot recall more. **Do not create a Calum note yet.** "Undeen Hand" is a typo for **Unseen Hand**. Treat it provisionally as an **organization**, not an NPC or automatically a sixth guild; the user's memory of its purpose is hazy. No standalone note for it in this patch. **Damascus** leads the Phantom Blades, with spelling still provisional.
4. **Nightwalkers / Resurgence:** Nightwalkers were rumored before Session 14 but were **first actually seen by the party this session**. Three per spire is a **reported number**, not independently counted at each structure. Resurgence rituals are broadly common knowledge; Diane and Weary had prior firsthand context from **Warrick** in an earlier Astodan campaign. Don't retell this as a brand-new discovery or prove a complete ritual-to-spire mechanism.
5. **Arthur / Damien:** Arthur is a **changeling**. During Session 12, Arthur/Damien were established as **different consciousnesses in one body** (privately revealed to Diane and Weary). The original Session 14 note conjectures that his dark-haired, Pirate-King-friendly guise was **Damien**, but this is **unconfirmed**. Flag Arthur's current `Human` frontmatter race for a later chronological Party metadata review rather than altering it in this journal patch.
