**Historical cutoff:** End of Session 21. **Scope:** Six party-member notes only. These are replacements based on the **Batch 7 Worldbuilding Review Patch**, not the older unpatched vault snapshot. Apply the earlier worldbuilding patch first, then apply these six notes. No other files are changed.

## Why these changes

- **Amorpha:** Filled Description, Background & History, and Personality & Motivations using her Session 11 introduction, thefts, dream encounters, Graves recognition and Narci's counsel. Physical appearance beyond being a hobgoblin is not verified.
- **Arthur Morgan:** Filled the same sections using Sparrow, the selectively disclosed disguise and shared-body details, and observed actions; separated motives for secrecy from other consciousnesses' actions. Did not claim Soren controlled the Session 21 events.

- **Jonks:** Added the **Session 20 first-person character introduction** omitted from earlier reviews: a dragon enslaved him and killed his wife; Jonks says he then served and robbed a necromancer before leaving to practice magic independently. **Player review strongly recommended**: this account is on the February 21, 2025 recording but was not independently corroborated; and the wealthy pursuer's identity is unresolved.
- **Linus Lightfoot:** Filled all empty prose sections and his empty Campaign History from the February 21, 2025 recording and the limited Session 21 evidence. He described himself as an exiled former courtier, appearance-conscious, and interested in returning home. Unstable/joking improv about killing orcs, his parents, and his French accent is intentionally not presented as established biography. Level/home/subclass remain unverified.
- **Thornwick:** Added fairy-king / skull-mask portrait, son and signet-ring background, and his ongoing desire to reunite with his son; kept long-term kingdom restoration unconfirmed.
