That same recording refers to Weary's soul fragment being returned and Weary later says he has his whole soul back. **Treatment:** Session 23 strongly favored for the restoration; do not import the result into Session 22 or infer exchange terms until reconstructing Session 23.

The vault has `Campaigns/Sands of Desolation/Other/Soren.md`, with a prior Session 21 appearance still noted as unconfirmed. **Treatment:** keep Soren's identification OOC-only in this journal; describe apparent activity without naming him as a character known to the party.
