# Sands of Desolation — Phase 3 / Batch 9 Worldbuilding Review

**Reconstruction scope:** Sessions 25–28 inclusive. Journal text is already delivered separately; this patch updates only scoped worldbuilding/quest/mystery material and adds two temporary PCs plus the Ring of Spelling.

## Key established outcomes

- **Session 25:** Cinderfang was already dead **before** the Sovereign encounter. The Sovereign used his skull in the battle. Noisy Owlbear **returned**, mechanism unknown; earlier true death remains in his history. The first evidence for Cinderfang's death is Session 25, **not** necessarily the date of death.
- **Session 26:** Thornwick's finishing blow defeats the Sovereign. The life-associated engine hemisphere is recovered in the Sovereign's chamber. The Sovereign's body later remains visible but his unused hearts/pods leave a narrow uncertainty over finality.
- **Session 27:** The luminous soul provisionally called **Luz reunites with Wonder's body**. She identifies herself as Warrick's wife and names Gideon as her patron. Isabelle recovers a soul fragment and keeps her Pirate King memory private. Havoc manifests at camp and gives a fiendish disguise to aid escape. The engine halves remain separate; Soren is the best-supported attribution for the gauntlet violence but the precise identity transition is unknown.
- **Session 28:** Engine halves **now joined**, player confirms they remained joined afterward, but whether separation is impossible is unknown. Mirage (Emily) and Jingles (Dalton) are confirmed temporary PCs; Jingles is autumn eladrin (class unknown), Mirage's race/class unknown. Zoff returns. The Ring of Spelling is homebrew, changes spell names by a letter, and is not Ring of Spell Storing. The entrance of Infinite Mausoleum is documented, with riddle completion in Zach's notes.
