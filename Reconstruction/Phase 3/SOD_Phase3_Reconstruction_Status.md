## Workflow for Batch 3 (Sessions 7-9)

1. Start from the **latest committed `SOD-Migration` branch**, not the original pre-migration journal ZIP.
2. Use the Phase 2 crosswalk to identify the evidence for Sessions 7-9, then review TXT/SRT against the journals, original sources, and Session 6 provenance; do not assume recording ordinals equal session numbers.
3. Keep chronological distinctions between what happened, what was known at the time, DM recap statements, original paper notes, and later player recollection.
4. Use path-qualified wikilinks with readable aliases, and link relevant creatures, items, spells, People, Places, quests, and mysteries.
5. Treat currently `Undiscovered` future quests as hidden until their discovery sessions. Maintain accurate worldbuilding frontmatter at each checkpoint.
6. End the next batch with a source/decision closeout like [[Reconstruction/Phase 3/SOD_Phase3_Batch2_Final_Closeout|Batch 2's closeout]], not only an initial import review. Preserve PDFs, photographs and ledgers unchanged.
7. If Dataview looks inconsistent with correct source metadata, **restart Obsidian first**; then inspect queries or cached metadata only if necessary.
