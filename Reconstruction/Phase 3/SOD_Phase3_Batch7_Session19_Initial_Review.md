The fiend's identity, the ritual's required living participant, and objective truth remain open.

- **Zoff goods:** No one draws from the purported Deck of Many Things in the captured Session 19 evidence; the hand axe is magical but not identified. The purchase is 75 gold for **both** items, not 75 each. Do not grant a separately proven cursed property.
