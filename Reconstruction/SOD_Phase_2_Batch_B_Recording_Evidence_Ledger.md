- **Files:** 27 TXT and 26 SRT files; B01 has 3 TXT transcription versions and 2 SRT versions. For B01 the **November 4, 2026** transcription is primary; the October 20/28 runs remain alternate readings.
- **Original recording dates are unavailable.** The supplied contextual note says the files were uploaded to iCloud Drive in bulk on **January 3, 2025**, potentially replacing original creation dates. This supplies an *upper bound only if the upload timestamp is authentic*. Do not assign January 3 as the capture date of each audio file.
- **Historical journal dates** in the session crosswalk below are from the existing Obsidian journals, **not** verified timestamps for these recordings. Session 10 and the fractional Session 2.5 contain suspect 2026 journal dates.
- **Transcript limitations:** Names, locations, speaker turns and chronology can be garbled by automatic transcription and enriched prompts. Narrative consistency supports session matches but does not certify every sentence.
- **Coverage estimate:** about **21 hours 7 minutes** of SRT timeline across the primary versions of all 25 IDs, including breaks/chatter. This is not a guarantee of playable, intelligible audio or uninterrupted sessions.

## Key result: the Session 1 narrative survives as a Session 2 recap

B01 opens with the DM **recapping** the mission to seek rejuvenating artifacts at the **Towers of Icarus** and another caldera (transcribed inconsistently; the historical journal names the **Luciferian Caldera**) and the party's trip through the Griffin Empire toward the **Gilded Lands**. This corroborates the user's memory of Session 1 and its two-site assignment, but **does not establish that the opening events themselves were recorded**. The first recorded new scene appears consistent with Session 2.

## Quality flags and priority follow-ups

1. **B21 is empty.** Both `Recording 21` TXT and SRT are **0 bytes**. Verify whether the original audio exists, is an empty recording, or needs to be transcribed again. Leave its session/date as unknown.
2. **B05 is only about a minute.** The text contains out-of-character introductions/names, not a dependable session scene. Do not use this for substantive historical reconstruction.
3. **B01 has three runs.** Prefer the November 4 TXT/SRT as requested by the owner. The October 20 TXT and October 28 TXT/SRT should remain as fallback evidence for uncertain terms, not counted as extra recordings.
4. **The expected 26th identifier is not present.** We received B01–B25 only. Check whether "26 recordings" was an approximate count or whether a source file was omitted; do not manufacture B26.
5. **Gap S8–S10.** Batch B jumps from S7 material (B11/B12) to the *aftermath* of the hag encounter in S11 (B13). Do not assume those sessions were captured just because a nearby recording mentions the same NPCs.
6. **B08/B09 boundary is uncertain.** B08 stops as the group enters a consequential chamber; B09 opens after Gideon disappears. Since combat was sometimes intentionally omitted, the Gideon battle may not exist in audio at all. Do not declare S5 fully recorded.
7. **B22–B25 late-boundary uncertainty.** Private character scenes and the transition to the Wastes are clearer than individual dates. Match against the owner's calendar and other contemporary notes before final assignment.
8. **S1 scope.** The DM's B01 introduction is exceptionally valuable *corroborating testimony* for the original two-artifact mission, but is technically a later recap. Preserve that source distinction in the Session 1 migration.
